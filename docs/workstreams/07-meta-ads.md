## 1. Purpose, role of Meta in the plan and honest sizing

### 1.1 What Meta is for
Strategy v2 funds Meta at **SEK 0.45 M in 2027 and SEK 0.60 M in 2028** (section 11.1 of the strategy, "Paid media by channel"). Q4 2026 has SEK 0.10 M for all paid media, not only Meta. Meta is about 12-13% of total marketing spend (450k of 3.6 M; 600k of 4.6 M), so Meta is a supporting channel, not the growth engine. Search (SEK 0.85 M / 1.10 M) protects and captures intent; Meta does four jobs Search cannot:

| Job | What it does | Credited to bridge lever |
|---|---|---|
| J1 Retarget and nurture | Keeps site visitors, quote recipients and engaged viewers warm; shortens the decision cycle on a SEK ~99k booking (48 MSEK / 485 bookings in strategy 11.1) | L2 conversion on existing flow (not credited to paid) |
| J2 Inspire and generate demand | Video and carousel prospecting of Swedes 40-70 who are not yet searching "Sydafrika resa"; builds the email list and feeds enquiries | L3 Tranche B (only this is credited to L3) |
| J3 Fill hosted and guided departures | "Platser kvar" ads for the 4-6 hosted departures and the ~41 guided departures a year (strategy, supply section) | L5 |
| J4 Distribute proof | Reviews, customer stories, adviser introductions, "Det här ingår, därför kostar det lite mer" | Supports the premium positioning (strategy 6) |

### 1.2 Honest sizing (read this before approving any budget)
- **Direct incremental yield is small.** Definition used throughout: a *qualified enquiry* is a CRM-qualified lead that receives a quote, and the 18% new-traffic close rate (strategy A10) is applied to it [M21: strategy applies 18% to quotes, i.e. 80% of enquiries; Meta qualified share M9 is 60-70%, so the two definitions must be reconciled against CRM data]. At an *attributed* CPQE of about SEK 2,000 (section 8.3), 18% close and SEK ~99k per booking, SEK 240k of 2027 prospecting yields about 120 qualified enquiries, about 22 attributed bookings, about SEK 2.1 M attributed order book (about 9x attributed). With an assumed 50% incrementality (M12) the incremental figure is about SEK 1.1 M (about 4.4x), in line with the strategy's "Tranche B about 4x" for 2028. Two consequences: (a) in the 2027 departure-year view of the strategy (Tranche B about 1.7x, L3 +1.2 MSEK) most Apr-Dec 2027 Meta bookings depart in 2028, so the 1.1 M is booked value, not 2027 turnover, and must not be added on top of L3 for 2027; (b) the plan case is **below** the 5x break-even. For 2028 (prospecting, departures and creator, SEK 370k) the same logic gives roughly SEK 1.4-1.8 M incremental, about 35-45% of L3 (+4.0 MSEK), with creator and departure ads partly credited to L5. The 24-month target does not rest on Meta.
- **Break-even is demanding and the plan case does not clear it.** At 20% gross margin (A7) a booking contributes about SEK 19.8k margin. Break-even incremental ROAS is 5x (strategy 11.2), which equals a **break-even incremental cost per qualified enquiry of about SEK 3,560 (rounded 3,600)** (19.8k x 18% close). The planning case (attributed CPQE 2,000 at 50% incrementality) is an incremental CPQE of about SEK 4,000, i.e. about 10% above break-even, and a media-only cost of about SEK 22k per incremental booking (240k / 11), above the strategy's SEK 20k marginal cap before creative and labour. Meta prospecting therefore only pays if attributed CPQE lands at or below about SEK 1,800 (at 50% incrementality) or incrementality is above 50%. This is exactly what the gated holdout is for. Anything above SEK 3,600 incremental is value-destroying on first-trip margin; the strategy's pause line (4x) corresponds to about SEK 4,450 incremental CPQE.
- **Repeat and referral value is deliberately excluded** from the 5x test (strategy treats repeat in L7). Leads that become newsletter subscribers or referrers are upside, tracked, not credited.
- **Statistical power is weak.** About 2,148 existing enquiries a year (strategy L2) is about 41 a week. A Meta effect worth 5x on about SEK 30k of test-region prospecting spend (section 9.3) is about 8 incremental qualified enquiries in six weeks, roughly 4-5% of the test regions' baseline, which is likely below weekly noise. Section 9.3 therefore defines a proxy-based, pooled test and a default rule: **inconclusive means not proven, so Tranche B Meta is not scaled**.

## 3. Approach in one page

1. **Measure first, spend second.** No prospecting money moves before tracking v1 (30 Nov) and the full holdout design (31 Jan). December and January spend is limited to retargeting, audience building and small creative-hook tests (at most SEK 20k in January, to build video-viewer audiences) inside the strategy's pre-Gate-1 paid cap. Learning prospecting for the holdout starts 1 Feb. Note: strategy 11.3 describes pre-Gate-1 paid as "brand and high-intent only"; the SEK 60k learning prospecting is an exception needed to produce the Gate 1 holdout read and needs CEO confirmation (see open questions).
2. **Four-layer funnel** (section 4) with exclusions between layers so retargeting is not paid to reach people who already have a quote open with an adviser.
3. **Concentrate, do not fragment.** Budget is small (about SEK 37k a month average in 2027) and Meta's learning phase needs volume, so few campaigns, few ad sets, many creatives (section 5, 6).
4. **Creative does the targeting.** Segment-specific creative to broad 40-70 audiences beats narrow interest stacks at this budget (Advantage+ audience/placements by default; interest and lookalike signals only as suggestions) [M5].
5. **Premium through proof, not discounts.** Value-adds and inclusions, named adviser, guide, resegaranti. No price-led creative (strategy 6.1). Hook: "Vinter hemma, sommar i Sydafrika" is a **test**, not a proven angle (strategy 6).
6. **Sales capacity gates the budget.** Per strategy, budget never moves up while the response-time SLA is missed (G8). Paid is cut to Tranche A if service quality gates are breached two consecutive weeks.
7. **Gated money.** Tranche B items (prospecting, departure/creator boosting) are released at Gate 1 only if first holdout read is at least 4x on proxy basis and G8/G4 pass. Retargeting is Tranche A and committed.

### 5.3 Settings
- Placements: Advantage+ placements on; exclude Audience Network. Review Reels vs Feed vs Stories monthly.
- Optimisation: with SEK 3-12k a week per ad set, Meta's guideline of about 50 optimisation events per ad set per week cannot be met [M6]. Mitigations: consolidate to two prospecting ad sets, optimise to the highest-volume reliable event (website `Lead`), run budgets with campaign budget optimisation, avoid edits during learning, and judge quality by CRM-qualified rate not Meta's Lead count.
- Lead capture: prefer **website form** (full context, CRM sync, consent text) over instant forms for quote requests; use instant forms with "higher intent" setting and a review step only for the guide/webinar. Every lead (any form) enters the same CRM queue with the same speed-to-lead SLA.
- Brand safety: inventory filter "limited", block lists; comment moderation rules (Swedish keywords; hide spam; reply to questions within one business day by an adviser).

## 7. Creative system and concepts

### 7.1 Creative principles
1. Real people and places: customer photos/videos (licence signed), advisers on camera, Swedish-speaking guides, real itineraries. Stock only as fillers.
2. First 3 seconds sells the destination, second 3 seconds the human. Subtitles on all video (sound-off).
3. Formats: 9:16 Reels/Stories 15-30 s; 4:5 feed video and still; carousel (5 cards) for itinerary and cost; one 60-90 s adviser story for YouTube re-use.
4. Safety rule from strategy and R09: state facts and link to UD/Ambassaden; **never** "Sydafrika är säkert". Show "så planerar vi för din trygghet".
5. **Claim checklist** (workstream 01, section 6) before every launch: no unverified superlatives, no "24/7" until costed and staffed (use "jourtelefon under resan"), "svarar inom en timme på vardagar" only after SLA is met, price only if true, in stock and including mandatory costs.
6. Refresh: new hero creative every 6 weeks per segment; retire when frequency above 4 (cold, 4 weeks) or CTR down 30% from peak.

## 9. Measurement, holdout and decision rules

### 9.1 Dashboard (weekly, one page, feeding the strategy dashboard)
Spend by campaign; impressions, reach, frequency; CPM, CPC, link CTR; hook and hold rates; leads by type; **cost per Lead, per CRM-qualified lead (CPQE), per quote sent, per booking**; qualified rate; close rate by source (Meta cold vs retargeting); attributed order book (7-day click, 1-day view, shown but not decision-grade) and CRM-based booked value; incremental ROAS (holdout); marginal marketing cost per marketing-created booking; response time of leads from Meta; email subscribers added; creative leaderboard.

### 9.2 Source of truth
CRM (stages, booked value, margin band) is the source of truth. Meta Ads Manager is a delivery tool. Offline events from CRM uploaded daily. "Hur hörde du om oss" answer compared monthly with attributed leads to estimate under-reporting. Server-side tagging and consent logging per strategy 16.1 (due 31 Jan 2027).

### 9.3 Holdout design (due 31 Jan 2027; run Feb-mid Mar 2027; read by Gate 1)
- **Design:** geo split (Swedish county clusters matched on population and historic enquiries per 100k; data needed from GA4/CRM); Meta prospecting withheld in two matched regions for 6 weeks (strategy 11.2). Where eligible and budget allows, a Meta Conversion Lift study is an alternative [VERIFY eligibility and minimum spend; may exceed budget].
- **Primary metric:** qualified enquiries per 100,000 inhabitants (test vs holdout), pre-period normalised. **Implied iROAS = (incremental qualified enquiries x 18% close x SEK 99k) / spend in test regions.**
- **Why proxy:** bookings are too few in six weeks. Bookings are validated cumulatively as they close.
- **Worked example:** learning prospecting in Feb-Mar is about SEK 40k nationally, of which about SEK 30k falls in the test regions (assuming test regions hold about 75% of the population [M24]). Break-even incremental revenue is 5 x 30k = SEK 150k = 1.5 bookings = ~8 incremental qualified enquiries. National baseline is ~248 enquiries in six weeks (2,148 / 52 x 6), about 186 in the test regions, so a break-even effect is about 4-5% of baseline, likely within noise. (v1 assumed SEK 45k of test spend, which exceeded what the SEK 60k learning cap allows.)
- **Therefore:** (a) pool the Q1 geo test with a second wave in Q2 (Apr-May) and a switchback test (2-week on/off) in H2 before any scale decision; (b) accept an 80% confidence interval, report the interval, and compare the lower bound to 3x and the point estimate to 4x/5x; (c) **default rule: inconclusive = not proven, no scale-up**; (d) Tranche B remains "learning" capped at SEK 60k until a read supports at least 4x.
- **Brand-search interference:** Meta can lift branded search; brand-search pause tests (strategy 11.2) are run by the Search owner, and Meta's holdout read must include the assisted branded effect through region-level total enquiries, not only Meta-tagged ones.

### 9.4 Decision rules (consistent with strategy 11.2, G4, G8)
| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Holdout iROAS under 4x after 8 weeks of a campaign group | Pause that group |
| iROAS at least 5x and response SLA met | Scale by max 20% per week, up to the gated budget |
| iROAS between 4x and 5x | Hold budget, optimise creative/funnel, retest |
| Attributed ROAS under 8x for 6 weeks | Investigation trigger (sanity check, not a kill rule) |
| Response-time SLA missed (any week) | No budget increase; two consecutive weeks of breached service gate: reduce to Tranche A (retargeting only) |
| Attributed CPQE above about SEK 1,800 for 4 weeks (proxy for incremental 3,600 at 50% incrementality, M12; incremental CPQE itself is only measurable at holdout reads) | Cut or reallocate; creative refresh. Holdout pause line (4x) equals incremental CPQE of about SEK 4,450 |
| Frequency above 4/week (hot) or 6 per 30 days (cold) | Refresh creative, widen audience |
| Marginal marketing cost per marketing-created booking above SEK 20k two quarters | Freeze B, review |
| Kammarkollegiet forecast not updated for volume surge | Pause scale-up until CFO confirms (R09) |
