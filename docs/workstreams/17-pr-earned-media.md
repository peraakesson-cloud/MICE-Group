## 1. Summary

1. PR is a **trust, proof and link-building layer**, not a volume engine. The strategy credits "PR, podcasts, media" with only about SEK 0.2 M of order book in 2028 and 0 in 2027 (workstream 11 split of L5, 2.0 MSEK total; L5 is +0.4 in 2027). We do not claim more. Its real jobs are: (a) third-party proof for a SEK 30-100k, high-consideration trip while Trustpilot sits at 2.7 on 8 reviews (R03); (b) editorial links and citations that feed L4 (SEO and AI-search); (c) filling the 4-6 hosted departures (L5); (d) positioning "Sydafrikaspecialisten sedan 2003" with named people; (e) a crisis-ready voice if South Africa makes negative headlines in Sweden (R09 section 7).
2. **The Swedish media landscape is thin in print and strong in digital, trade and niche.** Allt om resor (Expressen) closed at the end of 2024; Res and Aftonbladet Resa had already stopped print; Vagabond is the only dedicated Swedish travel magazine in print (Dagens Media, 13 Nov 2024, https://www.dagensmedia.se/tidningar-och-tidskrifter/tidskrifter/vagabond-blir-ensam-i-print-vi-ser-en-positiv-utveckling/). So the plan targets Vagabond (print and web), the daily-press travel desks, Travel Report for trade credibility, podcasts, niche communities (golf, wine, photography, 55+), regional and local press (customer hometown stories) and owned/earned hybrids.
3. **Five pillars**: (P1) data and trend PR from our own anonymised booking data and public data; (P2) people stories (guides, advisers, customers, hosted-departure personalities); (P3) press and creator trips, two a year at most, with strict return criteria; (P4) seasonal and service stories (shoulder season, safety/entry facts, travel guarantee); (P5) 2028 "25 years of South Africa" anniversary campaign [ASSUMPTION PR2: "since 2003" means 25 years in 2028; confirm].
4. **Budget is deliberately small**: cash SEK 55,000 in 2027 (aligned to `28-budget-allocation.md`, which allocates PR 55k) and SEK 100,000 in 2028, taken from the strategy line "Social, creators, hosted departures, PR" (no new money), plus capped in-kind press-trip costs that must be charged to the partnerships/SAT/airline co-op line and the departure P&L (section 10; a claim on another workstream's line that WS18 and WS28 must accept). The shared social/PR line is over-subscribed before PR (WS28); this plan therefore asks for **no cash in Q4 2026 and none before Gate 0 (15 Dec 2026)** and releases the gated part only at Gate 1 (31 Mar 2027).
5. **Gates**: no press push for any departure not backed by held supply (Gate 0, 15 Dec 2026; supply-readiness gate); no press trip or hosted-departure push while the service gate G8 is breached; no discount-led PR (G2). PR does not run "sale" stories.
6. **North star link**: PR is judged on credited booked-not-departed order book where traceable, plus leading indicators (qualified referral sessions, branded search, backlinks, enquiries citing media). Targets in section 11.

## 4. Audiences and message architecture

**Who we need to reach through media** (matches R02 and R08 segments): (1) 45-70-year-olds with long-haul experience and money to spend (Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, newspapers' digital travel sections, radio/podcasts); (2) 55+ guided-trip buyers (Senior Festival, regional press, magazines for seniors, club newsletters); (3) golf, wine and food, photography, hiking, wildlife interest communities; (4) families and grandparents travelling with grandchildren; (5) honeymooners (younger, social-first); (6) trade (agents, airlines, SAT, DMCs, Travel Report) whose credibility supports partnership levers L8.

**Core message (aligned with positioning)**: "Sydafrikaspecialisten sedan 2003" - a named personal adviser, guided departures with Swedish-speaking guides or fully tailor-made trips, and a booking protected by a travel guarantee. The premium shows in guide, inclusions, flexibility and support. We do not say "billigast" or "Sveriges bästa" (misleading-claim risk, R08 section 8). We do not promote the 24/7 local presence until costed and staffed; copy says "jourtelefon under resan".

**Proof points we may only use once confirmed and legally cleared** [ASSUMPTION PR5]: years in business; number of travellers per year; share of repeat travellers; number of guides and their languages; guarantee details (Kammarkollegiet registration, R09); awards and review scores. Each carries a source note in the press kit.

**Message discipline**: one story per pitch; one named person per story; a concrete number or date; an image that is cleared. No generic "upptäck Sydafrika" pitches.

## 6. Tactics

### 6.1 Foundation (Nov 2026 - Jan 2027)

1. **Press room** on sydafrikaresor.se (e.g. /press) and, from 2027, a Mynewsdesk (or equivalent) newsroom (Q4 2026: free trial or website page only, cash zero): boilerplate, fact sheet, spokesperson profiles with consent, cleared photo library with licences, logo pack, B-roll, FAQ for journalists, press contact with a mobile number, and the "no content approval, full disclosure" press-trip policy. Page is also a citation source for AI-search (L4). Cost: staff time plus tool fee [ASSUMPTION PR7: SEK 0-15k/year].
2. **Media map and audit**: build a list of about 60 contacts across: Vagabond (print and web), travel desks at national dailies (DN, SvD, Aftonbladet, Expressen, GP, SDS and local dailies), Travel Report and other trade press, radio and podcasts, freelance travel writers, niche press (golf, wine, photography, outdoor, 55+/senior), regional papers near sales hotspots (needs CRM postal-code data), and Facebook groups where journalists source cases. Record each outlet's policy on hosted trips. Method: outlet websites and bylines; no purchased lists in 2027.
3. **Coverage baseline**: 12 months of existing mentions via manual search and Google Alerts; log tone and links.
4. **Spokespeople**: CEO (strategy, anniversary, trade), Head of Product (itineraries, supply), two senior advisers, two guides. Each gets 2 hours of media training (Q4 2026: in-house by the PR lead, cash zero; 2027 refreshers by a freelance coach within the SEK 6k line) and a one-page Q&A on safety, health, price drivers and guarantee [ASSUMPTION PR8].
5. **Crisis and issue protocol** (section 9): a pre-approved holding statement, an on-call rota, a decision tree for UD warning changes, strikes, unrest, health alerts, flight disruption, tour accident, review-site pile-on. Link to the guarantee and cancellation-rights obligations in R09.
6. **Measurement set-up**: UTM standard for all press links (utm_source=media name, utm_medium=pr), a "Hur hörde du om oss?" field with a "Tidning/Podd/Artikel" option on quote forms and phone scripts (shared with WS14), PR tag in CRM, monthly coverage log, brand-search tracking in Search Console.

### 6.3 Press and creator trips

Principles:
- **At most one trip in 2027 and two in 2028** (invitations only after Gate 1 and held supply), each linked to a product we want to sell and a travel window with held supply.
- **Return criteria**: each invitation requires an agreed editorial plan (outlet, format, planned date) in writing, though without content approval from us. A trip proceeds only if invitees' combined verified reach and fit justify the cost [ASSUMPTION PR9: use outlet-supplied audience data; none is public in this research].
- **Cost control**: land arrangements at net cost through the DMC and lodges; flights co-funded by an airline partner or South African Tourism if possible (their trade pages indicate Europe market promotion; no agreement confirmed) [ASSUMPTION PR10]; hosting in shoulder months with spare lodge inventory; one hosted departure can host one journalist in a paying group at marginal cost.
- **Disclosure**: invitation and published pieces state the trip was paid by Sydafrikaresor and partners. We never write or approve editorial text; we may correct factual errors. For paid creators, Konsumentverket labelling applies (WS11).
- **Journalists decline hosted trips often.** Plan B: a "meet the guide" visit in Sweden, a briefing with photo and video material, or a customer-trip case.
- **Service gate**: no trip if G8 is breached or guide/DMC capacity is not confirmed.

Trip design (2027): 8 days, 3 invitees: one print/digital travel journalist (Vagabond or a daily's travel desk, if their policy allows), one freelance writer-photographer with niche reach (golf/wine/photo), one podcaster or video creator. Route: Cape Town - Winelands - Garden Route or Cape Town plus a safari, matched to the departure type we want to fill [ASSUMPTION PR11]. Timing: May 2027 (shoulder; invitations go out right after Gate 1 on 31 Mar, giving about 6 weeks lead) so articles appear Jul-Sep, ahead of the Aug-Oct indexing window and 2028 sales. 2028 trips: one in Feb-Mar for wine/golf and one for the anniversary in Sep-Oct.

### 6.4 Hosted-departure PR playbook (with WS10 and WS11)

For each hosted departure (4-6 a year): (1) T-150 days host agreement and held supply confirmed (supply gate); (2) T-120 press release in Swedish plus a tailored pitch to the host's own media network and relevant niche outlets; (3) T-100 personal interview offer with the host and a guide; (4) T-90 checkpoint: under 40% sold means review price, host or cancel (WS10); (5) T-60 target 80% sold; (6) during trip: daily stories shared by the host, a local-press note for travellers' home towns with consent; (7) T+14 results story with real customer quotes. Cost to PR: staff time, plus SEK 3k per paid release package for the first 2 departures in 2027 and about 5 in 2028; other releases are written in-house at zero cash [ASSUMPTION PR12]; host fees sit in the departure P&L.

### 6.5 Events and fairs

Africa's Travel Indaba (Durban, May 2027) is the one international South Africa trade event: suitable for supply, SAT relations and trade-press angle, but travel costs are not in the PR budget; decision belongs to Product/Partnerships (R12) and only if it supports DMC and lodge negotiations. Resemässan/Senior Festival in Stockholm is a consumer fair with a senior profile; 2027 dates and costs must be verified. Attend only with a measurable plan (lead target, follow-up SLA), otherwise skip; the marketing budget cannot carry an unmeasured stand [ASSUMPTION PR13]. The strategy roadmap lists a Resemässan/Seniorfestivalen exhibit in Oct 2027 (SEK 120-250k, gated line); PR's role there is limited to seminar slots. PR adds value at fairs by seminar slots with the guide, not by stand presence.

### 6.6 Digital PR and AI-search support (with WS04/05)

- Prioritise outlets that rank and get cited: national dailies' travel pages, Vagabond web, Travel Report, Wikipedia-eligible references (neutral, no promotion), and local news.
- Every press story links to a specific itinerary or guide page, not the home page.
- Reclaim unlinked brand mentions (monthly search) and request links.
- Never buy links or run sponsored "advertorials" disguised as editorial; labelled advertorials are a paid-media decision, outside this plan.
- Build "citeable" pages: facts about entry rules, seasons, costs ranges, safety, guarantee; each with sources and a last-updated date.

### 6.7 Reputation and review link (with WS12)

Trustpilot stands at 2.7 on 8 reviews (R03). Journalists check review sites. Before major pitches (A11, A12, A6) the review programme (P1) must show progress; if the rating is still below the G8 threshold of 4.2 with more than 30 reviews we skip consumer-protection angles (A11) and lead with people stories. Public answers to negative reviews are a trust asset the PR lead monitors weekly.
