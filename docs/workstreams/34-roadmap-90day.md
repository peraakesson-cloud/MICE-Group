## 1. Objectives of the 90 days

The 90 days do not aim to sell 80 MSEK. They aim to (a) start the no-regret actions that cannot wait, (b) replace the largest unknowns with company data, and (c) reach Gate 0 (15 Dec) and the end-January re-baseline with a defensible 2027 target. Everything irreversible waits for 31 Jan 2027 (strategy principle 7).

## 2. Approach and operating principles

1. **Sequence by dependency, not by enthusiasm.** Order of precedence: data and guarantee first, then SLA and tracking, then spend. No Google or Meta spend before the 13 Nov Kammarkollegiet call, the 20 Nov SLA and FX policy, tracking v1 (30 Nov) and held supply for any promoted departure.
2. **One programme owner, one backlog, one release calendar.** Website (WS04/15/26), CRM (WS13/25), measurement (WS16) share one backlog and a change freeze 15 Dec to 15 Mar for risky changes (WS04) so the Jan-Mar peak is not disturbed. [Conflict with the 6-hub publication by 31 Dec: hubs are low-risk content releases, allowed under the release checklist; see section 13.]
3. **Do the fast, free work first.** Manual response-time log from 2 Nov costs nothing and gives the first baseline in two weeks.
4. **Pre-approved only.** Pre-approved by strategy: hire 1 (start 1 Feb), booking-ops coordinator recruitment, SEK 100k Dec paid test, marketing envelope Q4 2026 SEK 300k. Everything else follows the gate table. The tool freeze (decision D2) covers CRM, quote tool, payment provider and email platform. Named exceptions inside the envelope: SEO tool stack, consent platform (CMP) and server-side GTM host, webinar platform.
5. **Work in weekly cycles.** Monday 09:00 15-minute stand-up of workstream owners (programme owner chairs); Friday dashboard snapshot; fortnightly 60-minute growth council steering until Gate 0, then monthly (strategy 16.3).
6. **Written outputs only count.** Each task has an acceptance test. A task is "done" when the output exists in the shared folder and the named acceptor has signed off.
