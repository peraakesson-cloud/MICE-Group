## 3. Portfolio architecture

### 3.1 Principles
1. Few signature products, many tailored variants. The ladder is sold as 6 named signature families, each with fixed anchor itinerary, 2-3 variants and an itinerary library for tailor-made.
2. Every product has an owner, a contracted-supply record, a margin target and a launch cap. No product without all four.
3. Premium shown through inclusions, not discounts. Price is justified by named adviser, Swedish-speaking guide or tailored design, flexibility (children, singles, private upgrades) and a guarantee-protected booking.
4. Extensions are modular and priced separately so they can be attached to any signature product at quote stage and at the pre-departure upsell moment.
5. Gate before launch (strategy 13.7): >= 60% of planned volume contracted, age policy checked, DMC and guide confirmed, margin >= 18% (>= 15% post-commission for partner sales).

## 4. Self-drive vs guided: decision rules

| Dimension | Guided (T1-T2) | Tailor-made with driver/transfer (T3-T4) | Self-drive (T5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role in plan | Brand, margin, controlled supply (blocks and fixed departures) | Volume core | DIY defence and entry; SEO and YouTube-led |
| Supply | Block contracts with minimums and cancel-by dates; guides | Allocation via DMCs and direct lodge rates | Rental cars, rooms; George Airport and CTIA capacity limits |
| Margin | Floor 18% (T1) / 20% (T2); lower risk of FX if priced early | 20-22% | 18%; thin but high conversion |
| Risk | Under-fill (cancel departure), guide key-person risk | Service load per booking is high (8 h per booking illustrative, strategy) | Safety (roads, night driving), peak car shortage |
| Volume cap | 41 departures in 2028 core | Limited by adviser capacity (5 FTE added) | Cap Garden Route self-drive to confirmed car and room supply |
| Safety/regulatory | Tour leader briefings | Standard | Route book, "safe-by-design" itineraries, no night driving, app, jourtelefon (strategy P2 safety hub) |

Rules for steering customers:
- Customers with children under 12, first-timers who fear driving, and 60+ are steered to T1/T2/T3 with transfers; self-drive is offered to repeat visitors, 30-55 couples and those who already searched "hyrbil" or "egen bil" [ASSUMPTION based on SEO cluster naming].
- A self-drive booking is never the endpoint: after 14 days an adviser offers a "guided day" upgrade (Cape Town day, winelands, safari day) as a priced add-on.
- Self-drive is planned at 8% of 2028 turnover, because strategy treats it as volume complement and DIY defence; if its margin falls below 15% it is cut back.

## 7. Pricing architecture

### 7.1 Principles
1. **No discounting for volume.** A 10% price cut destroys 50-70% of profit (research/10, secondary benchmark). Win with value-adds (extra night, upgrade, included excursion), never headline price cuts.
2. **Price is the plan's biggest unforced error.** Cape Town luxury ADR +8.7% CAGR, Kruger fee +14.9% from 1 Nov 2026 and a krona about 7% weaker vs rand over 12 months (research/05, research/10). If list prices lag costs, blended margin falls from 20% to about 17.8% in 2027 and 16.8% in 2028 (strategy section 11). Rate cards are therefore repriced on every renewal.
3. **Cost-plus floor, value-based ceiling.** Floor = fully loaded cost + margin floor by tier + FX buffer; ceiling = competitor cluster + 15-20% premium for the tier. Quote system shows both.
4. **Transparent price.** Price includes mandatory costs (park fees, resort fees where applicable); no hidden extras, in line with package law (research/09) and the "tips included" selling point.

### 7.2 Price build (per departure or per quote)

Price = (land cost + air cost + guide/hosting allocation + insurance of risk) x (1 + margin) + FX buffer + guarantee/levy + payment cost.

| Component | Rule | Source of rule |
|---|---|---|
| Land cost (ZAR-denominated) | From rate cards signed in ZAR with ZAR clause, or USD-linked for premium lodges | strategy G7; research/10 section 143 |
| Air | Fare class and carrier bought at group or consolidator rate; dual routing (Gulf plus European hub) | strategy 13.8 |
| Guide/host | Allocated per departure; full cost loaded even at low fill | strategy 13.5 |
| FX buffer | 6% on foreign-currency cost share of 60% (about 3.6% of price), hedge 50-70% | strategy A24 |
| Guarantee and levy | 0.6% levy on package price; guarantee increment 1.5-3.7 MSEK on core turnover | research/10 section 1; strategy A24 |
| Payment fees | Card/Klarna/bank costs 1.0-2.5% [ASSUMPTION] | to be replaced with PSP contract |
| Margin | Tier floors in section 3.2; blended >= 20% | strategy G1 |

### 7.4 Pricing mechanics

- **Published price and "från" price rule:** every "från" price states whether flights are included, duration, season and room type (e.g. "Från 41 900 kr, flyg ingår, 12 dagar, dubbelrum, avresa maj"). The "från" price shown must be achievable on at least 3 published dates [ASSUMPTION; consumer information rule to be confirmed with legal, research/09].
- **Single supplement:** fixed at 18-25% of pp price on guided; shared-room matching option; competitor SEK 7,998 is the reference.
- **Child pricing:** child 2-11 at 60-75% on malaria-free family products where lodges allow; many lodges require age 12+ [research/05], so the product page states age policy.
- **Departure-level pricing:** each guided departure is priced individually (date, flight cost, season) rather than a single price per itinerary. Three price levels per departure: base, last 8 seats, last 3 seats.
- **Staged release:** release 2028 dates and prices in Q1 2027 for early-bird; reprice when rate cards renew. No irreversible commitments before 31 Jan 2027 (strategy A1).
- **Early-bird by value-add:** booking >= 10 months ahead gets a defined add-on (extra night in Cape Town, or an excursion) not a discount; this also pulls cash forward (SEK 3,000 registration fee within 10 days is existing policy).
- **Price-change clause:** present in all contracts; pre-departure surcharge notifications only when costs warrant; the 8% cancellation threshold governs.
- **Partner and B2B price:** net rate = published price less commission such that margin after commission >= 15% (strategy L8). Each partner under 10% of turnover.
- **Price transparency to advisers:** margin shown per proposal; approval needed to go below tier floor.
- **Price monitoring:** monthly competitor price sheet for 8 reference itineraries (Albatros, Tourcompass, Temaresor, Afrikakompaniet, Afrika Safariresor, Ving/TUI/Apollo Cape Town) [owned by Product with Marketing].
- **Price test:** A/B test price display on 3 itineraries on the site (e.g. with and without flights, per person vs total); only after the baseline analysis, 8-week windows.
