## 3. Portfolio architecture

### 3.1 Principles
1. Few signature products, many tailored variants. The ladder is sold as 16 signature itineraries (S1-S16, section 3.3) across five tiers, each with a fixed anchor itinerary, 1-3 variants and a place in the itinerary library used for tailor-made quotes.
2. Every product has an owner, a contracted-supply record, a margin target and a launch cap. No product without all four.
3. Premium shown through inclusions, not discounts. Price is justified by named adviser, Swedish-speaking guide or tailored design, flexibility (children, singles, private upgrades) and a guarantee-protected booking.
4. Extensions are modular and priced separately so they can be attached to any signature product at quote stage and at the pre-departure upsell moment.
5. Gate before launch (strategy 13.7): >= 60% of planned volume contracted, age policy checked, DMC and guide confirmed, margin >= 18% (>= 15% post-commission for partner sales), and a launch cap equal to contracted supply.

## 4. Self-drive vs guided: decision rules

| Dimension | Guided (T1-T2) | Tailor-made with driver/transfer (T3-T4) | Self-drive (T5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role in plan | Brand, margin, controlled supply (blocks and fixed departures) | Volume core | DIY defence and entry; SEO and YouTube-led |
| Supply | Block contracts with minimums and cancel-by dates; guides | Allocation via DMCs and direct lodge rates | Rental cars, rooms; George Airport and CTIA capacity limits |
| Margin | Floor 18% (T1) / 20% (T2); lower risk of FX if priced early | 20-22% | 18%; thin but high conversion |
| Risk | Under-fill (cancel departure), guide key-person risk | Service load per booking is high (8 h per booking illustrative, strategy) | Safety (roads, night driving), peak car shortage |
| Volume cap | 36 scheduled plus 5 held departures in 2028 core (strategy: about 41; section 8) | Limited by adviser capacity (5 FTE added) | Cap Garden Route self-drive to confirmed car and room supply |
| Safety/regulatory | Tour leader briefings | Standard | Route book, "safe-by-design" itineraries, no night driving, app, jourtelefon (strategy pillar P2, safety and practical hub) |

Rules for steering customers:
- Customers with children under 12, first-timers who fear driving, and 60+ are steered to T1/T2/T3 with transfers; self-drive is offered to repeat visitors, 30-55 couples and those who already searched "hyrbil" or "egen bil" [ASSUMPTION based on SEO cluster naming].
- A self-drive booking is never the endpoint: after 14 days an adviser offers a "guided day" upgrade (Cape Town day, winelands, safari day) as a priced add-on.
- Self-drive is planned at about 8% of 2028 turnover (5.0 of 64.4 MSEK), because the strategy treats it as volume complement and DIY defence; if its margin falls below 15% it is cut back. Margin floor for T5 is 18% (G1 ladder-entry logic applied by this plan).

## 7. Pricing architecture

### 7.1 Principles
1. **No discounting for volume.** A 10% price cut destroys 50-70% of profit (research/10, secondary benchmark). Win with value-adds (extra night, upgrade, included excursion), never headline price cuts.
2. **Price is the plan's biggest unforced error.** Cape Town luxury ADR +8.7% CAGR, Kruger fee +14.9% from 1 Nov 2026 and a krona about 7% weaker vs rand over 12 months (research/05, research/10). If list prices lag costs, blended margin falls from 20% to about 17.8% in 2027 and 16.8% in 2028 (strategy section 11). Rate cards are therefore repriced on every renewal. Low-season rates from suppliers are passed through at the same percentage margin; this is cost-based seasonal pricing, not a discount.
3. **Cost-plus floor, value-based ceiling.** Floor = fully loaded cost + margin floor by tier + FX buffer; ceiling = competitor cluster + 15-20% premium for the tier. Quote system shows both.
4. **Transparent price.** Price includes mandatory costs (park fees, resort fees where applicable); no hidden extras, in line with package law (research/09) and the "tips included" selling point.

### 7.2 Price build (per departure or per quote)

Price = (land cost + air cost + guide/hosting allocation + risk reserve) / (1 - target gross margin), plus FX buffer, fund levy and payment cost, each expressed as a share of list price. Gross margin is a share of price (strategy A7), so a 20% margin needs a divisor of 0.80, not a 1.20 markup (a 1.20 markup yields only 16.7%).

| Component | Rule | Source of rule |
|---|---|---|
| Land cost (ZAR-denominated) | From rate cards signed in ZAR with ZAR clause, or USD-linked for premium lodges | strategy G7; research/10 section 5.2 |
| Air | Fare class and carrier bought at group or consolidator rate; dual routing (Gulf plus European hub) | strategy 13.8 |
| Guide/host | Allocated per departure; full cost loaded even at low fill | strategy 13.5 |
| FX buffer | 6% of list price (range 5-7%) with a legal price-adjustment clause; hedge 50-70% of known foreign-currency cost at confirmation. Do not apply the 6% to the 60% cost share only (about 3.6% of price): the strategy rejected buffers below 6% | strategy G3 and section 12.4 |
| Guarantee and levy | 0.6% levy on package price goes into the price; the guarantee increment (1.5-3.7 MSEK on core turnover) is a financing cost handled by Finance, not a per-trip price component | research/10 section 1; strategy A19, 12.4 |
| Payment fees | Card/Klarna/bank costs 1.0-2.5% [ASSUMPTION] | to be replaced with PSP contract |
| Margin | Tier floors in section 3.2; blended >= 20%; promotions and value-adds at most 2% of turnover | strategy G1, G2 |

### 7.4 Pricing mechanics

- **Published price and "från" price rule:** every "från" price states whether flights are included, duration, season and room type (e.g. "Från 43 900 kr per person, flyg ingår, 12 dagar, dubbelrum, avresa i maj"). The "från" price shown must be achievable on at least 3 published dates [ASSUMPTION; consumer information rule to be confirmed with legal, research/09].
- **Single supplement:** fixed at 18-25% of pp price on guided; shared-room matching option; competitor SEK 7,998 is the reference.
- **Child pricing:** child 2-11 at 60-75% [ASSUMPTION] on malaria-free family products where lodges allow; many lodges require age 12+ [research/05], so the product page states age policy.
- **Departure-level pricing:** each guided departure is priced individually (date, flight cost, season) rather than a single price per itinerary. Three price levels per departure: base, last 8 seats (+3%), last 3 seats (+6%) [ASSUMPTION]. Prices only step up as seats sell; there are no last-minute markdowns on fixed departures except by CFO-approved fill rescue (section 12 stop/cut rules).
- **Staged release:** release 2028 dates and prices in Q1 2027 for early-bird; reprice when rate cards renew. No irreversible commitments before 31 Jan 2027 (strategy exec summary item 7 and 3.4). Fixed-price departures are published only after the FX policy (due 20 Nov 2026) and the ZAR-linked price-adjustment clause are in the terms (strategy 11.3).
- **Early-bird by value-add:** booking >= 10 months ahead gets a defined add-on (extra night in Cape Town, or an excursion) not a discount; this also pulls cash forward (SEK 3,000 registration fee within 10 days is existing policy per research/10 section 4.1). The cost of the add-on is funded from supplier rebates and counts toward the 2% promotion cap (strategy G2); CFO signs off each campaign.
- **Price-change clause:** present in all contracts; pre-departure surcharge notifications only when costs warrant; the 8% cancellation threshold governs.
- **Partner and B2B price:** net rate = published price less commission such that margin after commission >= 15%, and the net-price floor is never below the direct public price (strategy G5, L8). Each partner under 10% of turnover.
- **Price transparency to advisers:** margin shown per proposal; approval needed to go below tier floor.
- **Price monitoring:** monthly competitor price sheet for 8 reference itineraries (Albatros, Tourcompass, Temaresor, Afrikakompaniet, Afrika Safariresor, Ving/TUI/Apollo Cape Town) [owned by Product with Marketing].
- **Price test:** A/B test price display on 3 itineraries on the site (e.g. with and without flights, per person vs total); only after the baseline analysis, 8-week windows.
