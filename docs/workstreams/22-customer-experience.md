## 1. Why this workstream exists

1. **Service quality is a hard gate, not a nice-to-have.** Strategy G8: Trustpilot rolling 4.2+ (target 4.5+), response SLA met, and complaints and ARN cases per 1,000 travellers inside a threshold set from baseline. If breached for two consecutive weeks, paid media falls back to Tranche A. This workstream defines how those numbers are produced and defended (strategy section 4, 13.9, 14).
2. **Volume rises about 57-60% by 2028 on a team that grows by about 5 FTE.** Core plan: 890 travellers / 405 bookings (2026) to 1,400 / 637 (2028 core) and 1,740 / 790 (stretch). Guided departures 26 to 41 (51 stretch). Peak load about 37 to 58 travellers a week (72 stretch) (strategy 13.1, illustrative [A]). Service that works at 405 bookings through personal heroics will fail at 637-790.
3. **The brand promise is personal.** "Named personal adviser", "Swedish-speaking guide", "booking protected by a travel guarantee" are promises the customer tests in the journey. Where we cannot yet staff a promise (24/7 local presence), copy must not make it (strategy 6, 13.4).
4. **Public proof is currently weak.** Trustpilot showed 8 reviews at 2.7/5 with two recent negatives about responsiveness and a guide (https://se.trustpilot.com/review/sydafrikaresor.se, via R03, as read for the strategy). Paid and SEO traffic will be sent into that comparison. Service at 2x volume is the only durable fix; workstream 12 harvests it.
5. **Legal exposure scales with volume.** The organiser must remedy errors during the trip, price reduction and damages are possible, and safety events can create refund rights within 14 days (R09 sections 2 and 6; https://www.konsumentverket.se/lagar/paketreselagen-konsument/). Incident volume scales with peak volume (strategy 13.4).
6. **Evidence caution.** The relation between NPS, review score and conversion is an assumption (R09 A5). We therefore measure it (section 9.3) before attaching spend to it.

## 4. Service standards (internal charter, "Servicenormer")

These are internal standards. Public promises are limited to those achieved (see 12.4). The word "garanti" is not used externally for these standards, to avoid confusion with the legal resegaranti.

**Sales and booking**
- S1 First reply to any enquiry per WS14 speed-to-lead SLA; first reply to any message in a booked file within 4 working hours (1 working day until Q3 2027 as ramp) [A].
- S2 Quote delivered within 2 working days of a qualified brief [A]; follow-up cadence D2/D7/D14/D21/D30 per R11.
- S3 One named adviser per booked file, plus one named back-up with file access. Every adviser's holiday cover is booked into the shared capacity calendar (strategy 13.3).
- S4 Booking confirmation, terms, standard pre-contract information form (including resegaranti and Kammarkollegiet lookup link) and cancellation conditions in one message (R09 sections 2 and 3).
- S5 Price, deposit, balance dates, FX clause and what is included in plain Swedish; any mandatory local cost shown (R09 pre-contract information).

**Pre-departure**
- S6 Touchpoint every 21 days (C4). Content: itinerary updates, health and malaria note where relevant (R09: malaria risk in parts of Kruger/Mpumalanga/Limpopo/KwaZulu-Natal, especially Oct-Apr), passport/visa reminder, link to UD/Embassy page.
- S7 Documents and briefing 21+ days before departure (C5). Emergency card (PDF + wallet) with jourtelefon, insurer number, DMC contact and Embassy contact.
- S8 Schedule change by airline or supplier communicated to the customer within 1 working day of our knowledge, with a proposed alternative (aligned with airline-schedule-change playbook, section 7).

**In destination**
- S9 Jourtelefon under resan staffed by a person with file access, urgent calls answered within 15 minutes, 24/7. This is the minimum until the 24/7 model decision of 31 Jan 2027 (strategy 13.4).
- S10 Arrival confirmation and day-2 check-in message ("Hur är det? Allt som det ska?") on all trips; for guided departures the guide reports daily to the in-trip desk.
- S11 Every incident logged (section 6.3), with owner, time to response, resolution and goodwill given.

**After the trip**
- S12 Welcome-home message at day 3 with the private rating and NPS question (SMS + email; one survey, aligned with WS12 5.3); call within 1 working day for NPS 0-6 or rating 1-3 (WS12 5.4); review invitation at day 10 to every returned traveller, not filtered by score, with reminders per WS12 5.1. Rationale: selective invitation of satisfied customers is treated as review gating and is reported to breach Trustpilot and Google rules (secondary sources, see 14.1; confirm in CX-A12).
- S13 Complaint standard: acknowledgement same working day, substantive answer within 5 working days, resolution in 15 (C9). Complaints about package-travel errors are treated as legal notices of non-conformity (no "we will look into it" without a date).
- S14 Standing offer to customers in dispute: reference to ARN. Public statement "vi följer ARN:s rekommendationer" is used only if true [VERIFY with CEO] (R09).

**Capacity safeguards for 2x**
- S15 No adviser carries more than the maximum active files for their stage mix (starting assumption 55 pre-departure files plus 12 in-trip files [ASSUMPTION], replaced by real data from the 30 Nov 2026 handling-time pull). When the cap is hit, new leads route to the next adviser; if all are at cap, the Head of Sales triggers a "capacity yellow" (section 10).
- S16 Shared capacity calendar with Pacific and MICE for the southern-hemisphere peak (strategy 13.3).

## 5. NPS and feedback system

### 5.1 Design
- **Metric.** Relationship NPS asked after the trip ("Hur sannolikt är det att du rekommenderar Sydafrikaresor till en vän eller kollega?" 0-10) plus one open question. NPS = % promoters (9-10) minus % detractors (0-6).
- **Transactional touchpoints.** Four micro-surveys of one question + comment: (1) after quote ("Var offerten tydlig?" 1-5), (2) after booking ("Hur var bokningsupplevelsen?"), (3) in-trip day 2 via SMS/WhatsApp, (4) after the trip (NPS). The in-trip day-2 check is expected to be the most useful early-warning item (hypothesis, to be tested against the first season of data). WS12 5.2 specifies the mid-trip pulse on day 3; there is one message only, owned by this workstream, and the send day (2 or 3) is fixed jointly with the WS12 owner by 20 Nov.
- **Segments.** NPS reported by product (guided / tailor-made / self-drive / extensions), departure month, adviser, DMC, guide, hotel/lodge. Driver questions: adviser, itinerary, guide, accommodation, transfers, support, value for money.
- **Consent and GDPR.** Surveys are service communication tied to the contract; marketing use of comments and review invitations require the correct legal basis and consent flags; legal review in November (R09: standard GDPR rules assumed [A]).
- **Tool.** Start in the existing CRM or a low-cost survey tool; Trustpilot invitation flow integrated per WS12. Budget in section 9.1; selection by 20 Nov 2026 (decision between native CRM survey, Typeform-class tool or Trustpilot invitations) [ASSUMPTION: no tool inventory available].

### 5.2 Closed-loop process
| Trigger | Action | Owner | Time limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| NPS 0-6 (or rating 1-3) | Personal call by adviser (not by the person who handled the trip, where possible); goodwill decided on the merits and never linked to a review (WS12 5.4) | Head of Sales / senior adviser | 1 working day |
| NPS 7-8 | Thank-you message plus "what could have been better" | Adviser | 3 working days |
| NPS 9-10 | Referral ask (L7, day 14) and story invitation (day 90), both per WS12 5.3. The review invitation is not a trigger here: it goes to everyone at day 10 | CRM manager | Day 14 / day 90 |
| Mentions of guide, DMC or lodge in a detractor comment | Added to supplier scorecard; discussed at monthly supplier review | Head of Product | Monthly |
| Public review 3 stars or below | Public reply within 2 working days (WS12, O5), then private recovery | CRM manager | 2 working days |

### 5.3 Reporting rhythm
- **Weekly (Monday, 30 min):** CX scorecard (section 9.2) to Head of Sales, Head of Product, CRM manager, in-trip desk lead.
- **Monthly:** supplier/guide/DMC scorecard, top 5 detractor themes, fixes and owners; fed to the programme owner.
- **Quarterly:** NPS drivers analysis, link to repeat/referral share and review velocity, input to the bridge re-baseline (strategy 16.3).

### 5.4 Credibility rules
- NPS bonuses to individual advisers are not used (gaming risk); use a team-level quality bonus linked to C3, C5, C6, C9 with 50% weighting on NPS trend [ASSUMPTION; HR to decide].
- The survey is sent to all returned bookings, not only the happy ones. The review invitation is also sent to all returned travellers (no satisfaction gate): the plan routes detractors to a private recovery call in addition, but never filters who is invited and never suppresses public reviews. Rationale: secondary sources report that Trustpilot requires businesses to invite customers without selecting on expected sentiment and that Google treats review gating as a policy breach (https://www.seologist.com/knowledge-sharing/what-is-review-gating-and-why-does-it-violate-googles-review-policies/ , secondary). The earlier draft of this plan (and strategy P1 wording "gated by satisfaction") conflicted with that and with WS12 5.3, which invites all travellers. [VERIFY against Trustpilot's current guidelines and with Legal (WS31) before launch.]

## 6. In-destination support model

### 6.1 Principle
The value proposition promises "one point of contact before, during and after" (strategy 8.1); this section is how that promise is kept. In destination, the DMC and guides are our hands, the in-trip desk is our brain, and the customer's single number is the jourtelefon.

| C. 24/7 phone line with named escalation (Swedish duty rota) | Swedish-speaking duty adviser on rota with phone number; DMC/guide executes locally | Lowest cost; Swedish-language; scalable | Time-zone (SA is CET+1 in winter and same time as CEST in summer, so day hours align; night is Swedish night); duty fatigue | SEK 0.15 M (2027) and 0.3 M (2028), strategy placeholder |

**Recommendation (to be decided by CEO by 31 Jan 2027):** start with C plus A for 2027 (duty rota with DMC SLA), use "jourtelefon under resan" in copy, and re-decide on B at Gate 2 (30 Jun 2027) using real incident volume, with trigger: more than 12 urgent in-trip incidents a week in two consecutive peak weeks, or peak guided departures above 5 concurrent [ASSUMPTION thresholds]. Only after B or a proven C/A with 95% C6 for three months can the "lokal närvaro dygnet runt" claim be considered, and then only after legal review of the wording.

Note on time zone: South Africa Standard Time is UTC+2 year-round; Sweden is UTC+1 in winter and UTC+2 in summer, so the difference is one hour in Swedish winter (the South African summer peak) and zero in Swedish summer. A Swedish duty phone therefore covers the South African day at close to normal Swedish hours; only South African night calls land in the Swedish night.

## 7. Crisis handling and disruption playbook

Strategy 13.8 requires a written disruption playbook (Gulf closure, schedule change, rebooking 100+ travellers in one week) and insurance/war-risk cover check by 31 Jan 2027. This section is the customer-facing and operational core of it; air-supply content is joined with WS18.

### 7.1 Severity levels
| Level | Definition | Examples | Lead | Response clock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S4 Minor | One customer, no safety issue, resolvable locally | Late transfer, room change, minor illness | Duty adviser | Respond 15 min; resolve same day |
| S3 Moderate | One or several customers, material trip disruption | Lodge overbooking, missed flight, lost baggage, guide sick | In-trip desk lead | Respond 15 min; plan in 2 h |
| S2 Major | Many customers or safety/health incident for one | Hospitalisation, crime against traveller, DMC failure, vehicle accident, schedule collapse for one departure | Head of Product + in-trip desk lead | Crisis team convened within 1 h |
| S1 Critical | Whole category or 20+ travellers affected | UD travel warning or Embassy alert, Gulf hub closure, civil unrest, major natural disaster, supplier insolvency, outbreak | CEO (crisis lead), CFO, Head of Product, Head of Marketing, Legal | Crisis team convened within 30 min; first customer message within 2 h |

### 7.2 Crisis team and roles (S1/S2)
- **Crisis lead:** CEO (S1) or Head of Product (S2).
- **Operations lead:** in-trip desk lead (customers in destination, rebooking, DMC).
- **Customer communications lead:** Head of Sales (customers on trip, booked, enquiring).
- **Public communications lead:** Head of Marketing (website banner, social, media, paid media pause).
- **Finance/legal lead:** CFO and Legal (refund obligations, guarantee, insurer, Kammarkollegiet, airline claims).
- **Scribe:** coordinator logs decisions and times.
- Meeting rhythm: every 2 hours during acute phase, then twice a day; status note after each.

### 7.3 First 24 hours (checklist)
1. Open incident record; confirm facts from at least two sources (UD/Embassy, DMC, airline, news); do not speculate publicly.
2. List affected travellers: in destination, departing within 14 days, departing within 90 days, booked beyond. Export from CRM within 1 hour.
3. Account for every in-destination traveller (contact, safe, needs). Target: 100% contacted within 6 hours for S1.
4. First customer message within 2 hours (template in section 12.5): what we know, what we are doing, when the next update comes. Update at fixed times even if nothing changes.
5. Pause or reduce paid media and scheduled social posts for affected markets/destinations within 2 hours (WS06, WS07, WS10 co-ordination); switch off promotional email.
6. Website banner with factual status and contact route; update the safety page.
7. Alternative arrangements: rebooking options via airlines and the airline partnerships of WS18; insurer notification; DMC capacity for extended stay.
8. Decision log: refunds, free rebooking, goodwill. Apply one consistent policy across customers.
9. Legal check: right to cancel without fee in case of unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances at destination or en route, refund within 14 days (R09, Konsumentverket). Message must not mislead about rights.
10. Notify Kammarkollegiet and bank if liquidity or guarantee exposure changes materially (strategy 12.4, R09 section 3).

|---|---|---|

### 8.3 Capacity signals (traffic light, reviewed weekly)
| Colour | Rule [A] | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Median first reply under 4 h and enquiry SLA (C13) met; adviser load below 85% of cap | Normal |
| Yellow | Median first reply 4-8 h for 2 days, or any adviser at 100% cap | Reroute leads; temp help from Pacific/MICE; pause quotes for low-intent leads; notify programme owner |
| Red | Reply above 1 working day for 5 days, or any G8 leg breached two consecutive weeks (definition below) | G8 breach process: cut paid media to Tranche A, freeze new campaigns, temporary hire or outsourced admin support |

**G8 breach definition (so the hard gate is operable).** Strategy G8 has three legs; a breach exists when any leg fails in two consecutive weekly readings: (1) enquiry SLA below 90% answered under 1 h (C13, WS14); (2) Trustpilot rolling score below 4.2 (C14); (3) complaints plus ARN cases per 1,000 travellers above the threshold set from baseline by 31 Jan 2027. Because weekly traveller counts are small (about 20 returning travellers in an average week), leg 3 is read as a rolling 13-week rate with a minimum of 100 returned travellers in the window, plus an absolute alarm of 3 or more written complaints in one week [ASSUMPTION thresholds]. The Red rule above applies in addition. Consequence of a breach: paid media falls to Tranche A until fixed (strategy G8).

**Open point for the CEO on leg 2.** Trustpilot is 2.7 on 8 reviews today (R03), so a literal 4.2 floor is breached from day one and would hold paid media at Tranche A regardless of service quality. Proposal [CEO decision]: apply leg 2 from Gate 1 (31 Mar 2027) on reviews posted in the trailing 90 days with a minimum of 15 reviews, consistent with the WS12 Gate 1 reading of 40 reviews at 4.0+; before Gate 1 paid spend is already capped at SEK 0.5 M (strategy 11.3).

### 8.4 Peak management
- Peak weeks (southern-hemisphere summer and Jun-Sep) flagged in the shared capacity calendar; no vacations for in-trip desk staff in peak weeks; seasonal temp resource for admin (documents and invoices) from Nov 2027 [A]; budgeted in 9.1 (SEK 50,000 for Nov-Dec 2027, SEK 120,000 in 2028).
- Shift balance: Pacific and MICE departures mapped to avoid overlap with SA peaks (strategy 13.3).

### 9.3 Measurement of the experience-revenue link (to avoid claiming unproven value)
- **Link 1 NPS to repeat/referral:** match NPS cohort 2027 with 2028 bookings and referrals; report by NPS band. Needs customer ID across systems.
- **Link 2 review score to conversion:** per WS12 design; do not count in the bridge, see L7 and WS12.
- **Link 3 responsiveness to close rate:** close rate by time-to-first-contact band (R11 cites HBR 2011, a US B2B lead study in which contact within one hour was associated with much higher qualification, https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads; directional only for leisure travel). It is consistent with, but does not prove, the L2 assumption (25% to 27%); the own-data test is what counts.
- **Link 4 incident handling to retention:** repeat rate of customers with a resolved incident versus none.
- No bridge value is booked for CX alone; its contribution is protecting L2, L7 and the gate G8 so that paid spend (L3) can continue.
