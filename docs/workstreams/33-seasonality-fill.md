## 1. Summary

L10 is deliberately the smallest lever in the bridge (+1.0 MSEK in 2028, about 4% of the +24.4 MSEK core increment and about 1.6% of the 64.4 MSEK plan of record; about 10 incremental bookings at about SEK 101k per booking [strategy sections 9 and 10]). Its real value is not the headline MSEK but three things:

1. **Supply relief.** The binding constraint on a 2x plan is peak beds and peak seats (R05, R04). Every booking moved from the peak periods (Jun-Aug and 15 Dec-10 Jan per workstream 03) into Apr-May and Sep-Nov uses less contested supply. Caveat: Hermanus and the Cape in September are themselves tight (section 3), so September is not automatically "free" capacity; Apr-May and Oct-Nov are the safer relief months.
2. **Margin.** Shoulder and green-season rates are usually lower, so the same product can carry the same SEK margin at a lower customer price, or a higher margin at the same price [ASSUMPTION: no rate cards available; Wesgro data in section 3 shows off-peak demand growing 22-31%, which may narrow off-peak discounts]. Target margin for season products is at least 18% (strategy 13.7 launch gate; blended 20% per G1), with 22%+ as an unproven goal on green-season bundles [ASSUMPTION].
3. **Fallback if Gate 0 shows thin peak supply.** Strategy section 13 says that if held supply at 15 Dec 2026 is below the 48 MSEK equivalent, marketing is aimed at the shoulder months and alternative lodges. This plan is that fallback, prepared in advance.

Plan in one line: build **five dated, capped "season stories"** ("Valarnas kust", "Blomning och vin", "Skörd och smak", "Grön säsong", "Påsk och maj"; section 5) on existing supply, open sales on each only after its supply gate is passed, sell with a 6-9 month lead through email, SEO and a small Meta/YouTube test budget, and measure them by departures per month, margin and incrementality, not by clicks.

## 5. Season products

All products are built from existing DMC and lodge supply wherever possible (R05 model A, net rate via DMC); no new irreversible commitments before 31 Jan 2027. Each product has a launch cap equal to contracted supply. Prices below are planning ranges, not quotes [ASSUMPTION: derived from the client's public "from" ladder SEK 34.5k-69.9k (R02) and a shoulder season index of 85-92 versus peak 100 from workstream 03; replace with rate cards].

Naming: the working names below are Swedish customer-facing names; they map to workstream 02 products S12 (whales, vin, Walker Bay) and S13 (green season). Product definitions and price tiers are owned by workstream 02; this plan only adds season timing, caps and marketing.

### 5.1 "Valarnas kust" (whales and Cape coast) - Aug-Nov departures
- **Concept:** Cape Town + Hermanus/Walker Bay (land-based whale watching, optional boat) + Winelands, optional Garden Route and a 3-4 night safari add-on in a malaria-free reserve.
- **Formats:** (a) guided, Swedish-speaking guide, 12-16 days, fixed departures in mid-Sep and early Oct; (b) tailor-made template for couples and 55+.
- **Price band:** SEK 42-52k pp incl. flights [ASSUMPTION]. The strategy positions the guided 13-day product (SEK 44.3k) about 15-20% above the group-with-flights cluster (SEK 36-40k); this band keeps the guided format at that premium and leaves the tailor-made format below it.
- **Why it works (hypothesis):** peak whale window (Aug-Oct) coincides with the cheapest flight month (Sep, R04) and the tail of the quiet Garden Route season; whale watching is possible from shore so the activity itself has no supply bottleneck. Temaresor already sells a whale-and-wine departure, which shows the combination is marketable but does not prove Swedish volume.
- **Supply gate:** Hermanus/Overberg and Cape Town rooms for September 2027 contracted by 31 Mar 2027 (Hermanus September is tight; book 2-3 months ahead per source, so the group block must be secured earlier).
- **Pilots:** 2 departures in 2027 (mid-Sep, early Oct), 4 in 2028.

### 5.2 "Blomning och vin" (wildflowers and wine) - Aug-Oct departures
- **Concept:** wildflower route (West Coast/Namaqualand-type area, dates depend on the year's rains) combined with Cape Town and Winelands food and wine. Targets 50+ couples and friends; fits the food-and-wine segment (SEK 44-58k, R02).
- **Risk:** wildflower bloom is weather-dependent and short. Therefore the flower is an add-on day or extension, not the sole reason to travel. Marketing must say "i blomningstid, beroende på årets regn" (no guarantee). [ASSUMPTION: bloom window Aug-Sep; not sourced in research, verify with DMC before any copy goes live.]
- **Price band:** SEK 42-58k pp incl. flights [ASSUMPTION].
- **Pilot:** tailor-made only in 2027 (no fixed departure); evaluate for a fixed 2028 departure on demand evidence.

### 5.3 "Skörd och smak" (wine harvest and food) - Jan-Mar harvest, plus Apr-May
- **Concept:** harvest season in the Winelands and Cape Town food scene; April/May variants with milder, calmer Cape and Garden Route [ASSUMPTION: general knowledge, verify with DMC].
- **Note:** harvest (Jan-Mar) overlaps peak A; sold as a premium add-on to existing departures rather than a standalone, to avoid cannibalising peak. The Apr-May version is the real shoulder play. [ASSUMPTION: harvest timing from general knowledge; verify.]
- **Price band:** SEK 44-58k pp [ASSUMPTION].
- **Pilot:** 1 fixed departure late April 2027 if supply is held, else tailor-made only.

### 5.4 "Grön säsong" (green season) - Nov, and Jan-Mar where supply is spare
- **Concept:** newborn animals, lush bush, lower rates, fewer vehicles, malaria-aware routing (malaria-free reserves for families; R01/R05). Includes a clear message on rain and heat so expectations are managed.
- **Target segment:** value-conscious safari buyers and families with flexible dates; pairs with lodge green-season rates (workstream 03 season index 75-85 [ASSUMPTION]).
- **Price band:** SEK 36-45k pp incl. flights [ASSUMPTION], i.e. the strategy entry ladder (SEK 36-45k, G1 margin gate >= 18%). Nothing below SEK 36k without CEO approval (workstream 02).
- **Pilot:** tailor-made in 2027; one family-friendly fixed departure in Nov 2027 only if age policy (many lodges 12+) is cleared.

### 5.5 "Påsk och maj" (April-May shoulder) - Apr-May
- **Concept:** Cape Town + Garden Route + short Kruger/Eastern Cape safari at the end of the green season; Ascension (Kristi himmelsfärd) used to cut required leave days. [ASSUMPTION, computed not sourced, verify against the Swedish calendar: Easter Sunday is 28 Mar 2027, i.e. outside the Apr-May window, so in 2027 Easter departures are a late-March/early-April edge case; Easter Sunday 2028 is 16 Apr, inside the window; Ascension is Thu 6 May 2027 and Thu 25 May 2028; 1 May 2027 is a Saturday and gives no leave-day saving, 1 May 2028 is a Monday.] The product is therefore branded "Påsk och maj" for 2028 and "Vår- och majresa" or similar for 2027 if the Easter hook does not fit.

- **Pilot:** one fixed 55+ small-group departure in late April / early May 2027 (only if guide and supply gates pass); tailor-made April/May templates.

### 5.6 "Valar på vintern" (opportunistic: June-July whale value)

## 6. Go-to-market: offers, pricing and promotion mechanics

Principle: do not discount the brand. Premium is held through value-adds (extra night, upgrade, guide, airport transfer), consistent with "do not compete on lowest price". Shoulder-season rate savings are used partly to lower the customer price and partly to protect margin.

| Mechanic | Detail | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Early-booking value-add | Booking with deposit 6-9 months before departure gets an extra night or excursion (not a % discount) | Cost of value-add capped at 2% of package price, funded from supplier rebates, CFO sign-off per campaign (strategy G2: promotions at most 2% of turnover) |
| Flex-date product | "Välj din månad" - fixed price band for travel in Apr-May or Sep-Nov, the client allocates the best-supplied week | Only on products with held supply |
| Combination bundles | Whales + wine, wine + safari, Garden Route + whales | Margin >= 18% |
| Fare-lock / price-lock | Optional lock of the land price at deposit; air fare lock tied to ticketing deadlines (R04 recommendation: fare-lock dates) | Hedge and buffer per strategy G3: price buffer 6% with legal price-adjustment clause, hedge 50-70% of known cost; fixed-price departures published only after the FX policy (approved by 20 Nov 2026) |
| Past-customer offers | Segmented CRM mailing to prior travellers (repeat share is part of L7, but season offers are the hook) | Frequency cap; avoid double counting with L7 (see sections 11 and 14, risk S7) |
| Group and friends | "Ta med vänner" - 4+ travellers on a shoulder departure get a host benefit | Counts inside the 2% promotion cap (G2); gross margin >= 18%, and >= 15% after commission when partner-sold |
| Partner/affinity | Wine clubs, bird/nature societies, 55+ associations, golf clubs for Apr-May and Sep-Oct (links to workstream 19) | Commission from margin, floor 15% |

Pricing rule: workstream 03 owns the season index; this workstream proposes which departures get promotions and asks for a monthly margin sign-off by the Head of Product.

## 11. Measurement and incrementality

Season products are exactly where "booked, but would have travelled anyway" misleads. Controls:

- **Baseline:** departures per month 2023-2026 from the booking system by product and month. Build a counterfactual from the pre-2027 trend; L10 only counts departures above baseline-adjusted trend in the target windows [ASSUMPTION: data exists].
- **Net-of-cannibalisation rule:** a booking counts as L10 only if the customer's original enquiry was for a peak month or the customer states flexible dates and accepts the shoulder offer (add a sales field "original desired month"). Moves from peak to shoulder are credited at the margin difference as well as the turnover (supply relief) and reported separately; they are not counted as growth.
- **Holdout test (Tranche B rule):** geo or audience holdout on paid prospecting for season campaigns; scale only if incremental ROAS is proven against the 5x break-even in strategy (A15).
- **Dashboard (fed into workstream 16/29):** order book by departure month vs plan, margin per season product, fill rate, cost per booking, lead-to-booking by story.

## 17. Decision rules

- **Scale** a season story if, after one departure cycle: margin >= 18%, fill >= 75%, L10-specific cost per incremental booking <= SEK 36k (2027, SEK 18k in 2028) and the share of customers with a peak-month first enquiry is below 50%.
- **Hold** if one of the four tests is borderline; fix in the next cycle.
- **Cut** if two or more fail, or if the strategy stop-loss (G10: cumulative programme net contribution worse than -3.0 MSEK at 30 Jun 2027) triggers; freeze paid season spend first, keep owned channels.
- **Escalate to fallback** if Gate 0 shows thin peak supply: move marketing weight to Sep-Nov and Apr-May stories and alternative lodges (KZN, Eastern Cape, mid-tier Garden Route).
