## 1. Role of events in the bridge (and what they are not)

Events are a **conversion and trust accelerator**, not a stand-alone lever. They add no new line to the bridge. They feed:

- **L2 (conversion on existing flow, 25% to 27%):** the ~2,148 existing enquiries a year and the CRM backlog are invited; a live session with an adviser answers the objections in WS14 section 9 (price, safety, "wait and see") faster than email.
- **L5 (social, creators, hosted departures, +2.0 MSEK by year 2):** events are the fill mechanism for 4-6 hosted departures and the place where creators/guides meet customers.
- **L7 (repeat and referral):** alumni evenings (photo-night plus "bring a friend") at the three moments in WS14 section 8.5.
- **L8 (partners/affinity):** co-hosted sessions with seniors' organisations, wine/golf clubs, airline and SAT (R12 section 4).

Planning contribution: **plan case about SEK 3 M of incremental booked value per event year (optimistic case 4.5 M), out of about SEK 9.5 M of booked value that events merely touch [ASSUMPTION, section 7]**. By departure year this is roughly SEK 1 M of 2027 turnover and SEK 3 M of 2028 turnover, assuming about one third of event-assisted bookings depart in the year they are booked and two thirds in the following year, and that 2028 events perform like 2027 [ASSUMPTION EV12]. That is about a third of the combined bridge allocation for L2 + L5 + L7 (3.2 MSEK in 2027, 8.5 MSEK in 2028, strategy section 9.1). It is **inside L2/L5/L7, not additive**, and it is a material dependency: if incrementality tests fail (section 8) the other levers must carry the gap. The event budget is cut first among discretionary lines (consistent with WS14's rule to cut events by half if close-rate lift is below +1 pp by Gate 2).

What events must not do: promise "24/7 local presence" (copy says "jourtelefon under resan" until the 31 Jan 2027 decision), publish fixed prices before the FX policy, or sell departures beyond held supply (Gate 0, 15 Dec 2026).

### 4.5 Consent, GDPR and marketing law (hard rules)
- Email marketing to individuals requires consent, with a narrow existing-customer exception; **[VERIFY section number and conditions with counsel]** (research 09). The exception is about customers who have bought; unconverted enquirers are **not** assumed to qualify, so they are invited only if a marketing consent is logged. Registration for an event is not marketing consent; use a separate, unticked checkbox and keep a log (timestamp, wording, source).
- Photo/video: signage at the door, opt-out wristband, written consent for any customer who is filmed or quoted.
- Cookie/pixel consent applies to the registration pages (Consent Mode v2, WS16).
- No consent means a personal 1:1 reply only to the person's own request (F11 rule in WS13). Consequence: the 24 h thank-you may contain the recording link and an answer to their own question; any offer (call invitation, itinerary, D7, D21) goes only to attendees with marketing consent, until counsel confirms in writing that a post-event follow-up to a registrant is a service message [VERIFY]. Telephone follow-up: check NIX-telefon and the consumer telemarketing rules with counsel [VERIFY]; the 48 h contact KPI therefore counts consented or requesting attendees.
- Alcohol: serving wine at an evening may need a serveringstillstånd or a licensed caterer even when free of charge, and a venue's licence does not automatically cover an external organiser; marketing that centres on alcohol is restricted by alkohollagen. Operations/Legal confirm with the municipality and venue before Round 1 invitations; invitation copy below avoids alcohol-led wording [VERIFY].
- Do not state guarantees or insurance specifics beyond what Legal confirms ("resegaranti" only as a verified fact); promotions and giveaways need a rules text approved by Legal and count toward the 2% promotion cap.
- Price claims at events: ranges and "från" only if verified; no fixed-price commitments before the FX policy.

## 7. Funnel model and economics (illustrative, [ASSUMPTION] throughout)

Revenue per booking = 2.2 travellers x SEK 45,000 = ~SEK 99,000 turnover (strategy A1); gross margin 20% = ~SEK 19,800 per booking (strategy A7). "Touched" means booked after attending; "incremental" means the booking would not have happened without the event.

| Channel | Inputs | Result 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Webinars (10) | 120 registrations each, 45% attend = 54; 540 attendees; ~340 parties at 1.6 people per party | 340 x 10% book within 120 days = 34 bookings touched; 40% incremental = ~14 incremental |
| Evenings (8) | 70 registrations x 70% show = 49, planned at 45 each = 360 attendees; ~225 parties | 225 x 15% = 34 touched; 50% incremental = ~17 |
| Fair (Oct 2027) | ~400 parties in leads captured (stand case; a seminar slot captures fewer) | 400 x 7% (booking within 6 months, mostly 2028 departures) = 28 touched; 50% incremental = ~14 |
| **Total** | | **~96 touched bookings (~9.5 MSEK booked value touched), ~45 incremental in the optimistic case (~4.5 MSEK)** |

**Plan case (used in section 1): 30% incremental share on all channels = ~29 incremental bookings = ~2.9 MSEK, about SEK 3 M.** The optimistic case uses the 40-50% shares above. Neither is measured.

Cost 2027 at the funded level (section 6): Q4 2026 pilot 8k + committed 80k + gated 200k = ~SEK 288k, plus about SEK 0.2 M of adviser capacity (section 6) = ~SEK 0.49 M all-in. Incremental gross margin: plan case 29 x 19,800 = ~SEK 0.57 M, optimistic 45 x 19,800 = ~SEK 0.89 M. Return on cash cost: ~2.0x plan case, ~3.1x optimistic; on all-in cost (cash plus capacity): ~1.2x and ~1.8x, which is thin. Own cash per incremental booking: 288k / 29 = ~SEK 9,900 (plan) against the G4 cap of SEK 20,000; all-in ~SEK 17,000, still inside. At 17% margin the plan-case margin falls to ~SEK 0.49 M, which is above cash cost but below all-in cost. Break-even on all-in cost at 20% margin needs ~25 incremental bookings, i.e. ~26% of touched bookings. That is exactly what the incrementality test checks. (The first draft showed 2.1x on SEK 415k of unfunded cost and a 20% break-even share; both are replaced.)

Walk-away rule: if the measured incremental share is below 25% after the first two quarters (Gate 2 read), cut evenings to Stockholm only, turn webinars into evergreen recordings, and skip the stand (seminar slot only). Between 25% and 30% (amber) hold Round 2 at three evenings. Above 30% keep the plan.

| Hosted departure fill | Seats filled from events | 30% of hosted-departure seats [ASSUMPTION] | per departure |
| Service guard | First human reply SLA met (strategy G8/G9) | Round 2, extra evenings and new invitation waves pause while the SLA is missed | weekly |

**Incrementality design (needed to avoid counting a stream we would have won anyway):**
1. For every invitation wave to the existing enquiry/customer list, hold out a random 20% from the invite (they receive the normal nurture). Compare booking rate of everyone invited with the holdout at 120 days (intent to treat, so self-selection into attending does not inflate the result).
2. Matched comparison of attendees with non-attenders (same enquiry month, source, bracket) is **directional only**: attenders are self-selected and the comparison overstates the effect.
3. "How did you hear about us" field (WS16 list includes "Mässa eller event") and a non-price benefit code (e.g. a private Q&A call).
4. Event bookings are labelled `event_assisted` in the CRM; the weekly order-book report shows them separately with margin.
5. Power warning: with an invite pool of about 1,500 and a 20% holdout (300), a 6 pp difference in booking rate is only about 2 standard errors, so single waves will often be inconclusive (the same problem WS16 documents for paid holdouts). Pool waves per quarter, report confidence intervals, and do not cut or expand on one wave.
