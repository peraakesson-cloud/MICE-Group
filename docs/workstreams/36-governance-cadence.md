## 1. Purpose and objectives

The programme has ten guardrails, six gates, 14 levers and about a dozen workstream owners who are mostly existing staff in a 22-person company with a new CEO. The failure modes are known from the strategy: revenue is chased at the cost of margin and service; paid money is released on attributed rather than incremental ROAS; hires and supply commitments run ahead of evidence; a new CEO either micromanages or lacks a single truth. Governance is the mechanism that prevents these, at low overhead.

## 3. Governance design principles

1. **One rhythm, nested.** Weekly steers, monthly allocates, quarterly re-plans, gates release money and headcount. Each forum has one decision type; anything else is information.
2. **Decide at the lowest level that has the evidence and the cost of being wrong.** Pausing spend needs no approval; releasing it needs the four conditions (SLA, incrementality, supply, margin).
3. **Evidence over narrative.** Every proposal to release money carries: the KPI, the baseline, the test or data, the margin effect and what would make us stop. Revenue alone never passes a gate (strategy 16.2).
4. **Two keys for money.** Any release of gated money needs the budget owner (Head of Marketing, Head of Product or Head of Sales as relevant) and the CFO's cash and margin check. The CEO holds the third key only above the committed envelope.
5. **Reversible by default.** No irreversible commitment (long contracts, hires beyond those planned, prepaid supply above cap, multi-year agency terms) before 31 Jan 2027 re-baseline, and afterwards only at a gate.
6. **Default to action on red.** Every red threshold has a pre-agreed action and owner (workstream 29 section 9). The council can override within one meeting; silence means the default executes.
7. **Own the data and the accounts.** The company, not an agency, owns ad accounts, tag containers, analytics, CRM and the customer data. Agencies get manager access.
8. **Small and visible.** A one-page weekly, a six-page monthly, a ten-page quarterly. If a report is not used to make a decision, it is cut.
9. **Decision log, not memory.** Every decision above workstream level is recorded with date, owner, evidence and review date.
10. **The CEO is a decision-maker, not a reporter.** Owners bring decisions and recommendations; the CEO's time goes to gates, trade-offs between workstreams and people.

## 7. Gate governance

Gates are the main control point. All conditions must pass; revenue alone never passes (strategy 16.2). The gate date is fixed; the pack is not allowed to move the date, only the decision (pass, pass with conditions, hold, recut).

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 1 | 31 Mar 2027 | 2027 order book at least 24 MSEK; margin on new bookings at least 19%; SLA met; Trustpilot trending to 4.5; first holdout read; marketing per booking within cap; supply held at least equal to trajectory | Second consultant and booking-ops hire; Tranche B only if iROAS at least 4x; GL1 scope decision | Programme owner |

| 3 | 31 Oct 2027 | Trajectory to 19 MSEK (core) or 24 MSEK (stretch) of 2028 departures by 31 Dec; margin at least 19%; proven iROAS | 2028 budget release (4.6 MSEK; stretch tranche up to 1.0); GL1-GL4 go/no-go; 2028 loss budget; marketing FTE decision | Programme owner |
| 4 | 31 Mar 2028 | 2028 order book 32 MSEK (core) or 40 (stretch) | Scale winners | CFO |
| 5 | 30 Jun 2028 | 2028 order book 48 MSEK (core) or 60 (stretch); EU Directive 2026/1024 re-engineering plan [VERIFY] | Scale winners; 2029 outlook | Programme owner |

Gates 4 and 5 carry only order-book thresholds in the strategy. This document proposes scoring the standing tests on them as well (margin at least 19%, G8 service, cumulative net contribution against the 2028 loss budget); CEO to confirm at Gate 3 [ASSUMPTION].

Gate mechanics:
- **T-8 weeks:** Analytics lead and CFO publish a gate forecast (probability the gate passes, driver by driver); amber or red starts a recovery plan owned by the Programme owner. Not applicable to Gate 0 (8 weeks before is 20 Oct, before this programme existed).
- **T-10 working days:** pack outline agreed; data extracts frozen at T-5.
- **T-5 working days:** pack issued to CEO and council. The independent assurance read covers the latest closed month for Gate 1 and the closed quarter for later gates; there is no external read before Gate 0 (CFO self-certifies the baseline pack; Analytics lead cross-checks).
- **Gate 0 exception:** draft pack 2 Dec (workstream 34), issued 10 Dec (T-3 working days), because the baseline data (margin, 40 MSEK definition, order book) arrives late; the CEO may postpone the decision, not the date, if the data is not in.
- **Gate day:** 60-90 minutes; decision record (template D) signed by CEO; minutes within 24 hours.
- **Owners do not score their own gate conditions:** the Analytics lead and CFO do (workstream 29).
- **Outcomes:** Pass; Pass with conditions (owner, date, re-check); Hold (named money or hire stays gated; re-check date at most 6 weeks); Recut (target reset, bridge re-run, board informed).

### 8.3 Contract standard (every agency, every freelancer)

1. **Ownership:** the company owns Google Ads, Meta Business Manager, Tag Manager, GA4, Search Console, CRM, pixels, creative files and audience lists; partner has manager or editor access only; two company admins.

8. **Confidentiality and data:** no customer data to the partner except through agreed, consented flows; data processing agreement signed.
