## 1. Why this workstream exists

1. **The reputation gap is the most visible competitive weakness in the public data.** Trustpilot for the client: 8 reviews at 2.7/5, no recent invitation history, two recent negative reviews about service responsiveness and a tour guide (https://se.trustpilot.com/review/sydafrikaresor.se, R03). Competitors: Safariresor.se about 623 reviews at 4.8 (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/safariresor.se), MyChoice Sydafrika 5.0 on 236 on Reco (https://www.reco.se/mychoice-sydafrika) and 4.7 on 32 on Trustpilot, Afrikaresor.se 4.9 on 124 (https://se.trustpilot.com/review/afrikaresor.se).
2. **The client already has a strong asset that is under-used:** positive Google reviews shown through a Trustindex widget that name individual advisers, and customer travel stories on the site (R03, R08 section 4.3). We do not need to invent proof; we need to collect it systematically, put it where buyers decide, and answer criticism in public.
3. **Trust is the conversion constraint for a SEK 35-60k per person purchase** (strategy A1: SEK 45k planning value, 35-60k plausible; R09 point 5). A buyer who shortlists operators on reviews (R02, journey stage 3) is likely to see 8 reviews at 2.7 next to 124-623 reviews at 4.8-4.9. Paid traffic (Tranche A and B) would leak to those competitors, which is why the strategy puts the review programme into the first 30 days (strategy section 3.4 no-regret list, section 15 prerequisites, section 16.1 timeline).
4. **Evidence caution.** That review volume and score drive conversion is an assumption (R09 A5, no company data). This plan therefore includes a measurement design (section 9.3) that tests the effect before more money is attached to it, and claims no bookings of its own: the effect is credited inside L2 (close-rate lift, +3.5 MSEK) and L7 (repeat and referral, +3.0 MSEK), not added on top.

## 3. Principles (non-negotiable)

1. **Invite every traveller, not only the happy ones.** Selective invitation ("review gating": asking only satisfied customers) is generally against Trustpilot's guidelines and is a misleading-practice risk under EU and Swedish consumer rules [VERIFY with Trustpilot terms and Legal]. R11 and strategy P1 ("gated by satisfaction") mention a 4+ star survey gate; strategy P1 should be amended accordingly (question Q2). This plan overrides that for the public review request: a private rating question may be asked, but the public review request goes to everyone regardless of that rating; a detractor also triggers a service-recovery call (section 5.4) but is never removed from the invitation list.
2. **No payment, discount or prize in exchange for a review.** Trustpilot prohibits incentivised reviews [VERIFY current terms]; Swedish and EU rules require disclosure of any incentive or fake-review control [VERIFY]. Thank-you gestures are allowed only if given to all who respond to a feedback survey regardless of score and never tied to a public review.
3. **Real people, real trips.** No staff reviews, no friends-and-family, no purchased reviews, no AI-written reviews. Staff may not post on the client's own profiles; this is written into the staff policy (section 8).
4. **Answer in public, fix in private.** Replies are short, personal, factual, with a named owner; detail moves to phone or email.
5. **Licence before you use.** A review text or photo is used in ads, site or social only with explicit consent, or under the platform's embed terms (widgets).
6. **The promise must be true.** Copy uses "jourtelefon under resan" until 24/7 local presence is costed and staffed (strategy positioning). Reviews quoted that praise things we have not committed to are not used as promises.

## 5. The review engine (process)

### 5.1 Trigger and timing
- Source of truth: booking record stage "travelled" (R11 stage list: ... travelled, review/referral asked). Trigger = return date + 3 days for the thank-you, +10 days for the review request (R11 recommends day 10-14 for review/referral), reminder at +17 days, final reminder at +30 days for non-responders only. Maximum three review asks (+10, +17, +30); the +3 thank-you and +14 referral message are separate and carry no review request.
- Group bookings (average 2.2 travellers per booking, strategy A1/A9): invite the booker plus each adult traveller with a separate email where a lawful contact route exists; one review per person. Response rates in section 2 are per booking, so per-person invitations are upside, not counted.
- Channel: email first, SMS for those with SMS consent. WhatsApp only if already used in the service relationship.
- Timing guard: no request while an open complaint exists; the claim is resolved first, then the request is sent (the person is still invited, only delayed, max 30 days).

### 5.2 Mid-trip pulse (service recovery before the review)
- Day 3 of the trip: a two-question message (SMS or WhatsApp) "Hur går det? 1-5" plus "Behöver du hjälp?" with the jourtelefon number. Score 1-3 triggers an adviser call within 4 working hours. Purpose: fix problems while the traveller can still be helped, which is how rebooking-help reviews (floods, cancelled safari) were earned by MyChoice (R03 line 72).
- Dependency: staffed jourtelefon; do not launch if no one can answer (G8).

### 5.3 Post-trip flow (all travellers)
1. +3 days: "Välkommen hem" email from the named adviser, with a one-question rating (1-5) and free text, and photo-sharing link (UGC consent, section 7).
2. +10 days: public review request from the adviser's name with one primary link (Trustpilot or Google, by the section 2 split), plus the adviser's note on what to mention (guide name, lodge, moment) without scripting content or star rating.
3. +14 days: referral and repeat ask (CRM workstream; share of L7), a separate message to travellers whose private rating was 4-5, so it is not conflated with the review request. Travellers who rated 1-3 are handled by the detractor routine first.
4. +17 and +30 days: reminders to review non-responders only. The +17 reminder mentions both Trustpilot and Google (and Reco if live).
5. +90 days: story invitation, selected from the private +3-day rating (4-5) and opt-in, never from public review status and without mention of reviews ("Vill du dela din resa?"), see section 7.

### 5.4 Detractor handling
- Pulse or survey score 1-3: adviser or Head of Sales calls within 1 working day; service-recovery gesture decided by Head of Sales within a budget cap [ASSUMPTION: SEK 1,000-3,000 goodwill per case, funded from service budget, not marketing; confirm with CFO].
- The goodwill gesture is decided on the merits of the complaint, communicated as unconditional, and never linked to a review (before or after). Customer remains in the review flow. Never ask to remove or edit a review in exchange for compensation (platform rules, [VERIFY]); only ask "if our solution resolved your concern, you are welcome to update your review".
- Log in CRM: reason code (flight, guide, lodge, response time, price, other); monthly top-3 reasons to Head of Product and Head of Sales; this feeds G8 and complaints-per-1,000 metrics.

### 5.5 Public replies (SLA and templates)
- Positive review: reply within 5 working days, name the adviser, one specific detail. Not a copy-paste.
- 3 stars or below: reply within 2 working days, acknowledge, no defensive language, offer direct contact, no personal data (booking numbers, medical data) in public. Owner: CRM manager drafts, Head of Sales approves; legal escalation if the review alleges illegal conduct.
- Fake or abusive reviews: flag via platform with evidence; never retaliate. Log the case.
- Swedish templates in section 11.

### 5.6 Backlog campaign (one-time, Nov 2026 - Jan 2027)
- Audience: customers who travelled Jan 2025 - Oct 2026 with a valid contact route.
- Legal basis: see section 8 (existing-customer exception for marketing email; a service-feedback request is not necessarily marketing, but this is treated conservatively) [VERIFY with Legal/DPO].
- Staged send: week 1 pilot to 50 recent travellers (last 6 months) to test deliverability and response; then batches of 100 per week (the first two batches limited to clearly lawful, recent customers; later batches only after Legal answers, due 15 Dec 2026) so the profile does not see an unnatural spike and so advisers can answer replies; stop if the spike triggers a Trustpilot review-integrity flag [VERIFY].
- Personal touch: the first 100 invitations are signed by the adviser who sold the trip.

### 7.2 Capture mechanics
- Consent form (digital, section 11) with three levels: (a) internal use only, (b) site, social and email, (c) paid ads. Default is none; each level is a separate tick-box; withdrawal by email at any time; data stored in CRM with date, level and version of the text.
- Licence duration: 24 months, renewable; a withdrawal removes organic content within 14 days and paid creative within 5 working days [ASSUMPTION; Legal to confirm].
- Photos with identifiable third parties (guides, other travellers, children): no use unless each visible person (or guardian) consents. Children: never in ads without guardian written consent [VERIFY with Legal/DPO].
- Reward: not tied to reviews. For licensed stories, a SEK 500 voucher on a future booking or a printed photo book up to SEK 500 is offered after publication, as a thank-you for a story/photos, never for reviews [ASSUMPTION; Legal to confirm that the structure keeps stories and reviews separate]. For ambassadors (micro-creators, R08 tier C), up to SEK 1,500 each; paid collaborations are labelled "Annons/Reklam i samarbete med Sydafrikaresor" (R08 section 8).
- Quality: stories are edited for clarity, never for sentiment. Claims that are not true to the product (e.g. "alltid 24/7 hjälp") are removed or corrected with the traveller's approval.
- Archive: all published UGC with consent record, date and where it ran (Konsumentverket marketing guidance, R08 section 8).

- Moderated Facebook group "Sydafrikaresor - resecommunity" for past and future travellers (R08 section 7), launched Q1 2027 [dependency: community manager]. ### 9.2 Attribution rules
- No bookings are credited to this workstream in the bridge. Effect is observed through close-rate (L2) and repeat/referral (L7) and tested below. Marginal marketing cost cap (G4, SEK 20k per marketing-created booking) is not applied because no bookings are claimed as created.
- Add "Hur hörde du om oss?" with options "Omdömen/rekommendation" and "Vän/familj" to the enquiry form and the adviser's call script to measure self-reported influence.

### 9.3 Test design (before widening spend on proof placement)
- Statistical caution: at about 2,400 enquiries a year (strategy section 2) and 1.5-2.5% visitor-to-enquiry, a 100-enquiries-per-arm test can only detect very large effects (a relative difference of roughly 30-40% or more, because the relative standard error is about 14%). A 5% lift cannot be proven by these tests. They therefore serve as guardrails (is the block harmful?) and directional evidence, combined with self-reported influence ("Hur hörde du om oss?") and before/after trends. Run the sample-size calculation with GA4 traffic by 31 Jan 2027 and extend the test duration or pool pages if needed [ASSUMPTION].
- Test 1 (Feb-Mar 2027): on four high-traffic itinerary pages, split traffic 50/50 between versions with and without the review block (live rating + 3 reviews). Metric: enquiry rate and quote request rate; minimum 4 weeks and 100 enquiries per arm [ASSUMPTION; actual traffic unknown]. Decision: keep the block unless it is clearly negative on enquiry rate or lead quality (trust and ads-risk reasons justify it regardless); size any claimed lift only if the confidence interval supports it.
- Test 2 (Apr-Jun 2027): quote email with the "Kunder som rest samma resa" page versus a 20% holdout without it. Metric: quote-to-booking at 30 days. Volumes (about 600 quotes in the quarter [ASSUMPTION]) allow direction only.
- Test 3 (Q3 2027, only if Trustpilot is 4.3+ with 50+ reviews): ads quoting the exact current Trustpilot rating versus the same ads without it, at the same budget, via Google Ads experiment (workstream 06). Metric: conversion rate, cost per qualified lead.
- Results go to the growth council monthly. If tests show no lift, reduce proof placement scope and shift budget to service quality.
