## 1. Why this workstream exists and what it must deliver

Strategy lever L7 is worth **+0.8 MSEK in 2027 and +3.0 MSEK in 2028** (about 8 and about 30 incremental bookings), counted as incremental to simply holding a 15% repeat-and-referral share on a grown base. The total repeat-plus-referral book is about 12.8 MSEK, about 125 bookings, in 2028 (about 20% of 637 bookings), against about 15% today [A12, unverified; there is no CRM measurement behind it]. The strategy withdrew v1's 25% share (about 184 bookings) because the pool of past customers is small and same-destination repeat is structurally low (R11 section 6).

Three things make this lever worth doing even though it is small:

1. It is probably the cheapest booking the business can buy [ASSUMPTION until measured]. Referral leads close at 45-60% quote-to-booking in the Rework figure cited in R11 (Tier C, https://resources.rework.com/libraries/travel-tour-growth/booking-conversion-metrics, not verified; the ivyforms page https://ivyforms.com/blog/lead-generation-for-travel-agents/ only gives a 9.5% lead-level figure) against 18% for new cold traffic in the strategy. Even haircut to 40% here, a referral booking costs about SEK 2,000 in rewards against the strategy's marginal marketing cap of SEK 20k per marketing-created booking (strategy G4/A6).
2. It depends less on ad auctions and on search demand, but it is not shock-proof: referrals and repeat bookings fall when the whole category is hit (strategy shock case about 38 MSEK in 2027, R4). Its advantage is that reward cost is variable and paid only after departure.
3. It feeds the other levers: reviews (L-reputation, WS12), hosted departure fill (L5), and the 2028 order book (strategy checkpoint: 19 MSEK of 2028 departures by 31 Dec 2027).

What this workstream is not: a points scheme, a discount club or a paid-ad programme. Strategy positioning says no lowest-price play and 15-20% premium shown through inclusions, guide, flexibility and support. Rewards are therefore value-adds and travel credits released after departure, never price cuts, and sit inside the promotion cap (2% of turnover, strategy G2).

## 3. Objectives, definitions and KPIs

### 3.1 Definitions (frozen by 15 Dec 2026, owned by CRM manager, signed off by CFO)

- **Household:** one record per household (WS13 4.1), merged by email, phone, address.
- **Repeat booking:** a booking by a household with at least one previously completed (travelled) booking, any destination the company sells. Reported separately as (a) same destination, (b) other southern Africa, (c) other long-haul (Pacific, Australia, NZ).
- **Referral booking:** a booking whose lead carries a valid referral code or a named referrer who is a past or current customer, confirmed in the "how did you hear" field and by the adviser. If a lead is both repeat and referred, count once, as repeat.
- **Repeat-and-referral share:** (repeat + referral bookings) / all bookings, measured on bookings made in the period (order-book basis), not on departures.
- **Incremental (L7):** bookings above the 15% share of the same period's total bookings. This is the only number added to the bridge.
- **Dedupe rule:** a referral that arrives via a partner (L8) or a paid click is attributed to referral only if the referral code or named referrer was captured before the booking; otherwise it stays with the channel. This prevents double counting with L3, L4, L8 (the strategy applies a 15% overlap haircut already).

## 4. Strategy of the programme (approach in one page)

1. **Earn the right first.** Referral is a by-product of a good trip and a good ending. Sequence: service recovery, then review ask (WS12, day 10), then referral ask (day 14, separate message, WS13 F10), then story ask (day 45), then next-trip conversation (day 90, 10 months). Never combine review and referral asks in one message (WS13, WS12 rule).
2. **Status, not discounts.** The "club" gives access, priority and personal service: first access to new and hosted departures, a named adviser who already knows the household, an annual "Sydafrikaträff", and a small value-add on the next booking. This respects the price positioning and costs near zero in cash.
3. **Cross-sell ladder rather than same-destination repeat.** Most second trips are not "South Africa again". Design three explicit next-trip paths (section 6.3).
4. **Groups of friends and families are the multiplier.** Past customers who organise a friend group or a family trip produce several travellers per lead and fit the group and MICE unit's capabilities (section 6.4).
5. **Keep it legal and non-creepy.** No marketing to friends' addresses supplied by customers; no incentive on review ratings; no reward before departure; disclosures on every benefit (section 11).
6. **Small and measured.** All-in budget about 0.26 MSEK in 2027 and about 0.40 MSEK in 2028 (section 12), spend gates tied to measured referral and repeat bookings, kill switch at the L7 checkpoint of 30 Jun 2027 (reported into strategy Gate 2).

## 5. The alumni model: "Sydafrikavänner" [ASSUMPTION: name to be trade-mark and domain checked]

Membership is automatic after a completed trip (travelled booking), no fee, no points, opt-in to marketing separately. Tiers are by relationship, not spend, to avoid cost and complexity:

| Tier | Who | Benefits (all value-add, no price cut) | Cost control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vän | Completed one trip | Welcome-home card and photo prompt; first access to new departures and hosted trips 7 days before public release (only where supply allows); invitation to alumni evenings; Facebook group access; referral link and credit | Access is free; credit paid only after friend's departure |
| Återvändare | Completed two or more trips, or one trip plus a referral that travelled | Priority adviser line (same adviser if possible); up to SEK 1,500 value-add at cost on the next booking (for example upgraded transfer, one extra night in a lodge, a private game drive; cost to company, not retail value); invitation to the annual advisory evening | Cap 1,500 at cost per booking; needs margin check by CFO; counts in 2% promo cap |
| Ambassadör | Invited: NPS 9-10, wrote or filmed a story with a signed licence (WS12), willing to answer prospect questions | Meet-the-ambassador call listing on the website with consent; contributes to Q&A; first access to a hosted departure as guest-of-honour discount is not offered; instead a travel credit SEK 2,000 per signed-off story or per 3 referral bookings (whichever first, cap SEK 6,000 a year) | Annual cap and written terms; disclosure on every public post: "Ambassadör, har rest med oss" |

Design choices made deliberately:
- No points, no tier miles. Frequency is too low (one long-haul trip per household every few years; the Promodo rebooking interval of 312 days is for a tours marketplace and is not transferable). Points schemes also create a liability and accounting overhead on a 20%-margin product.
- No cash rewards. Travel credits only, valid 24 months [ASSUMPTION], non-transferable except to a travel companion on the same booking, stated clearly in terms to avoid Konsumentverket and tax questions [VERIFY with Legal and CFO: tax treatment of credits to private persons, VAT on credit redemption, accounting as marketing cost or liability].
- No claim of exclusivity that is not true. "Först att få veta" is only used where the 7-day early window is real and scheduled.
- The 24/7 local-presence promise is not marketed; alumni copy uses "jourtelefon under resan" only (strategy positioning).

### 6.2 Referral engine (live 1 Feb 2027)

The flows themselves (F8 +14 days, F10 referral reminder at +30 days and for 4-5 star reviewers) are specified and owned in WS13 section 7.2 and 18.8; this workstream owns the rules, reward economics, landing page, tracking, and reporting. Components:

1. **Landing page** `sydafrikaresor.se/tipsa-en-van` [ASSUMPTION: URL] explaining the benefit, terms and a form for the friend (self-submitted) plus "ring mig" option. Friend arrives with the referrer's name shown only if the referrer agreed in the mail flow.
2. **Dual benefit** (ranges from WS13 F10, WS13 assumption W13-9; confirm by CFO):
   - Friend: a pre-trip planning call with a named adviser and one small extra on booking (for example upgraded airport transfer), cost about SEK 500 at cost [ASSUMPTION].
   - Referrer: travel credit SEK 1,500 [ASSUMPTION; test 1,000 vs 2,000] credited only after the friend's trip has departed, usable on the referrer's next booking within 24 months.
   - Cost per referral booking about SEK 2,000 all-in against gross profit of about SEK 20k per booking (10% of gross profit), inside the O12 limit.
3. **Qualifying rules:** friend is a new household; booking value at least SEK 20,000 per person [ASSUMPTION to avoid reward on trivial add-on bookings]; credit stacking limit of three referral credits per year per household.
4. **Likely moments of high willingness** [ASSUMPTION, to be tested with the data] (apart from day 14): after a 4-5 star review (WS12), after a story is published, after an alumni evening, at the first anniversary of return, and when a customer mentions friends in a call (adviser prompt logged in CRM as "Friend interested").
5. **Adviser rule:** every referral lead gets a call within 1 hour in office hours (same SLA as the strategy), with the referrer's name and trip context visible. Referral leads are a priority queue ahead of cold leads (the strategy SLA is first reply under 1 hour in business hours for 90%). If SLA is missed on referral leads two weeks in a row, referral promotion pauses (strategy capacity gate 11.2).
6. **Thank-you loop:** the referrer receives an adviser message when the friend has booked ("Tack, din vän har bokat") and when the credit is released after departure. No silent rewards.

Do not do (and why): do not ask customers to upload their friends' email addresses for us to contact (no lawful basis to market to the friend [VERIFY], damages trust); do not offer anything tied to a review's rating or existence in the same message (platform and consumer-law risk, see section 11); do not run a "win a trip" referral lottery in 2027 (cost, tax and legal complexity, and strategy forbids discount-led tactics).

### 6.3 Repeat: the cross-sell ladder

Strategy and R11 agree that same-destination repeat is structurally low, so the ladder is built around the household's first trip. Product names are [ASSUMPTION] and must be reconciled with WS02 (product portfolio) and supply (allocation calls from the week of 2 Nov 2026).

| Path | Who | Next-trip idea | Timing of outreach | Supply check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-A Deepen South Africa | First trip was Cape Town + Garden Route or a classic | A different region (Kruger and Panorama, KwaZulu-Natal, Eastern Cape, Wild Coast), or a wine-and-food or golf week | +90 days preference question; +10 months anniversary; 12-18 months departure window | DMC and guide capacity (strategy: unverified) |
| P-B Widen Africa | First trip was SA safari | Botswana, Namibia, Zimbabwe/Victoria Falls, Mozambique or Mauritius extension (R02, R05: combos exist) | +12 months, plus when a new combined offer is published | Contracted supply >= 60% of planned volume (strategy L9 gate) |
| P-C Other long-haul | Any first trip; especially younger couples and families | Australia, New Zealand, Pacific (the company's other core regions) | +18-24 months or after a big life event (milestone birthday, anniversary) | Cross-sell consent; shared product team |

Mechanics:
- Preference capture is the point of the +90-day mail: one question "Vart vill du resa härnäst?" with 5 click answers; stored as tags; adviser sees the tag.
- Anniversary flow at +10 months and +300 days (WS13 F8/F9) invites to a no-pressure planning chat; 10% holdout.
- Life-event triggers captured by advisers (milestone year, retirement, children's ages) as optional fields; used only for the household's own account (no purchased data).
- Advisers own personal outreach for the top 100 households by value and fit each half-year: a short personal call or note, logged in CRM. At 3 minutes per household the cost is small [ASSUMPTION].

### 6.4 "Ta med vännerna": friends-and-family group trips

Past customers are the best organisers of 4-8 adult groups (friends, golf club, extended family, multigenerational trips). It converts one lead into several travellers and uses the groups/MICE unit's pricing and handling skills.

- Offer: when a past customer brings a group of at least 6 adults [ASSUMPTION], the group gets a joint planning session and a single point of contact. The organiser ("gruppvärd") receives the Återvändare value-add (at cost, capped) and a credit of SEK 1,000 per booked household that travels (cap SEK 6,000).
- Pricing: group price discount is not offered by default (positioning); value-add is offered through included extras; any group price must pass the margin floor from the CFO.
- Capacity: the groups unit prices and the product team confirms allocation; no promise before supply is held.
- Target: 8 group bookings in 2027 and 18 in 2028 [ASSUMPTION], included in repeat and referral totals (about 30% of 2028 repeat bookings).

### 6.5 Alumni community and events

Division of work with sister workstreams:
- WS10 owns the Facebook group "Sydafrikaresor - resecommunity" (name to be checked), moderation, calendar and daily posts. WS21 supplies alumni members, a monthly alumni topic, ambassador voices, and the "photo of the month" format.
- WS20 owns event production (kundkvällar, alumni nights inside each round, "bring a friend"). WS21 supplies the guest list segmentation, invitations, consent, post-event follow-up and measures the alumni attendance and referral outcomes. Event costs sit in WS20/WS14 envelopes, not here.
- WS12 owns review requests and story licences; WS21 picks stories for ambassadors.

New elements owned here:
1. **Welcome-home package (+3 days).** A printed postcard with a trip photo or a thank-you from the adviser and guide, plus the digital card (cost in margin, about SEK 70 per booking [ASSUMPTION]).
2. **Sydafrikaträffen (annual alumni evening).** Once a year, one city (Stockholm 2027; add Göteborg or Malmö in 2028 if cost per qualified lead is under SEK 500 per WS20). Format: photo night, guide or ambassador on stage, wine tasting, a "bring a friend" seat. Organised through WS20 as one of the alumni evenings.
3. **Hosted-departure priority.** Alumni get a 7-day early window on the 4-6 hosted departures (WS10/WS17); the hosted departures also become the gathering where alumni meet again and are the natural venue for P-A and group offers.
4. **Customer advisory board.** 8 volunteer travellers meet three times a year (digital) for product, pricing and message input; modest thank-you (SEK 15k per year including gifts) [ASSUMPTION]. Gives early warning on service issues.
5. **Photo and story programme.** At +45 days "Dina bilder" request with licence option; best stories on site and social (WS12, WS10). Photo of the month with a small prize (SEK 500 value) [ASSUMPTION], sponsored by a partner supplier if possible (WS18).
6. **Quarterly alumni newsletter** (WS13), 1 in 4 mails commercial: new departures, cross-sell paths; 3 in 4 content: stories, tips, "så reste vi".

## 11. Legal, trust and compliance guardrails (all [VERIFY] with counsel before launch)

- Marketing consent: use the existing-customer exception only as confirmed by Legal (R09 section 5); otherwise re-permission. Keep consent records (date, wording, source).

- Referral rewards and ambassador benefits count as commercial communication: clear disclosure ("Ambassadör, har rest med oss och får förmån") and archive all posts (R08 operational rules: label, claim approval, archive).
- Reviews: no incentive linked to a review or its score, no review gating; referral and review asks stay separate in time and content; check Trustpilot, Google and Reco platform rules on incentives [VERIFY].
- Consumer law: rewards must not change the price information shown to the friend; the price including mandatory costs applies (R09 section 5.1). Terms in plain Swedish.
- Package travel and guarantee: do not describe credits or benefits as guaranteed travel; mention the travel guarantee (resegaranti) only after Finance confirms current status (R09, WS13).
- Credits: expiry, refundability, cancellation treatment, bankruptcy protection and tax treatment [VERIFY with CFO and Legal].
- Data: alumni profiles, children's ages and life events are personal data; minimise; data retention schedule; access control; privacy policy at /villkor/personuppgiftspolicy/ updated.
