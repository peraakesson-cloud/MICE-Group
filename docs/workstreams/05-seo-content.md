## 1. Purpose, objectives and where this sits in the bridge

**Role in the strategy.** This workstream owns the content half of lever **L4 (SEO, content and AI-search)**: +0.2 MSEK in 2027 and +1.5 MSEK by year 2 (2028) in the core bridge, evidence grade B, payout mostly on late-2028 and 2029 departures because of a 4-9 month ranking lag (00-strategy). It also supports L2 (conversion, through objection-removing content), L7 (referral, through customer-story content), L9 (new products, through launch pages) and L10 (season spreading, through month and offer pages). It does not own technical SEO (WS04), paid, social or CRM.

**What the site already has (R06 section 6, public pages only):** #1 for several head and package queries; about 100 programme pages, about 40 customer stories (latest Sep 2026), about 13 excursion pages added Aug-Sep 2026, a "reseinspiration" hub (Sep 2026) and only about 6 "tips" articles. **What it lacks:** best-time hub, cost guide, safety and practical hub, safari pillar, itinerary pages by length, month pages, segment pages with depth, comparison pages (R06 section 7). Competitors rank for all of these. The content plan is therefore mostly gap-filling plus depth, not a rewrite.

**Sanity check against the bridge.** L4 = +1.5 MSEK of turnover by 2028. At the strategy's SEK 101k per booking (45,000 x 2.2 travellers) that is about 15 incremental bookings, or about 104 incremental organic enquiries a year at the strategy's 80% quote rate and 18% new-traffic close rate (15 / (0.8 x 0.18); WS04 gets 105-115 on a 15-17 booking basis). The 2027 figure (+0.2 MSEK) is about 2 bookings, roughly 14 enquiries. This is a modest volume target, which is why the plan weights quality, conversion and margin over page count. It also means the in-window cost per directly attributed organic booking is high (content spend of SEK 610k in 2028 over about 15 bookings is about SEK 40k) versus the SEK 20k marginal marketing cap in guardrail G4 (in 2027 the ratio is far worse: SEK 455k of content cash for about 2 bookings). L4 is one of the levers inside the strategy's G4 calculation (2.0 MSEK incremental over 109 bookings from L3-L5 and L8), so this plan cannot claim to sit outside the cap: it passes only on the pooled figure, and L4 on its own is above the cap in both years. We accept this only because (a) the pooled L3-L5 + L8 test is what G4 measures, so paid, social and partner lines must come in under SEK 20k to carry SEO, (b) evergreen pages keep paying into 2029 and beyond, (c) content raises close rate and cuts paid cost for the same flow (assist value, not counted in L4), and (d) a hard review at Gate 2 and Gate 3 cuts spend to maintenance if organic enquiries are off track (section 12, risk C1). **The CEO should treat this as a long-payback investment, and say so to the board.**

## 2. Approach and principles

1. **Commercial intent first, traffic second.** Every piece is mapped to one cluster, one primary query, one primary funnel stage and one conversion path. A page with no path to a quote request, a "boka ett samtal" or a newsletter sign-up does not get commissioned.
2. **Own the cluster, not the keyword.** Pillar pages (Tier A) own the broad query and link to spokes (Tiers B and C) that own long-tail variants. One primary URL per intent; the keyword-to-URL map lives with the SEO lead (WS04 4.1) and blocks cannibalisation before a brief is written.
3. **Experience is the moat (E-E-A-T).** Positioning says "Sydafrikaspecialisten sedan 2003, named personal adviser" (00-strategy). Every Tier A and B piece carries a named adviser byline or "granskad av" line, first-hand detail (own trips, own guides, customer stories) and original photography. Generic text that a model could write from the open web will not rank and will not be cited. This is the main difference from the competitors that rank on the same queries (R06 section 5).
4. **Honest content wins the DIY searcher.** The competitor field for "på egen hand" and cost queries is dominated by DIY blogs and price sites (R06 clusters 6 and 10). We publish honest comparisons and real price ranges and then show why a specialist is worth the premium (inclusions, guide, flexibility, support, travel guarantee). Positioning is premium, about 15-20% above the group-with-flights cluster, so content must explain value, never price-shout.
5. **Answer first, then depth.** Each page opens with a 40-80 word direct answer, a "Snabbfakta" box and a table where relevant, followed by depth. This serves featured snippets and AI answer engines and also readers on mobile.
6. **Price and claim integrity.** Prices in content are shown as "från" ranges taken from one price source (WS04 4.5), dated, and with a clear statement of what is included. No page may claim 24/7 local presence: until costed and staffed the copy says "jourtelefon under resan" (00-strategy positioning). The travel guarantee wording is checked by Legal/Ops (R09). Price ranges are labelled indicative, show the date and what is included, and state that prices can change with the exchange rate (the ZAR price-adjustment clause is in the terms by 31 Mar 2027, 00-strategy section 15); no fixed-price promise is made in content. Response-time claims ("svar inom en timme") appear only once the SLA is met (guardrail G8). Safety copy never says "Sydafrika är säkert"; it states facts and links to UD and the Embassy. Safety, visa, health and insurance text links to official sources and is dated.
7. **Margin-aware internal linking.** Guide pages link to bookable products ordered by order-book margin and available supply, not alphabetically (WS04 4.2). We never push a product that supply cannot deliver (supply-readiness gate, 00-strategy L9).
8. **Refresh beats new.** Rankings decay and freshness matters for month, price and "2027/2028" queries. A fixed refresh budget (section 10) is protected before new production is added.
9. **Respect the lag.** Content published in Nov 2026 - Mar 2027 mostly feeds 2028 departures and the 2028 order book (target 19 MSEK of 2028 departures by 31 Dec 2027). It will not move 2027 turnover much. Do not judge content on 2027 revenue.
10. **Sequence behind the technical base.** New hubs go live only on templates that pass WS04 checks (schema, internal-link blocks, CWV). Release freeze 15 Dec 2026 - 15 Mar 2027 applies to templates and URLs, not to publishing content on approved templates (to be confirmed with the web developer; open item D2).

## 5. Tactics (what we actually do)

### 5.1 Core production (the calendar in section 9)
Tier A/B/C production as above, in the order of the ranked gaps: best-time, cost, safari, safety (Q4 2026), then itineraries, Kapstaden, family, Kruger, wine, honeymoon, holiday seasons, combinations (2027), then depth and long-tail in 2028.

### 5.2 Commercial landing support
Where a segment already has a live product page (family, golf, luxury, honeymoon, business), the content hub sits above it and passes authority down. We enrich the existing URL in place (WS04 rule) rather than create a competing page.

### 5.3 Original-data assets for links and AI citations
- **"Sydafrikaindex"** (annual): what trips cost by length and style, based on the company's own quotes and bookings, anonymised and aggregated. Needs the data pack (R10 request) and Legal/Ops sign-off, and must not publish margin or unit cost. Published Jan 2028 (first edition); the Oct 2028 piece in the calendar is a mid-year price update to feed the 2029 booking season, and from 2029 the index is refreshed each January. It is the asset most likely to attract press and links (a hypothesis, not proven). [ASSUMPTION-C6: company can release aggregated pricing data without competitive harm.]
- **Season calendar and "bästa tiden" interactive month picker** with crawlable links (WS04 4.2).
- **Customer-story hub**: re-edit the existing ~40 reseberättelser into topic-tagged quotes that feed destination pages (reuse beats new writing; also a referral asset for L7).
- **Adviser interviews and "Så gör jag"** pieces (first-hand expertise), each recorded as a short video and transcript.

### 5.4 AI-search and answer-engine content
- Direct-answer first, entities named consistently (Sydafrika, Kapstaden, Krugerparken, Garden Route, Sydafrikaspecialisten sedan 2003), and a stable "Om oss" and "Resegaranti" fact base.
- A monthly **citation audit**: run 30 fixed Swedish prompts ("bästa tiden att resa till Sydafrika", "vad kostar en safari i Sydafrika", "rundresa Sydafrika med svensktalande guide" etc.) in the main answer engines and Google AI features, log who is cited, and adjust pages. Owner: SEO lead. Technical access for AI crawlers is a WS04 decision (allow by default).
- No tactics that only game systems (mass AI-generated pages, scaled thin pages). Search quality guidelines on scaled low-value content are a real risk to a site that is #1 on its head terms (risk C2).

### 5.5 Link and mention earning (editorial, not paid links)
- Targets: South African Tourism Sweden contact, regional DMC and lodge partner pages that link to us, Swedish travel media, wine and golf communities, the 12-20 partner sites in WS12/R12, and review sites. Mechanism: Sydafrikaindex, hosted departures (L5), guide interviews, "gäst-guide" articles on partner sites.
- Rule: no purchased links, no link exchanges at scale. Budget in section 10 is for digital PR time, not links.
- Target: 25 new referring domains from relevant travel, tourism and Swedish media sources per year [ASSUMPTION-C9].

### 5.6 Repurposing (hand-off to social and email, workstreams 06 and CRM)
Each Tier A and B piece becomes: 1 short video cut (adviser to camera, 45-60 seconds), 3-5 social posts, 1 newsletter section, 1 FAQ set for the customer-service knowledge base. Social and email teams own distribution; SEO owns the source page and the UTM convention. Content gets a "repurposing sheet" at publication (one row per piece).

### 5.7 Conversion optimisation of content pages
Measured per template: scroll depth, click on product strip, quote-form starts, callbacks. Two A/B tests a quarter on the best-time hub and the cost guide (CTA wording, budget-bracket form position). Heat-mapping via existing Clarity tag (R06 section 6). Owner: SEO lead with Analytics lead.

### 5.8 Content that supports the other levers
- **L9 new products:** launch pages are produced only after the supply-readiness gate (60% volume contracted, age policy checked, guide and DMC confirmed). Placeholders in the calendar are marked "[gate]".
- **L10 season spreading:** Apr-May and Sep-Nov offers and the green season get dedicated pages from Feb 2027.
- **L6 revenue per traveller:** extension and excursion content with add-on CTAs.
- **GL1 Nordic scope:** no Norwegian or Danish content is produced before the scope decision (31 Mar 2027). If approved, a separate plan and budget apply (not in this plan's figures).
- **GL2 MICE cross-sell:** no content in this plan; the MICE sister site is handled by the MICE unit.

## 8. Workflow and quality control

1. **Commissioning (monthly, by day 20 of the previous month):** SEO lead produces briefs for next month's pieces: query, intent, outline, competitor gap, internal links, product strip, schema type, adviser, CTA.
2. **Draft (5-8 working days):** freelancer drafts; adviser provides a call or voice note with first-hand detail.
3. **Fact check (2 days):** adviser verifies every factual claim against an official source; Legal/Ops for price, guarantee, health, visa, insurance. Sources are noted in an internal brief footer.
4. **Edit and SEO QA (2 days):** editor checks voice and clarity; SEO lead checks title, H1-H3, direct answer, links, schema, images, alt text, canonical.
5. **Publish** on the approved template; request indexing; add to repurposing sheet.
6. **Review at +14, +60 and +180 days:** impressions, position, CTR, enquiries; decision: leave, improve, merge or prune.

Definition of done: reads well on a phone, passes WS04 template checks, has a named adviser, has a product strip and a CTA, and has an entry in the keyword-to-URL map.

## 17. Swedish sample snippets for the first three hubs

All three are drafts for the adviser and Legal/Ops to approve. Prices are placeholders to be replaced by the price owner.

**Hub 1: Bästa tiden att resa till Sydafrika**
- Title tag (max about 60 tecken): Bästa tiden att resa till Sydafrika, månad för månad
- Meta description: Vilken månad passar din resa? Se väder, safari, valar och vin per region, med råd från Sydafrikaspecialister sedan 2003.
- Direct answer: Den bästa tiden beror på vad du vill göra. För safari är torrsäsongen från maj till september ofta lättast, medan Kapstaden och Garden Route brukar vara som mest sommarlika från november till mars. Nedan går vi igenom varje region och månad, så att du kan välja rätt tid för just din resa.

**Hub 2: Vad kostar en resa till Sydafrika?**
- Title tag: Vad kostar en resa till Sydafrika? Prisguide med exempel
- Meta description: Så mycket kan en rundresa, safari eller skräddarsydd resa till Sydafrika kosta, vad som ingår och hur du väljer rätt budget.
- Direct answer: Priset beror på resans längd, boendenivå och om du reser med guide. Exempel från oss: [FRÅN-PRISER OCH PRISBASIS (per person, flyg ingår/ingår ej, datum), ÄGARE: PRODUKTCHEF]. Priserna är vägledande och kan ändras med växelkurs och säsong. Nedan ser du vad som ingår.

**Hub 3: Safari i Sydafrika**
- Title tag: Safari i Sydafrika: Kruger, privata reservat, malariafritt
- Meta description: Vilken safari passar dig? Jämför Krugerparken, privata reservat och malariafria alternativ och få hjälp av en personlig rådgivare.
- Direct answer: Du kan göra safari i Sydafrika i nationalparker som Krugerparken, i privata reservat och i malariafria områden. Vad som passar bäst beror på budget, resans längd, om du reser med barn och hur nära djuren du vill komma. Nedan jämför vi alternativen.

Note: title tags are written without the brand suffix to stay under about 60 characters; the CMS appends "| Sydafrikaresor" only where space allows (WS04 section 7.1 formulas govern; the cost-guide title there, "Prisexempel", should be aligned with the hub title before publication).

Call-to-action phrases (internal style): "Prata med en Sydafrikaspecialist", "Få ett förslag på din resa", "Se bokningsbara resor". Avoid "24/7 på plats" (not marketed until costed and staffed); use "jourtelefon under resan".
