## 1. Summary

1. The creator programme is a **small, commission-first trust-and-reach layer**, not a big-spend channel. The strategy line "Social, creators, hosted departures, PR" is only SEK 0.35 M in 2027 (0.15 committed, 0.20 gated) and SEK 0.45 M in 2028 (0.20 / 0.25). Other workstreams already draw on it (section 9). The programme must therefore be frugal by design.
2. The core buyer is 40-70 and sits on **Facebook (77-83% of those born 1960-1989), YouTube (73-83%) and Instagram (67-76%)**; TikTok reaches only 5-12% of the 1950s-1970s cohorts (Internetstiftelsen, https://svenskarnaochinternet.se/utvalt/sociala-medier/ via R08). So the programme is built around trusted niche authorities, hosted-departure personalities and real customers on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. TikTok creators are a capped test only.
3. Five tiers: **A** hosted-departure personalities, **B** niche authorities (golf, wine and food, photography, wildlife, hiking), **C** customer ambassadors, **D** honeymoon and family creators, **E** our own guides and advisers as faces (employee advocacy, zero cash).
4. **Pay for results where possible**: low fixed fee, plus a capped commission on bookings that are fully paid and not cancelled. Free seats are priced at marginal cost and counted as marketing cost. The commission cap is set by gate G5 (margin after commission at least 15%), which means **at most about 5% of booked value** on 20%-margin products. This lowers the 5-8% range in workstream 08 (flagged in section 10).
5. **Compliance is a hard gate**: the company has the main responsibility for what creators post for it, cannot contract that away, and serious breaches can cost up to 4% of annual turnover (Konsumentverket, reviewed 3 Sep 2026, https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/influencers-reklaminlagg-regler-for-foretag/). Payment can be in goods, so free trips must be labelled too. No creator posts before a signed contract, approved brief and label check.
6. **Plan numbers (planning values)**: 2027 programme cash SEK 115k plus in-kind cap SEK 40k; 2028 cash SEK 140k plus in-kind cap SEK 75k (Tier A host costs are carried in the departure P&L, section 5.2). Credited order book from this programme (Tiers A-E, tracked source): about SEK 0.4 M for 2027 (hosted 0.2; creators and ambassadors 0.2) and about SEK 1.8 M for 2028 (hosted 1.1; niche and honeymoon/family creators 0.5; ambassadors 0.2), which together with PR (0.2 in 2028, other workstream) makes L5's 0.4 / 2.0 (section 2). Most of the 2028 value depends on hosted departures with a personality, which is the least proven part. The 2027 result is an investment year, as the strategy already assumes for marketing per booking (about SEK 36k vs the SEK 20k cap).
7. Creators will not move the north star alone. The programme is judged on **booked-not-departed order book (SEK) credited by tracked source, with the gross margin of those bookings**, and on whether it passes the same incrementality and service tests as paid media.

### 4.2 Principles
1. **Proof before spend**: no fixed fee above SEK 15k and no repeat contract without a tracked result. Strategy 11.3 and WS08 say influencer deals pay on booking only and "no fixed-fee placements without proof"; this plan therefore treats every fixed fee below as a **receipt-based production contribution that needs CEO approval per creator**, and the first contact with any Tier B/D creator offers commission plus in-kind trip only. Fees in section 9.2 are released at Gate 1 and are the first line cut.
2. **Trust over reach**: the product is SEK 28-120k tailored or guided trips (WS02). A smaller authentic creator beats a large generic one.
3. **Honest content**: creators keep editorial control of their opinion. We approve facts and legal claims, not enthusiasm. We never require positive reviews.
4. **Claim discipline**: no "Sveriges bästa", no "största" (removed from copy until proven, WS01), never "Sydafrika är säkert". Safety is stated as fact with a link to UD or Ambassaden. The 24/7 local-presence promise is **not used**; the allowed phrase is "jourtelefon under resan" (strategy positioning).
5. **No discounts as the hook**: the offer is the personal adviser, guide and inclusions. Creator codes give a value-add (for example an upgrade or private-guide half day funded from supplier rebates) not a price cut (G2: promotions at most 2% of turnover, CFO sign-off).
6. **One source of truth**: every creator gets a unique code and landing page; credit is only given when the CRM source field matches.
7. **Service gate**: if G8 triggers (Trustpilot rolling below 4.2, SLA missed for two weeks), new paid creator activity pauses and only organic and contracted content continues. The Trustpilot score is a visible risk (2.7 on 8 reviews, strategy R9), so creators must not be sent to the review page as a "proof" until the review programme has lifted it.

Verification tools: start with manual checks plus the creator's own analytics. A paid audit tool trial is budgeted at SEK 15k/year [ASSUMPTION C4, no quote taken]. Do not buy a tool before 5 candidates are shortlisted.

### 5.2 Rates and fee guardrails [ASSUMPTION C5: no rate cards collected]

| Tier | Fixed fee | Variable | In-kind | Max cash + commission per booking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Host fee SEK 5-8k per departure | SEK 1,500 per code-booked pax (about 2.9% of a SEK 52k seat), within the commission cap | Host seat at marginal cost (about SEK 20-25k, C17), in departure P&L | Total host cost (fee + bonus + seat at marginal cost) at most 6% of departure revenue and departure margin after host cost at least 18% (see example) |
| B | SEK 5-15k | up to 4-5% of booked value | one supplier-hosted seat, cap SEK 20-25k marginal | 4-5% commission |
| D | SEK 10-15k | up to 4% | one seat, cap SEK 20-25k marginal | 4% |
| C | none | none | gift card SEK 1-3k | no sales link |

**Commission cap logic**: G5 requires at least 15% margin after partner commission. At a 20% planning margin the maximum is 5% of booked value; for T1 and T5 (18% margin) the maximum is 3%. Commission is calculated on net booked value (excluding taxes and pass-through) and is a cost against the marketing cap per booking (SEK 5k at SEK 101k). The WS08 range 5-8% (and strategy 11.3's 5-8% for affiliate and influencer deals) must be reduced to the cap above, and the cap applies together with any fee: for a 18%-margin product the commission is at most 3% before any fixed fee. The 3% / 5% figures are maxima, not the plan rate: the plan budgets about 4% on Tier B/D (section 9.2).

**Hosted-departure economics example (illustrative, all figures [ASSUMPTION C6])**: a T2 seat of about SEK 52k at 20% gross margin gives SEK 10.4k margin per pax; a departure of 11 pax has revenue of about SEK 572k and margin of about SEK 114k. Host cost: fee SEK 8k + bonus 5 code-booked pax x SEK 1,500 = SEK 7.5k + comped seat at marginal cost SEK 22k = about SEK 37.5k, or 6.6% of revenue. That is slightly above the 6% cap (about SEK 34k), so in this example the fee would be cut to SEK 5k or the bonus capped at 3 pax. **Important:** a host cost of 6% of revenue takes a 20% departure margin down to about 14%, which is below the strategy's 15% hard floor (G1, G5) and the 18% ladder minimum. A hosted departure is therefore only allowed if it is priced so that margin after all host cost is at least 18%, i.e. about 24% before host cost or a price premium of roughly 6-8% over the same trip unhosted. The host also needs to pay back: 5 extra code-booked pax add about SEK 52k margin against SEK 37.5k host cost, break-even is about 4 pax. If the host wants more, the departure gets a higher price or is not hosted. This is why R08's SEK 150-400k Tier A range does not fit the plan. Whether the Signatur/wellness/golf departures can carry a 6-8% premium is a Product (WS02) decision.

**Free trips**: counted at marginal cost, not list price. Rule: comp seats only on departures already above break-even fill, or on supplier-hosted fam trips (product plan assumes some fam trips are supplier-hosted, WS02). Cap: SEK 40k (2027), SEK 75k (2028). Each comp trip needs a deliverable plan before booking. Free trips are payment in goods and must be labelled; tax treatment for the creator and VAT treatment for us are checked with the accountant [VERIFY C7].

## 6. Briefs and content

### 6.1 Brief structure (one page, signed as part of the contract; template in Appendix B)
1. Who we are (one line): "Sydafrikaspecialisten sedan 2003 - personlig rådgivare, guidade resor eller helt skräddarsydda, och resegaranti."
2. Audience and goal: who should feel what; one primary call to action: "Boka ett samtal med en rådgivare" or request quote via the creator's landing page.
3. Key messages (maximum 3) from the brand tone guide (WS01): trygg, personlig, kunnig.
4. Must-haves: label in first line and in video, link with code, one fact about safety linked to UD/Ambassaden when safety is mentioned, the creator's honest personal experience.
5. Must-nots: unsupported superlatives, "säkert", price claims not on the site, "24/7", health claims, promising wildlife sightings, stereotyping, children's faces without parental consent, other brands' trademarks, comparisons with named competitors.
6. Formats, number of deliverables, dates, approval steps (section 6.3).
7. Usage rights and reporting.

### 6.2 Content formats by tier (fit to cohort platform use)

| Format | Platform | Tier | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long film (8-15 min) "Så planerar du X dagar i Sydafrika" with chapter markers | YouTube | B, A | Search and AI-search asset; evergreen; reused in sales emails (WS08) |
| Reels and carousels: "3 saker jag inte visste", "En dag på safari", route maps | Instagram | B, D, C | Reach and saves |
| Live Q&A "Fråga en Sydafrikarådgivare" with creator | Facebook, YouTube | A, B, E | Trust and lead capture |
| Departure announcement "Följ med [namn] på vår hosted-resa" with real seats left | Facebook, Instagram, email | A | Fill (scarcity must be real) |
| Customer story: 60-90 s film or photo story and a text version for the site | Instagram, Facebook, website | C | Social proof and SEO (stories hub) |
| Podcast guest or sponsorship (golf, travel) | Podcast apps | B | Niche authority; sponsor/guest slots at 3-5 shows per year are PR workstream scope [ASSUMPTION C8] |
| Paid-partnership boosting of the creator post (whitelisting) | Instagram, Facebook, YouTube | B, D | Gated Tranche B (see WS07 MP-05, WS08) |

### 6.3 Approval workflow
1. Concept (creator sends outline) -> Social lead reviews within 2 working days.
2. Draft (video or text) -> Brand lead runs the claim checklist (WS01 section on checklist) within 2 working days; counsel on request for safety, price or health content.
3. Label check by a second person (Head of Marketing or delegate): label wording, placement and platform tool.
4. Publish; Social lead takes a dated screenshot and archives the post, link and caption in the creator log.
5. Day 7 and day 30 reporting. Takedown or correction within 24 hours if a problem is found.

### 6.4 Labelling rules (binding for all tiers)
- Mark "Annons" or "Reklam i samarbete med Sydafrikaresor" in the **first line** of the text, in the first seconds of the video (spoken and in writing), and on stories on each frame. Also use the platform paid-partnership tool (Instagram/Facebook "Betalt partnerskap", YouTube "Innehåller betald marknadsföring", TikTok branded content). The recipient must understand it is advertising even on a fleeting look (Konsumentverket guidance above).
- Applies to payment in money **and** goods (free trip, gifts), and to posts the creator makes on their own after a collaboration (the company may still be responsible).
- Customer ambassadors (Tier C) who receive a gift card for telling their story: label "Reklam" as well; the safe route is to treat them as paid content.
- Never ask for, or reward, a positive review. Reviews are requested through the review programme (P1), separately, to all travellers on the same terms.

## 8. Ambassador programme (Tier C) in detail

- **Pool**: after each departure, the CRM review request (day 10-14, P1) goes to all travellers on the same terms (6.4; strategy P1 says "gated by satisfaction", which Legal should check against Trustpilot's and consumer-law rules on selective review invitations). The separate ambassador invitation ("Vill du dela din resa?", Appendix B5) goes to travellers who rated 9-10 or left a good review, because it is a story invitation and not a review request. Target: 12 stories in 2027, 24 in 2028. Against the strategy's ~1,070 and ~1,400 travellers that is about 1.1% and 1.7% of travellers, or roughly 20% of the 9-10 raters who are invited, if about 60 are invited in 2027 [ASSUMPTION C11; replace with traveller counts and the real rating distribution].
- **Offer**: SEK 1-3k travel credit or gift card, a feature on our channels, an optional invitation to a yearly "Sydafrikakväll" (see events below). Not tied to a sale or to a rating.
- **Output per ambassador**: 1 photo story plus 1 short text; optional 60 s film; permission for Instagram, Facebook, site and email.
- **Referral**: the referral reward belongs to L7 and the CRM workstream. A Tier C story that later leads to a booking is credited to L5 only if the booking's source is the ambassador's public content; a personal referral goes to L7. This prevents double counting.
- **Community**: a moderated Facebook group "Sydafrikaresor - resecommunity" run by an adviser (R08: 26% prefer smaller circles, and Facebook is the main platform for 45+). Owned by the CRM and community line (CRM automation, review programme, community, SEK 0.30 M in 2027); this workstream supplies content and ambassadors only.
- **Events**: one annual customer evening (online or in Stockholm/Göteborg) with a personality and guide; cost in the partnerships/fairs line and not here.

### 9.4 Programme cost per credited booking (planning arithmetic)
- 2027: cash 115k + in-kind 40k + commission about 4k = about SEK 159k. Credited bookings for Tier B/C/D about 2 (SEK 0.2 M) -> **about SEK 80k per booking**. This is high because the hosted-departure upside is credited to Tier A. Counting hosted bookings too (about 4 bookings total, L5 0.4) gives about SEK 40k per booking **excluding** Tier A host cost, in line with the strategy's investment-year figure (about 36k) but **above the SEK 20k cap**. Accepted only as a 2027 investment, with the kill rules in section 12.
- 2028: cash 140k + in-kind 75k + commission 20k = about SEK 235k for about 7 creator and ambassador bookings = **about SEK 34k per booking**; including about 11 hosted-departure bookings (18 in total) the programme cost is about SEK 13k per booking excluding host cost, and about SEK 17-19k including host cost for 2-3 personality departures (about SEK 37k each). [ASSUMPTION C14: these are planning values on 101k per booking; true booking values and party sizes are unknown.]
- Break-even check: a SEK 101k booking at 20% margin yields about SEK 20k of gross margin, so a creator-sourced booking is margin-positive only if its programme cost is below roughly SEK 20k. Programme cost above that is brand and trust investment that the CEO must accept explicitly or cut.
