## 1. Purpose and what this workstream must deliver

The strategy says sydafrikaresor.se must sit about 15-20% above the group-with-flights cluster (Albatros SEK 36,998-38,998, Tourcompass SEK 36,095-51,095, African Tours SEK 39,995; R03) without competing on price. Reviewer check: the strategy's own example, the 13-day guided product at SEK 44,300, is +14% to +20% against Albatros, +23% against the Tourcompass entry price and only +11% against African Tours, so "15-20%" is true for part of the cluster only, and holds only if the products are comparable in route, length and flights included (unverified). Treat 15-20% as a working figure to be recomputed per product pair by 15 Dec 2026 [VERIFY]. A premium that is argued rather than shown will not convert.

Reviewer flag on a strategy tension: strategy P4 builds the flights-included guided ladder at SEK 36-45k, which sits at, not above, the group cluster. The premium-proof block (4.2) is therefore needed on products priced above the cluster; on ladder-entry products the message is "same price band, more included" and must not claim a premium that is not charged. CEO to confirm which products carry the premium (open question 1). This workstream turns the positioning statement into (a) a messaging house every channel plan draws from, (b) a tone of voice and copy rules that are legally safe, (c) a proof system that makes each claim true and visible, and (d) a measurement plan that tells us whether the brand is working.

## 3. Brand positioning

### 3.1 Positioning statement (binding, from strategy 6.1)
För svenskar som planerar en långresa till Sydafrika och vill göra det ordentligt är sydafrikaresor.se **Sydafrikaspecialisten sedan 2003**: en namngiven personlig rådgivare, rundresor med svensktalande guide eller helt skräddarsydda resor, och en bokning som skyddas av resegaranti.

English: for Swedes planning a long-haul trip to South Africa who want it done properly and will pay for expertise, we are the South Africa specialist since 2003: a named adviser, guided trips with Swedish-speaking guides or fully tailor-made itineraries, and a guaranteed booking. We cover all of South Africa and southern Africa and do not compete on lowest price.

### 3.2 Brand idea (internal, not a tagline)
"Vi har varit där, och vi tar hand om det." Expertise that is personal, specific and on your side. Everything customer-facing should make the reader feel that a particular person who knows the country is doing the worrying for them.

### 3.3 Four brand attributes and what they mean in practice

| Attribute | Means | Does not mean | Visible proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kunnig (knowledgeable) | Specific: names the lodge, the month, the reason | Jargon, travel-brochure adjectives | Adviser bios with years and regions; "varför vi lägger upp resan så här" notes on each itinerary |
| Personlig (personal) | A named person answers; the same person follows the trip | Chatty over-familiarity | Named adviser on quote, on confirmation and in the jourtelefon message |
| Trygg (secure) | Facts on safety, money and protection; no hype | Reassurance without evidence; "säkert" | Resegaranti lookup link, standard information form, safety hub quoting UD/Ambassaden |
| Rak (straight) | Plain about price, inclusions and risks | Cheap or blunt | All mandatory costs in the price, "ingår/ingår inte" tables, honest "passar inte dig om..." sections |

### 4.3 Safety messaging framework
1. State the official position as the Embassy words it: very high crime level and many traffic accidents, yet the vast majority of visits pass without serious incident; be alert, especially in traffic, after dark and when handling money (swedenabroad.se, R09). Link to the source and quote it without softening.
2. Say what we do: pre-booked transfers, daytime driving, vetted lodges, guided township visits, pre-departure briefing, jourtelefon.
3. Say what the traveller does (short practical list).
4. Never promise "säker", "riskfri" or "inga problem". Do not use "Boka helt riskfritt" (Tourcompass language, R03).
5. Crisis rule: if UD issues a travel warning, safety copy is pulled and replaced with the incident template (section 11, risk R3) within 4 hours; ad groups pause until counsel confirms wording.

## 6. Governance: claim checklist and approval

Every public asset (ad, page, email, social post, creator brief, press text) passes this checklist before publishing. Owner: Head of Marketing; legal escalation: CEO/CFO with counsel.

1. Is each factual claim in the claim register as GREEN (or AMBER with condition met)?
2. No unsupported superlative or comparative ("störst", "bäst", "billigast", "Sveriges ledande").
3. Price claims include taxes and mandatory costs; "från"-price has a bookable departure behind it; flights-included is explicit.
4. Safety text quotes UD/Ambassaden and promises no outcome.
5. Creator/partner content is labelled "Annons" in first line and in video; brief states claim limits.
6. Consent exists for emails and for use of customer quotes/photos.
7. Does the copy match what sales and ops can deliver this month (SLA, guide, supply)?
8. Does it respect the supply gate: no push for departures without held allocation (strategy 13.2, 13.7)?

Approval levels: copy from the bank = Head of Marketing; new claim or comparison = CEO plus counsel; crisis = CEO. Claim register reviewed monthly in the growth council (strategy 16.3).

## 14. Success decision rules

- Continue and extend investment in brand proof if at Gate 1 the Trustpilot trend, SLA and enquiry-rate test are all in range.
- Narrow to the pillars that test best (and drop weak ones) if Gate 1 shows no lift in the proof-block test and new-review average is below 3.5 (and in any case below the G8 4.2 floor triggers the spend rule).
- Hold all new claims, escalate to CEO and counsel, if any complaint or authority inquiry reaches the company.
- Never trade margin for brand: discounts above 2% of turnover or price-match promises require CFO approval (A20; strategy 14).
