## 1. Purpose and what this workstream must deliver

The strategy says sydafrikaresor.se must sit about 15-20% above the group-with-flights cluster (Albatros SEK 36,998-38,998, Tourcompass SEK 36,095-51,095, African Tours SEK 39,995; R03) without competing on price. A premium that is argued rather than shown will not convert. This workstream turns the positioning statement into (a) a messaging house every channel plan draws from, (b) a tone of voice and copy rules that are legally safe, (c) a proof system that makes each claim true and visible, and (d) a measurement plan that tells us whether the brand is working.

## 3. Brand positioning

### 3.1 Positioning statement (binding, from strategy 6.1)
För svenskar som planerar en långresa till Sydafrika och vill göra det ordentligt är sydafrikaresor.se **Sydafrika-specialisten sedan 2003**: en namngiven personlig rådgivare, rundresor med svensktalande guide eller helt skräddarsydda resor, och en bokning som skyddas av resegaranti.

English: for Swedes planning a long-haul trip to South Africa who want it done properly and will pay for expertise, we are the South Africa specialist since 2003: a named adviser, guided trips with Swedish-speaking guides or fully tailor-made itineraries, and a guaranteed booking. We cover all of South Africa and southern Africa and do not compete on lowest price.

### 3.2 Brand idea (internal, not a tagline)
"Vi har varit där, och vi tar hand om det." Expertise that is personal, specific and on your side. Everything customer-facing should make the reader feel that a particular person who knows the country is doing the worrying for them.

### 3.3 Four brand attributes and what they mean in practice

| Attribute | Means | Does not mean | Visible proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kunnig (knowledgeable) | Specific: names the lodge, the month, the reason | Jargon, travel-brochure adjectives | Adviser bios with years and regions; "varför vi lägger upp resan så här" notes on each itinerary |
| Personlig (personal) | A named person answers; the same person follows the trip | Chatty over-familiarity | Named adviser on quote, on confirmation and in the jourtelefon message |
| Trygg (secure) | Facts on safety, money and protection; no hype | Reassurance without evidence; "säkert" | Resegaranti lookup link, standard information form, safety hub quoting UD/Ambassaden |
| Rak (straight) | Plain about price, inclusions and risks | Cheap or blunt | All mandatory costs in the price, "ingår/ingår inte" tables, honest "passar inte dig om..." sections |

### 3.4 Where the brand sits (competitive map)

| Axis | Mass (Ving, TUI, Apollo) | Group (Albatros, Tourcompass, Temaresor) | Tailor-made specialists (MyChoice, Safariresor.se, Afrikaresor.se) | **sydafrikaresor.se** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Range of South Africa | Cape Town centred | One or two fixed routes | Broad, often land-only | Whole of South Africa and southern Africa, guided and tailor-made |
| Personal contact | Call centre | Tour leader only | Named advisers | Named adviser plus Swedish-speaking guide on guided trips |
| Flexibility | Low | Low (fixed dates, 16 pax minimum, no children under 12 at Albatros) | High | High, with published guided departures as an easy entry |
| Price level | Lowest | SEK 36-40k with flights | SEK 25-45k, often land-only | About 15-20% above group cluster [A: strategy 6.2] |
| Visible proof | Brand | Reviews 4.6 | Reviews 4.7-5.0 | Weakest today (2.7 on 8); must be fixed first |

The open ground: specialist breadth plus guided departures plus a named adviser. No competitor in R03 combines all three with published dates. This is the lane. The weak point is reviews, so proof comes before volume.

### 4.2 Proof-of-premium block ("Därför kostar det mer än en gruppresa") - the key conversion asset
Because we sit 15-20% above group trips (e.g. Albatros SEK 36,998-38,998 versus our Mat, Vin & Big Five SEK 44,300, R03), each guided product page carries a plain table. Content must be product-specific and verified; the rows below are the structure, not facts.

| Row | What we show | Data needed from Product |
|---|---|---|
| Flyg | Whether international flights are included and which routing (if not included, state it plainly in the headline price) | Per product [VERIFY: unknown whether "from" prices include flights] |
| Gruppstorlek | Actual maximum group size | Per departure |
| Guide | Name, languages, years with us | Guide roster |
| Boende | Lodge names and category | Itinerary |
| Flexibilitet | Children, solo travellers, private extension, date changes | Terms |
| Support | Named adviser and jourtelefon | Ops |
| Skydd | Resegaranti | Compliance |

Rule: no comparison against a named competitor's price in customer copy (misleading-comparison risk, to be cleared with counsel [VERIFY]). Compare categories ("typisk gruppresa med fast datum") not brands.

### 4.3 Safety messaging framework
1. State the official position: UD/Ambassaden say high crime level, most visits pass without serious incident (R09). Link to the source.
2. Say what we do: pre-booked transfers, daytime driving, vetted lodges, guided township visits, pre-departure briefing, jourtelefon.
3. Say what the traveller does (short practical list).
4. Never promise "säker", "riskfri" or "inga problem". Do not use "Boka helt riskfritt" (Tourcompass language, R03).
5. Crisis rule: if UD issues a travel warning, safety copy is pulled and replaced with the incident template (section 9, risk R3) within 4 hours; ad groups pause until counsel confirms wording.

## 5. Tone of voice

### 5.1 Principles

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Konkret | "Sabi Sand i maj, när gräset är lågt och djuren syns bättre" [check fact with Product before use] | "En oförglömlig upplevelse i hjärtat av Afrika" |
| Varm men saklig | "Vi ringer dig i dag" | Utropstecken-kedjor, emoji-överdrift |
| Rak om pris | "Priset inkluderar flyg, ... Det ingår inte: ..." | "Från endast!" utan förbehåll |

## 6. Governance: claim checklist and approval

Every public asset (ad, page, email, social post, creator brief, press text) passes this checklist before publishing. Owner: Head of Marketing; legal escalation: CEO/CFO with counsel.

1. Is each factual claim in the claim register as GREEN (or AMBER with condition met)?
2. No unsupported superlative or comparative ("störst", "bäst", "billigast", "Sveriges ledande").
3. Price claims include taxes and mandatory costs; "från"-price has a bookable departure behind it; flights-included is explicit.
4. Safety text quotes UD/Ambassaden and promises no outcome.
5. Creator/partner content is labelled "Annons" in first line and in video; brief states claim limits.
6. Consent exists for emails and for use of customer quotes/photos.
7. Does the copy match what sales and ops can deliver this month (SLA, guide, supply)?
8. Does it respect the supply gate: no push for departures without held allocation (strategy 13.2, 13.7)?

Approval levels: copy from the bank = Head of Marketing; new claim or comparison = CEO plus counsel; crisis = CEO. Claim register reviewed monthly in the growth council (strategy 16.3).

## 14. Success decision rules

- Continue and extend investment in brand proof if at Gate 1 the Trustpilot trend, SLA and enquiry-rate test are all in range.

- Hold all new claims, escalate to CEO and counsel, if any complaint or authority inquiry reaches the company.
- Never trade margin for brand: discounts above 2% of turnover or price-match promises require CFO approval (A20; strategy 14).
