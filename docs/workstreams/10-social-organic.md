## 1. Role of organic social in the 40 to 80 MSEK plan

### 1.1 What it is for, and what it is not

Organic social is the **proof and relationship layer**, not a volume engine. For a 30-80k SEK, high-consideration trip, social inspires and validates; the quote is requested on the site, by phone or after Google (R08 section 3). Our job on social is to make a Swede aged roughly 45-70 who is thinking about South Africa believe three things within a few weeks: (1) these people know the country, (2) real travellers like me were looked after, (3) there is a dated departure or a named adviser I can talk to.

| Role | How organic social serves it | Lever |
|---|---|---|
| Proof distribution | Reviews, customer stories, named advisers and guides turned into posts; feeds Meta and email creative bank | L2 (trust lifts close rate), L7 |
| Hosted-departure demand | Announce, fill and follow hosted departures (2-4 in 2027, 4-6 a year from 2028); real scarcity only | L5 (+2.0 MSEK by 2028, evidence grade C) |
| Community and referral | Moderated Facebook group for past and future travellers; referral and repeat loop | L7 (+3.0 MSEK) |
| Audience for retargeting | Video-view and engager audiences feed Meta Tranche A retargeting (workstream 07) | L3 (supporting) |
| Search and AI-search support | Social proof signals, YouTube/Instagram embeds on itinerary pages, brand-search demand | L4 |
| Reputation early warning | Social listening on Flashback, Reseguiden, Trustpilot mentions, comments | G8 service gate |

### 1.2 Honest sizing

- Lever L5 is +0.4 MSEK in 2027 and +2.0 MSEK by 2028 combined for social, creators, hosted departures and PR (strategy 9). At about SEK 99k per booking in 2027 and SEK 101k in 2028 [A1, 45k/46k x 2.2 travellers] that is roughly 4 bookings in 2027 and 20 in 2028 credited to L5. Organic social alone will not deliver this; hosted departures, creators and PR do, and social is how they are announced and filled.
- Scale check (reviewer addition): one hosted departure of 12 travellers [A23] at about SEK 45k is roughly SEK 0.5 M of turnover, so 4-6 full departures would be SEK 2-3 M, more than the L5 credit in 2027 (0.4) and about equal to it in 2028 (2.0). Only bookings that would not have happened without the hosted departure, creator or PR push (incremental) may be credited to L5; the rest are existing flow (L2) or other levers. This workstream therefore plans 2-4 hosted departures in 2027 and 4-6 in 2028 [SO14], and the CEO should confirm how incrementality is judged (open question 4).
- Direct last-click bookings from organic social will be small and hard to see. The plan therefore measures assisted conversions, "how did you hear about us" answers and qualified enquiries from tagged links (section 9), and does not claim bookings it cannot trace.
- Marginal marketing cost cap: SEK 20k per marketing-created booking (G4). The organic social line is small (SEK 0.35 M in 2027, section 11). Its labour is NOT inside the strategy's five incremental FTE (strategy 12.1 and 13.3 list consultants, a booking-ops coordinator and a product/contracting manager only), so the social manager must be reallocated from existing staff or added as a CEO decision that worsens the loss budget (section 11.2). On line spend alone the L5 cost per credited booking is about SEK 87k in 2027 (350k / 4) and SEK 22.5k in 2028 (450k / 20), above the SEK 20k cap; the strategy-level G4 figures (2027 about 36k, 2028 about 18k) pool L3, L4, L5 and L8. This is an investment year; the line is justified mainly by its proof, community and retargeting-audience role, and the quarterly G4 check applies (section 9.4).
- Audience fit: R08 section 2.2 shows the 1960-1989 cohorts on Facebook 77-83%, Instagram 67-76%, YouTube 73-83%; TikTok only 7-12%. Facebook and Instagram are therefore the organic core; YouTube is covered in workstream 08; TikTok and Pinterest are repurposing experiments only.

## 3. Approach: principles

1. **People before places.** Named advisers, guides and customers are the content. Generic scenery is what every competitor posts. Pictures of lodges and landscapes are supporting actors (R08 sections 4.2, 6).
2. **Show the premium, do not argue it.** Positioning sits 15-20% above the group-with-flights cluster. Social shows what is included, who the guide is, what the adviser does and what happens when something goes wrong (workstream 01 section 4.2).
3. **Real scarcity or none.** "Platser kvar" appears only when the supply truth owner (Head of Product) confirms held allocation. No departure promotion before Gate 0 supply confirmation (15 Dec 2026) except non-bookable inspiration.
4. **Claims discipline.** Workstream 01 section 6 checklist applies to every post. Banned: "störst/bäst/billigast/säkrast", "riskfri", "24/7 lokal närvaro" (use "jourtelefon under resan"), "exotisk", "äkta Afrika". The home page's own "Skandinaviens största" line is not repeated on social until substantiated.
5. **Respect for the country.** Name places and people correctly; include local guides and communities; no poverty-tourism framing; no filming of cub-petting, feeding or off-road driving (workstream 08).
6. **Honest about risk.** Safety posts quote UD and the Swedish embassy, promise no outcome, and describe practices only if they exist in the product spec (R09; workstream 01 section 5.3).
7. **Reuse before create.** One long-form asset (YouTube film, customer interview, adviser Q&A) is cut into 6-10 social assets. Production budget is small by design.
8. **Two-way, not broadcast.** Questions in comments become next week's content (Reseguiden-style questions are the best brief; R08 section 5).
9. **Capacity gate.** No community push or giveaway that creates enquiries while the response SLA is missed (strategy 11.2).

## 6. Community management

### 6.1 Roles and rota

| Task | Owner | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Comment and DM triage | Social and community manager | 3 check-ins per working day (09:00, 12:30, 16:00) |
| Sales hand-off of enquiries from DMs/comments | Social and community manager to Head of Sales rota | Immediately; CRM record with source "organic social" |
| Product questions | Head of Product or named adviser | Answer within 1 working day |
| Complaints and negative reviews | Customer service lead; CEO informed if it concerns safety, a guide or a media risk | Reply within 4 working hours |
| Evening/weekend monitoring | Rota of 2 people on peak weeks (Jan-Mar) | 20 minutes/day; urgent issues to on-call |

### 6.2 Reply principles and templates (Swedish)

Rules: reply as a named person ("Hej, det är Maria på Sydafrikaresor"), use "du", never argue in public, move sensitive details to DM or phone, never delete a critical comment unless it breaks the page rules (abuse, hate, personal data, spam). Never reply to Flashback or anonymous forums as the brand.

Template - question about price:
"Hej [namn]! Bra fråga. Priset beror på månad, boende och hur du vill resa. Skriv till oss eller ring, så får du ett förslag med exakt vad som ingår och inte ingår: [länk]. / [Rådgivarens namn]"

Template - safety question:
"Hej [namn]! Sveriges ambassad i Sydafrika beskriver brottsnivån som mycket hög, men de flesta besök förlöper utan allvarliga händelser. Det här gör vi för att minska riskerna: [verifierad punktlista från Product]. Läs UD:s aktuella information här: [länk]. Vill du prata igenom din resa kan en rådgivare ringa dig."

Template - negative review or complaint (public):
"Hej [namn], tack för att du berättar. Vi tycker det är tråkigt att resan inte blev som du förväntade dig. Vi vill gärna förstå vad som hänt och hjälpa dig. Skicka ditt bokningsnummer i ett meddelande så ringer [namn] dig i dag. / [namn], Sydafrikaresor"

Template - praise:
"Tack, [namn]! Det värmer. Vi hälsar till [guidens namn] och teamet. Får vi lov att dela din bild i vårt flöde?" (consent captured in writing before reuse)

Template - off-topic or spam: hide, do not reply; log if repeated.

### 6.3 Escalation matrix

| Level | Trigger | Action | Owner | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normal question or mild criticism | Reply per template | Community manager | 4 working hours |
| 2 | Complaint about a guide, supplier, refund or delay | Private channel, call back, log in CRM | Customer service lead | Same day |
| 3 | Safety incident involving travellers, media enquiry, viral negative post, legal threat | Pause scheduled posts, holding statement, CEO and Head of Marketing | CEO | Within 1 hour |
| 4 | Incident abroad affecting a departure | Follow crisis section in workstream 01 section 9; no marketing content until cleared | CEO | Immediate |

Pause rule: when a Level 3-4 event occurs, all scheduled posts and paid social are paused until the Head of Marketing resumes them.

### 6.4 Group governance (from 1 Feb 2027)

- Name [VERIFY availability]: "Sydafrikaresor - resecommunity". Closed group; admins: community manager plus one adviser; moderator rules pinned.
- Rules: no competitor selling, respectful tone, no personal data, no medical advice, posts about the brand's own departures clearly labelled.
- Seed with 50-100 past customers by personal invitation from their adviser (email consent basis, not mass-add).
- Weekly rhythm: Monday "Veckans fråga" (adviser answers), Wednesday customer photo, Friday "Var är du nu?" posts from travellers in country.
- GDPR: group posts are public to members only; moderator never copies member data to CRM without consent. Contest data collection follows the company's privacy policy (R08 section 8).

## 9. Measurement

### 9.1 Principles

Organic social is judged on **assisted revenue and qualified enquiries**, plus leading indicators, never on likes. Source of truth is the CRM (source field plus the question "Hur hörde du om oss?"), reconciled weekly with GA4 and platform analytics.

### 9.2 Tracking spec

- UTM: `utm_source={facebook|instagram|linkedin|group}&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign={pillar_or_departure}&utm_content={post_id}`. Creators: unique `utm_source=creator_{name}` plus a discount-neutral code for tracking (no price promises without Product approval).
- Link in bio: tracked landing page "Planera din resa" with the budget-bracket quote form (shared with workstream 08).
- Phone: call-source question and call tracking number for social-only phone display [SO8].
- Post IDs in a content register so any enquiry can be traced to the post.

### 9.4 Decision rules

- **Channel check each quarter.** If a platform's tracked cost per qualified enquiry (labour + production allocated) exceeds 2x the blended level for two consecutive quarters, reduce cadence by half and redeploy to the top pillar.
- **Pillar check.** After 60 days, the pillar with the lowest saves/shares and lowest click-through loses a slot to the best.
- **Creator kill.** Fewer than 3 qualified enquiries within 45 days of publishing and a cost per qualified enquiry above 2.5x blended paid CAC: no renewal.
- **Hosted departure kill.** Under 40% sold at T-60, i.e. after an announcement push of at least 4 weeks from T-90: review price, host or cancel (minimum numbers per package law notice dates, R09).
- **Service gate.** If G8 is breached (response SLA missed two consecutive weeks), no new hosted-departure pushes and no giveaways until fixed.
