## 2. Approach: how the roadmap works

1. **Four phases**, each ending in a gate. Phase 0 (Nov-Dec 2026) is baseline and no-regret work; Phase 1 (Q1-Q2 2027) proves the funnel and the model; Phase 2 (Q3-Q4 2027) scales what is proven and builds the 2028 order book; Phase 3 (2028) harvests, scales winners and decides on a 2029 base.
2. **No-regret versus irreversible.** Start now: allocation calls, speed-to-lead SLA, review automation, tracking, FX policy, the first SEO hubs. Hold until 31 Jan 2027 data or the named gate: hires beyond the pre-approved two (consultant 1 and the booking-ops coordinator), Tranche B paid, 24/7 local-presence promise, any prepaid supply above SEK 4 M, any gap-lever spend beyond screening.
3. **Gates test four things, not revenue alone**: margin (order-book gross margin at or above 19% on new bookings), service (response SLA, Trustpilot, complaints per 1,000 travellers), supply (held supply at least equal to the order-book trajectory) and incrementality (holdout-proven ROAS). Missing two or more triggers the stop-loss.
4. **One cadence.** Weekly one-page dashboard; monthly growth council (CEO chairs); quarterly bridge re-run with actuals and budget re-allocation; gate meetings on fixed dates with a written pass/hold/cut decision recorded in the decision log (section 9).
5. **Seasonality drives the calendar.** The plan assumes enquiries peak Nov-Mar (strategy A16 puts 50-60% of paid spend there) and that 2028 departures are largely booked during Q4 2027 to Q2 2028 [ASSUMPTION: booking-date curve unknown, replace with company data by Gate 0; the order-book checkpoints of 50% / 75% / 30% are strategy A11]. Content must be indexed by Aug-Oct to serve the January peak (strategy section 11.3).
