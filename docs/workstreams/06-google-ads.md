## 1. Purpose, role in the bridge and objectives

Google Search is the largest single paid line in the strategy: **SEK 0.85 M in 2027 and SEK 1.10 M in 2028** (strategy 11.1), inside a total paid-media line of 1.5 and 2.0 MSEK. It serves two different jobs that the strategy deliberately separates (strategy 11.2):

- **Tranche A: defend and capture existing demand.** Brand and high-intent search (people ready to book or request a proposal). This demand is already counted in the 2,148 existing-channel enquiries, so it protects the base and is judged on cost per qualified enquiry and a brand holdout, not on "ROAS".
- **Tranche B: prospect for new demand.** Route, "when/how much", segment, extension and competitor-adjacent search, plus tests of Performance Max / Demand Gen. This is the only Google spend credited to lever L3 (+1.2 MSEK in 2027, +4.0 MSEK in 2028 across all channels). It is released only on holdout-proven incremental ROAS, and it is the lever the strategy says is most likely to be cut.

What Google must deliver in numbers (plan of record, all [GA] assumptions, section 10): about 600 qualified-intent enquiries a year from Tranche A defence in 2027 (651 in 2028) and about 190 (2027) to 310 (2028) new enquiries from Tranche B, at a planned cost of about SEK 890-940 per A enquiry and about SEK 1,450-1,500 per B enquiry. Against the strategy's new-traffic need of about 1,200 enquiries in 2028, Google Search B supplies roughly a quarter; SEO, Meta, partners and referral must supply the rest.

Contribution to the bridge: Tranche A is not credited to any lever (it defends existing flow). Search B is credited with about 0.5 MSEK (2027) and 1.8 MSEK (2028) of L3's +1.2 and +4.0 MSEK, allocating L3 pro rata by spend across channels [GA20]. Planned this way Search B returns about 1.7x (2027) and 4.0x (2028) incremental ROAS, below the 5x break-even at 20% margin, and alone costs more per incremental booking than the SEK 20,000 programme cap (section 10.2). The plan of record therefore funds Search B as a gated learning investment, not as a proven return.

## 3. Approach: ten principles

1. **Start narrow, prove, then widen.** December 2026 to March 2027 is brand plus high-intent only. Prospecting waits for Gate 1 and then ramps as a staged pilot with its own holdout.
2. **Optimise to qualified enquiry, then to margin.** Raw form fills are a secondary signal. The primary conversion is a *qualified* enquiry (travel dates or month, number of travellers, budget band, route/type chosen) or a call of useful length; booked turnover and gross margin are imported from the CRM (workstream 03 asks for margin, not only value).
3. **Incremental, not attributed.** Attributed ROAS on Tranche A will look enormous (section 10) because it largely captures people who would have found us anyway. It is a sanity check only. Decisions use holdouts and the incremental ROAS rules in strategy 11.2.
4. **Own the terms the giants ignore.** Per R03 recommendation 5: Kruger safari, Garden Route, golf, bröllopsresa, familjeresa, Sydafrika med barn, best time, long-tail combination trips. Avoid pure "Kapstaden hotell" and flight-only queries where TUI/Ving/momondo dominate.
5. **Premium shown, not argued.** Ads sell the adviser, the guide, the flexibility and the guarantee, never the lowest price. No discount-led copy; early-booking offers are value-adds (strategy 11.3).
6. **Never promise what is not costed.** No "24/7" or "lokalt kontor dygnet runt": copy says "jourtelefon under resan" (strategy 6). No "Sveriges största" or other superlatives. No Trustpilot rating in ads until it is at or above 4.5.
7. **Capacity gates budget.** No budget moves up while the response SLA is missed; ads for departures that are sold out or not held in allocation are paused the same day (Head of Product feed, section 12.3).
8. **Control the account, do not outsource the thinking.** If an agency is used, the company owns the Google Ads account, the Tag Manager container, GA4 and the CRM link, and gets read access to everything.
9. **Simple structure that survives low volume.** Few campaigns with enough data each, not 200 ad groups. Volume is modest (SEK 35-100k a month in 2027, up to SEK 150k in the peak months of 2028), so consolidation beats granularity.
10. **Compliance by design.** Claims, price statements and consent are fixed before launch because a policy disapproval or a Konsumentverket complaint in the main booking window is expensive.

How to read this. (1) Tranche A's 20x+ attributed ROAS is **not** a performance claim: it is mostly existing demand and says nothing about incrementality. (2) The share of Google A in existing-flow enquiries (about 600 of 2,086 in 2027) is an unverified guess and must be reconciled with the account in January. (3) Tranche B in 2027 is planned at 1.7x incremental ROAS and in 2028 at 4.0x, both below the 5x break-even at 20% margin (strategy 11.2). Applied as written, the 4x continue rule (strategy 11.2) means B continues only if the holdout read is far better than this plan (4x versus 1.7x). The cap is programme-wide, so SEO, referral and partners can offset a B overshoot, but B cannot be assumed to pay for itself.

### 10.3 Break-even and allowable cost per enquiry

First-trip gross profit per enquiry from new traffic is about 0.8 x 0.18 x SEK 19.8k = **SEK 2,850**; from existing-style traffic about SEK 4,100. If only 40% of attributed B enquiries are truly incremental (the 2028 plan), the break-even *attributed* cost per enquiry is about SEK 1,140, below the planned SEK 1,455-1,500. B therefore only pays back through repeat/referral value (L7) and mix upgrades, or if incrementality is higher than planned. That is why B is gated and why holdouts matter more than any optimisation tactic.

### 14.3 Offline conversion import and the click-identifier window

Imports run daily from the CRM using click identifiers (owner: CRM manager). **Known constraint:** Google limits the age of a click identifier for offline imports; at the time of writing the documented limit is 90 days [VERIFY in Google documentation], while the average consideration-to-booking time for long-haul bookings is likely longer than 90 days (unknown; request from sales data). Mitigation: (1) import intermediate stages (qualified, quote sent, deposit) that occur inside the window, (2) use stage values that reflect expected downstream value, recomputed monthly from CRM close rates by source, (3) use enhanced conversions for leads for the hashed-contact route, (4) report booked turnover and margin by first-touch source in the CRM and compare with Ads attribution quarterly.

### 14.4 Incrementality design

| Test | Design | Start | Read | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brand pause | Pause C1 for matched weeks (or for alternating geos where volume allows). Compare total enquiries from brand-related paths (organic + paid) with and without the ad | Jan 2027, 4 weeks (low volume: directional) | 31 Mar 2027 | Keep C1 at current spend only if loss without ads is meaningful; else cut to a floor |
| High-intent holdout (A) | Geo split on matched Swedish regions: withhold C2-C5 in one matched set of regions for 6 weeks. Compare qualified enquiries per capita; adjust for organic ranking #1 | Feb-Mar 2027 | First read by 31 Mar 2027 (Gate 1) | Tells how much of A is incremental to existing organic and direct |
| Tranche B pilot | C7/C8 from 1 Apr 2027 with a matched-geo holdout from week 1 | 1 Apr 2027 | 8 weeks: leading read on incremental qualified enquiries x CRM close rate; booked read at 6 and 9 months | Pause if incremental ROAS under 4x after 8 weeks; scale only at 5x and SLA met (strategy 11.2) |
| Demand Gen / YouTube remarketing | Audience holdout 10-15% | Q2 2027 | 8 weeks | Scale only on incremental result |

Power: monthly volumes are low (tens to a few hundred enquiries). Back-of-envelope [GA22]: at about 600 A enquiries a year, a 6-week holdout over roughly a third of the population covers only about 25-35 enquiries, so a difference smaller than roughly 40-50% cannot be told from noise. The A holdout can therefore only expose a gross over-attribution, not size a modest one. The brand pause is cheaper still but also less informative: C1 is only SEK 30-35k a year, so run it only if it costs negligible staff time and otherwise keep C1 as insurance. Small tests are directional; use longer windows (6-8 weeks), matched regions and pooled reads rather than chasing significance. The booking read lags 3-9 months, so the 8-week decision uses the **leading** metric (incremental qualified enquiries x observed close rate for that source) and is confirmed on booked value later. If the leading read and the booked read disagree by more than 30%, the close-rate assumption is updated.
