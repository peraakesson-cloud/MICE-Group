## 2. Operating principles

1. **The partner owns the audience, we own the trip.** Partners (clubs, societies, organisations) endorse and distribute; we hold the booking, contract, guarantee and service. This keeps GDPR and package-travel responsibility clean (section 12).
2. **Value-adds, not discounts.** Consistent with the positioning (about 15-20% above the group-with-flights cluster, no price race) and rule G2. Member benefits are experiences, access and flexibility, funded from supplier rebates and margin.
3. **Benefit is paid only on incremental customers.** Commission, donation or organiser credit is paid on bookings from customers who are new to our CRM (suppression-list match). Existing contacts who book with a partner code are attributed but earn the partner nothing. This is what makes the channel margin-safe.
4. **Margin floor travels with the benefit.** Total partner benefits (commission, club contribution, organiser place) are capped at 8% of booking value and must leave at least 15% gross margin on that booking. Group quotes are therefore priced for at least 23% before benefits (23% minus the 8% cap = 15%; the v1 figure of 22% would have allowed 14%, below the G1/G5 floor) [ASSUMPTION; if the true margin is lower the benefit cap shrinks automatically, see section 6.4].
5. **Supply first.** No dated partner departure is published until allocation is held at the lodge, golf and DMC level (strategy sections 13 and 14: at least 60% of planned volume contracted, guide and DMC confirmed).
6. **Named adviser and Swedish-speaking guide are the product.** The partner promise is a person, not a price. The copy says "jourtelefon under resan"; the 24/7 local-presence promise is not used until costed and staffed. "Skandinaviens största" is not used.
7. **Small, sequenced pilots.** Three partners before any scale-up, kill criteria at each gate, no irreversible spend before 31 Jan 2027.

Reach is the lever to watch: 43,000 contacts is about 7% of the golf membership base alone (588,069), but it is spread over all three segments, and it is reached mainly through partner newsletters, magazines and social posts. ## 5. Approach in five steps

1. **Build the target list and the offer pack** (Nov 2026 - Jan 2027). 12 named affinity targets and 10 named corporate accounts go into the partner pipeline of 30 named targets due 31 Jan 2027 (strategy 16.1).
2. **Prove with three pilots** (Feb - Jun 2027): one golf, one wine/food, one member organisation. Each pilot has a signed one-page agreement, a tracked landing page and a member communication.
3. **Gate 2 decision** (30 Jun 2027): scale winners, drop the rest, decide the GL2 pilot outcome.
4. **Scale with dated departures** (Jul - Dec 2027): publish winter 2027/28 and 2028/29 hosted departures once supply is held; run about 8 club or chapter evenings before year-end (section 7.3), the rest in the Jan - Mar 2028 window.
5. **Repeat and expand** (2028): turn first travellers into ambassadors, add up to 14 partners, renew or exit partners by performance.

## 6. Partner programme design

### 6.1 Three partnership tiers

| Tier | What the partner does | What the partner gets | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Introducer** | Shares a tracked link/code and one communication (newsletter, post, member evening slot) | Member benefit (value-add); donation or credit to partner cash box on incremental bookings, 3% [ASSUMPTION] | Wine clubs, member orgs, podcasts |
| **2. Host partner** | Names an ambassador (club pro, sommelier, chair) who co-hosts a dated departure and fronts two communications | Organiser place at 1 free per 15 paying travellers or equivalent credit (about 6% of revenue at list price, about 5% at cost [ASSUMPTION: cost about 80% of price]) plus 2-3% contribution, total at most 8% of booking value | Golf and wine hosted departures |

Rule clarification: the organiser place counts only new-to-CRM paying travellers (principle 3). With departures of 8-16 people this threshold is rarely met, so in practice Tier 2 earns the 2-3% contribution and a pro-rata credit. If the CEO prefers that all partner-code travellers count toward the organiser place, margin safety falls (see open question in section 16).
| **3. Private group organiser** | Brings a defined group (6-14) for own dates | Group-leader benefit within the 8% cap, tailored quote, priority handling | Golf societies, friends' groups, corporate |

### 6.2 Standard one-page agreement (legal review by 31 Mar 2027)

Scope, tracked code, benefit formula and cap, payment trigger (after final payment and departure), no exclusivity unless explicitly priced, data handling clause (section 12), brand-use rules, claims rules (section 14), 12-month term, exit on 30 days. Concentration guardrail: no single partner above 10% of total company turnover (G5; trivially met at these volumes) plus an internal early warning: no partner above 35% of affinity-channel turnover from Gate 2 onward [ASSUMPTION], because with 3 pilots one partner will exceed 10% of the channel by construction.

### 6.3 Tracking and attribution

- Each partner gets a unique URL with UTM parameters and a code; CRM source field "Affinity: [partner]"; "how did you hear" field mandatory (workstream 16).
- Incrementality test: match every partner booking against the CRM at enquiry date (24-month look-back); new-to-CRM customers are incremental. Matched-pair check: for the first pilot, compare enquiry volume from partner members with a similar unexposed list.
- Monthly partner report: reach, enquiries, quotes, bookings, margin, benefits owed.

### 6.4 Margin rules

- Group quote priced at least 23% before partner benefits, 15% floor after (G1/G5). If order-book margin falls below 19% (Gate 1 test) the benefit cap drops from 8% to 5% automatically.
- No cash discounts; value-adds only, funded from supplier rebates; total promotions and value-adds stay within 2% of turnover (G2).
- Hosted departures: allocation or rates held for at least 60% of planned volume with release dates of 60-120 days (strategy 13.7 and G7) before publishing; prepaid commitments stay within the SEK 4 M prepaid cap (A24) and the cash plan. (v1 said 80% of seats, which contradicted the 60% rule and raised at-risk supply.)
- FX clause and ZAR-linked price adjustment apply to all fixed-price departures (strategy 11.3).
