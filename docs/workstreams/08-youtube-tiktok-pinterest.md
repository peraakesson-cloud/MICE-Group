## 3. Approach: principles

1. **Proof before reach.** The strongest raw material is already public: named advisers, Google reviews, Swedish-speaking guides, customer stories, celebrity-led trips (R08 4.3). Video turns them into trust. We film people before landscapes.
2. **One shoot, many cuts.** Every long film is planned as 1 long (6-12 min), 3 to 5 shorts (20-60 s, 9:16) and 5 stills. The same shorts feed YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook, TikTok and Pinterest video pins. Marginal cost of TikTok and Pinterest organic is therefore close to zero.
3. **Incremental, not attributed.** YouTube view-through numbers are vanity. Decisions use holdouts, branded-search lift and cost per qualified enquiry (strategy 11.2). Attributed ROAS is a sanity check only.
4. **Premium shown, not argued** (strategy 6). Copy sells the adviser, the guide, the flexibility and the travel guarantee. No discount-led creative; early-booking value-adds only after FX policy and the ZAR clause are in the terms (strategy 11.3).
5. **Never promise what is not costed.** "Jourtelefon under resan", not "24/7 lokalt". No superlatives such as "Sveriges största" or "bäst". No Trustpilot rating in video while it is below 4.5 (R03 shows 2.7 on 8 reviews).
6. **Capacity gate.** No budget increase while the response-time SLA is missed (strategy 11.2).
7. **Start small, stage-gated.** Before Gate 1 (31 Mar 2027) total paid media is capped at SEK 0.5 M, brand and high-intent only. YouTube prospecting, TikTok and Pinterest paid are therefore Tranche B and wait for Gate 1.

## 7. TikTok plan

Test and learn, not scale. The core buyer is not on TikTok (5-12% reach in 1950s-1980s cohorts). The two reasons to be there: (a) the 1990s cohort at 27% and the 2000s at 69% for honeymoon, first long-haul and family influencers, and (b) organic discovery that costs almost nothing because it reuses Shorts and Reels. Aftonbladet Resa covers TikTok-driven destination trends among the young (https://www.aftonbladet.se/resa/a/W0W3pr/resmal-som-trendar-pa-tiktok-paverkar-ungas-resande), which supports the test but not a revenue claim.

### 7.2 Organic

- Cadence: 2 posts a week from Dec 2026, all repurposed shorts, trimmed for 9:16 and native captions; no cross-posts with watermarks. Supply check: the plan produces about 80 unique shorts in 2027 (section 9.2) for YouTube Shorts, Reels, Facebook, TikTok and Pinterest; 2 posts a week is about 100 a year, so about 20 posts must be re-cuts of older shorts with a new hook. Do not raise cadence without raising the shorts budget.
- Content pillars: "Vad ingen berättar om Sydafrika" (practical myth-busting), "En dag med guiden", "Smekmånad i Sydafrika", "Sydafrika med barn", no "billigt" or budget-hack content (positioning is not lowest price).

(Seychelles link to existing site offers in R08 4.4).
- Two creator tests: 1 honeymoon creator and 1 family creator, both 10-100k followers, tier D in R08 (indicative fee SEK 10-40k plus trip), paid on booking only, 5-8% of booked value, with the trip in kind (strategy 11.3: no fixed-fee placements without proof). ## 8. Pinterest plan

A slow-burn inspiration channel that behaves partly like search (saved pins resurface for months) and links to the itinerary and best-time pages. Evidence is weak for Sweden: 18% annual reach, 2% daily, 13-17% in the 1950s-1980s cohorts and 26% in the 1990s (R08). Sourced ad benchmarks (cross-industry, low confidence): CPC USD 0.83-1.50, CPM USD 3-10, CTR 0.50%, e-commerce CPA USD 5-30 (https://www.benchmarketing.org/benchmarks/pinterest-ads, https://www.mbadv.agency/pinterest-ads/pinterest-ads-vs-other-social-media-ads). Pinterest ad availability and targeting options in Sweden must be verified when opening the ad account [GYT9].

### 8.2 Organic

- Business account, claimed website (sydafrikaresor.se), rich pins enabled.
- Eight boards (Swedish): "Rundresa Sydafrika", "Safari - vad du kan se", "Kapstaden och vinlandet", "Garden Route", "Sydafrika med barn", "Smekmånad Sydafrika", "Bästa tid att resa", "Packlista Sydafrika".

- Cadence: 2 new pins a week in Dec 2026, 3 a week from Jan 2027 (about 150 a year, derived from the 5 stills per long film, shorts and itinerary-page images with Swedish text overlays; templates are in the 12,000 tools line), via a scheduler (Buffer or the native scheduler), produced in one monthly batch. Raise only if the test passes.
- Tracking: UTM-tagged links; GA4 landing-page report by source "pinterest".

### 9.3 Phasing and rules

- Before 31 Mar 2027 only YT-A1 remarketing spends (SEK 25k), counted inside the strategy's SEK 0.4 M Dec-Mar Tranche A allocation. TikTok and Pinterest paid are zero until Gate 1.
- 50-60% of annual paid spend in Nov-Mar, secondary push Sep-Oct (strategy 11.3, [A16]). The channel calendar in section 11 follows that.
- Budget moves only at the monthly performance review and never while the response-time SLA is missed.
- Marginal marketing cost cap of SEK 20k per marketing-created booking (strategy 11.2): measured on YouTube media plus a usage-weighted share of production. Honest read: 2027 channel cost of about SEK 382k against roughly 3-8 incremental bookings (one third of 10-24) is far above SEK 20k per booking. Strategy G4 already treats 2027 as an investment year (about SEK 36k); this channel is a drag on it, which is why production is shared with SEO, Meta and email and why only the media line is tested against the cap at Gate 2, with the full cost reported alongside.
