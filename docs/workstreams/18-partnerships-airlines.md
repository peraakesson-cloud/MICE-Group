## 1. Purpose and how this workstream earns its place

This workstream does two different jobs, and they must not be confused.

1. **Secure supply (defensive, binding).** The strategy says people, supply timing and trust proof are the binding constraints, not media. Flagship lodge beds, peak-week air seats and December DMC capacity are the scarce items (R05, RT-S). Contracts with hotel/lodge groups, DMCs and two airlines decide how much of the 48 MSEK (2027) and 64.4 MSEK (2028) can actually be sold at 20% gross margin.
2. **Add incremental demand at low cost (offensive, gated).** SAT North Europe Hub co-funding, airline co-op marketing, affinity partners (seniors, golf, wine) and independent agents. This is lever L8 (+1.0 MSEK 2027, +3.5 MSEK by year 2, net of overlap) and supports L5 and L7. GL4 (wholesale/land-only, +3.6) and GL2 (MICE/bleisure, +3.0) are gap levers with their own gates and are only prepared here, not funded.

Air margin is probably not the prize (judgement, not a sourced fact). Published "from" fares of roughly SEK 6,000-6,800 on sampled late-2026 dates (Turkish ARN-JNB from SEK 6,004, ARN-CPT from SEK 6,502; KLM ARN-CPT from SEK 6,723; R12 [S7], R04) are lowest-fare snapshots, not peak-week or group prices. At those levels air is roughly 15-19% of a SEK 36-45k flights-included ladder product (about 20-25% at the assumed SEK 9,000 peak group fare in 5.5), and whether the client's "from" prices include flights is unknown (strategy 3.3). The prize from airlines is therefore trade fares, peak-week seat access, disruption handling and co-op marketing money.

## 2. Principles and guardrails (inherited from the strategy)

1. **Contract the supply, then spend on demand.** No partner marketing launches for a period until the supply for it is held (Gate 0 rule). Co-marketing is always aimed at departures where allocation is held.
2. **Options before commitments.** Until Gate 1 (31 Mar 2027) no air block seats and no prepaid lodge deposits beyond the CFO cap. Total prepaid/at-risk supply is capped at **SEK 4 M at any one time** [A24 in strategy]. Flagship allocations may carry deposits and 90-120 day release; mid-tier 60-90 day release (strategy 13.2).
3. **Do not compete on lowest price.** Positioning is "Sydafrikaspecialisten sedan 2003", about 15-20% above the group-with-flights cluster, premium shown through inclusions, guide, flexibility and support. Partner offers use value-adds (extra night, guide, upgrade), never headline discounts.
4. **Partner economics (G5).** Commission 8-12% from margin; margin after commission at least 15%; single partner under 10% of turnover; net-price floor equals direct public price.
5. **Package-travel compliance by design.** Partner-sold packages must carry correct insolvency protection ("resegaranti") information and the organiser stays liable. Legal review of the agent agreement before any agent sells (R09; EU Directive 2026/1024 re-engineering before transposition, about Sept 2028 [VERIFY] per strategy).
6. **No 24/7 local-presence claim.** Until costed and staffed (decision by 31 Jan 2027), partner copy says "jourtelefon under resan". This workstream feeds that decision through DMC SLA terms (section 6).
7. **Concentration limits.** No more than 40% of any year's flagship-lodge volume with one lodge group, and no DMC above 50% of ground volume [ASSUMPTION: guardrails to prevent single-point failure; CFO/Head of Product to confirm].

Route to market per R05: DMC net rate for ground (core 2027), direct contracts for the top 10-15 lodges and Cape Town hotels, bedbanks for long-tail city hotels only (no safari peak allocation there).

### 4.2 What we offer in return (the negotiating currency)

We are a small buyer in a market where flagship beds are scarce. We win allocation through certainty, not price:
- **Forward visibility:** a rolling 18-month forecast by month, shared quarterly, once Gate 0 data exists.
- **Fixed departure dates:** published guided departures (about 41 in 2028 core) give lodges fill in shoulder weeks.
- **Shoulder and green-season volume** (L10): Apr-May and Sep-Nov volume the lodge struggles to fill, bundled with peak requests ("a peak week comes with a shoulder week").
- **Payment reliability:** short payment terms and a deposit within the CFO cap in exchange for flagship peak weeks.
- **Quality of guests and reviews:** Swedish, longer stays (14-day average overseas stay, Stats SA via R12 [S2]; this is a market-wide figure, the plan's own unit conversions use 12 nights, strategy A23), review programme to Trustpilot/Google. Do not quote Trustpilot to lodges until the score is rebuilt (2.7 on 8 reviews today, R03).
- **Content:** Swedish-language co-marketing, hosted departures with Swedish personalities, FAM/press trips.

### 4.3 Supply-readiness gate (inherited, applied here)

A new product (malaria-free, KZN, Eastern Cape, family, 55+) launches only when: allocation or rates contracted for at least 60% of planned volume, age policy checked, DMC and guide confirmed, margin at least 18% (15% post-commission for partner sales), launch cap equal to contracted supply (strategy 13.7).

### 4.4 Allocation register (the Gate 0 deliverable)

Owner: Head of Product. Format: one sheet per lodge/hotel with month, rooms held, rate, release date, deposit, contact, status (asked / offered / contracted / released), plus total "SEK-equivalent of 2027 turnover supportable". Gate 0 rule: if held supply is below 48 MSEK equivalent, 2027 is recut downward and marketing aims at shoulder months and alternatives (KZN, Eastern Cape, mid-tier Garden Route). Conversion basis [ASSUMPTION]: 12 nights average per traveller, 2 travellers per room-night, SEK 45k revenue per traveller (strategy A1); the register converts held room-nights to supportable turnover. Count only nights actually held under allocation or a signed DMC capacity letter; nights expected from bedbanks or unconfirmed DMC net rates are shown separately and not counted toward the Gate 0 test. Lodges are only part of each itinerary (city, Garden Route and transfer nights are sourced elsewhere), so the register must also show the binding scarce item per departure month (usually flagship or mid-tier safari beds) rather than a single total.

### 5.4 Airline scorecard (decide by 20 Jan 2027, sign by 28 Feb 2027)

### 5.5 Block-seat sizing (illustrative, [ASSUMPTION])

Guided departures: 12 travellers each (strategy 13.1). Pilot options: 3-4 departures x 12 = 36-48 seats plus 10% buffer = 40-53 seats.
