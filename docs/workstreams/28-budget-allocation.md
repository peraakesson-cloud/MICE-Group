## 1. Purpose and objectives

The strategy fixes the envelope (SEK 0.30 M in Q4 2026, 3.6 M in 2027, 4.6 M in 2028; 7.5% and 7.1% of core turnover) and splits it into committed and gate-released money. The workstream plans then each asked for a share. This document does four jobs:

1. **Turns the envelope into one monthly ledger by channel** for Nov 2026 to Dec 2028 that sums exactly to the strategy lines (sections 4 to 6).
2. **Settles the overlapping claims** that the sixteen workstream plans make on the same lines and shows what had to be trimmed (section 7).
3. **Sets scenario budgets** (base, downside, upside, shock) so the CEO knows the spend at each turnover outcome (section 9).
4. **Fixes the rules** for moving money: who may move what, which metric triggers which move, what is cut first, what is never cut (section 10).

Objectives and KPIs this plan is accountable for (details in section 12):

| # | Objective | Target |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | Spend exactly the approved envelope, by month, by line | Annual variance within +/-2% of released budget; monthly variance within +/-15% per line (section 11) |
| O2 | Marketing efficiency inside strategy guardrail G4 | Total marketing at most 7.5% of turnover; blended at most SEK 8,000 per booking; marginal at most SEK 20,000 per marketing-created booking (2027 investment year accepted at about SEK 36,000 and falling) |

## 3. Approach: nine allocation principles

1. **Envelope first, channel second.** The line totals in the strategy are fixed; this plan only decides timing and the channel mix inside them. Anything that raises an annual total is a CEO decision (section 10).
2. **Commit only what is no-regret; gate the rest.** 2027 committed money is 2.40 MSEK of 3.6 in this plan (equal to strategy 11.1); the gated 1.20 MSEK is released by gate and by evidence, not by calendar.
3. **Defend before you prospect.** Brand and high-intent search, retargeting and CRM follow-up serve flow that already exists and are funded before prospecting (section 10.3 ladder).
4. **Time follows the booking curve and the lead time.** About 50-60% of paid goes into Nov-Mar [A16]. Money spent after March mostly buys 2028 departures (section 8), so the 2028 plan is built by 2027 H2 spend.
5. **Incremental ROAS, not attributed ROAS, binds Tranche B.** Attributed 8x is a sanity check only.
6. **Capacity and supply gate every increase.** Sales SLA, held allocation and margin on new bookings are conditions, not footnotes (strategy G8, G9, G1).
7. **Unspent is released, not hoarded.** No spending to the cap for its own sake; no carry-forward except one-month roll of Tranche A early-booking money.
8. **One ledger.** Every spend line carries four tags: line, channel, tranche (A/B/other) and departure year. Without the tags the incremental and per-booking rules cannot be applied.
9. **Reconcile, do not stack.** Workstream budgets are claims on shared lines. The ledger records the settled amount, and the owner of each workstream signs it.

## 8. Seasonality and departure-year logic

**Booking-date seasonality [ASSUMPTION A16]:** main peak Jan-Mar, secondary peak Sep-Nov; cheapest Meta months Nov-Feb (research 07: Superads Sweden cross-industry Meta CPC USD 0.64 in Feb to 1.71 in May, Nov-Feb USD 0.64-0.83, https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpc-cost-per-click/sweden; travel-vertical traffic-campaign CPC USD 0.42-0.51 comes from US LocalIQ/WordStream data, https://localiq.com/blog/facebook-advertising-benchmarks/, and is not a Swedish figure). No Swedish long-haul seasonality source exists; the plan therefore front-loads only up to the 50-60% Nov-Mar share, and the first quarterly rebase (section 11) replaces it with the company's own curve.

**Spend versus departure year [ASSUMPTION: share of each period's spend that books departures in the same calendar year].** The 2027 envelope plus Q4 2026 is SEK 3.9 M; applying 90% (Q4 2026), 85% (Q1), 60% (Q2), 25% (Q3) and 5% (Q4) gives about SEK 1.75 M (45%) aimed at 2027 departures and about SEK 2.15 M (55%) aimed at 2028 departures. Consequences:
- The 2027 turnover target of 48 MSEK is largely decided by the Q4 2026 and Q1 2027 spend (about SEK 1.17 M) and by the existing flow; this is why the pre-Gate-1 cap and the 1 Dec start matter more than any later optimisation.
- The 2028 target of 64.4 MSEK, including 19 MSEK of 2028 departures booked by 31 Dec 2027, is financed by the Q2-Q4 2027 envelope. A Gate 2 or Gate 3 decision to cut spend in H2 2027 cuts 2028 turnover with a 6-12 month lag; this is the main reason the cut ladder in section 10.4 protects Tranche A and SEO first.
- Order-book timing in the strategy (24 MSEK by 31 Mar 2027, 36 MSEK by 30 Jun 2027) means 50% and 75% of the 2027 target is booked when only 30% and 51% of the Q4 2026 to Dec 2027 money has been spent (cumulative Q4 2026 to Q1 2027: SEK 1.17 M of 3.9 M = 30%; to Q2: SEK 1.97 M = 51%). The plan therefore tests lower spend against the order book, not the reverse.

### 9.3 Economics sanity check (strategy 12; no new data)

At the planning gross margin of 20% [A7] the programme nets about -1.7 MSEK in 2027 and -2.2 MSEK in 2028; break-even margin is about 23.5%. The budget in this plan is inside those numbers (only the part of marketing above the assumed 2.6 MSEK baseline, +1.0 in 2027 and +2.0 in 2028 [A18], is inside the 3.3 and 7.1 MSEK incremental cost; if the real baseline is not 2.6 the incremental cost and the loss change one for one). Therefore: (a) the downside budget does not rescue the economics by itself because turnover also falls; (b) the main protection is the stop-loss and the gate structure; (c) allocation changes that shift money from low-margin partner volume to direct bookings change the margin, not just the cost ratio, so every reallocation proposal must show the effect on gross profit, not only turnover.

### 9.4 The conditional stretch tranche (2028, up to SEK 1,000k)

Release conditions: Tranche B holdout iROAS of 5x or more for two consecutive quarters; response SLA met; margin on new bookings at least 19%; supply held for the promoted departures. Earliest release: after the Q4 2027 and Q1 2028 reads, i.e. May 2028. Planned phasing if released [ASSUMPTION]: Jun 60, Jul 60, Aug 100, Sep 150, Oct 170, Nov 240, Dec 220. At the 5x floor the full tranche returns at least SEK 5.0 M of incremental turnover (SEK 1.0 M of gross profit at 20% margin, which only repays the media), and most of it books 2029 departures. It therefore adds to the 2029 order book far more than to 2028 turnover, so it cannot be counted on to close the 64.4 to 80 MSEK gap by itself.

### 10.2 Metric triggers (monitored weekly, acted on monthly unless stated)

| Tranche B holdout iROAS | Under 4x after 8 weeks: pause the campaign group. 4x to 5x: hold and optimise. 5x or more: scale by at most 20% a week, up to the gated ceiling and while capacity is within SLA | Strategy 11.2 and workstreams 06/07 |
| Cost per qualified enquiry, Tranche A | Over SEK 1,375 (SEK 1,100 planning value for Search plus 25%) for four weeks [ASSUMPTION] | Move up to 20% to the best A campaign or cut the worst ad group; review keywords and landing page |

| Marginal marketing cost per marketing-created booking | Over SEK 20,000 from 2028 (2027 accepted at about 36,000 only as an investment year) for two consecutive months | Freeze gated lines of the lever concerned (L3, L5, L8) |

### 10.3 Where extra money goes first (add ladder, highest evidence first)

1. Tranche A brand and high-intent search that is budget-limited (lost impression share), only with the SLA met.
2. Retargeting and abandoned-enquiry recovery (workstream 09 planning iROAS 5.6x; weak evidence, to be proved).
3. Speed-to-lead and follow-up capacity (lever L2): not media, but the cheapest conversion gain; bought with salary and CRM lines, so if budget is added anywhere it should be tested here first.
4. SEO content refresh and Tier B guides (the lag is 4-9 months, so extra money in Q1-Q2 pays on 2028 departures).
5. Partner pilots with a signed partner and verified tracking (WS19 Gate 1 condition).
6. Tranche B groups that have passed 5x.
7. Creators and PR with commission-only structure.
8. Tests (TikTok, Pinterest, programmatic).

### 10.4 Where cuts come from (cut ladder, weakest evidence first)

1. Tests line (TikTok, Pinterest, programmatic, affiliate fees).
2. Tranche B campaign groups that fail the holdout rule.
3. Unbooked fair stands and second-city fairs; extra evenings.
4. Creator fees and PR events (evidence grade C).
5. Affinity expansion and SAT joint campaign.
6. Tranche A non-brand groups with falling efficiency.
7. SEO content volume (cut last among growth lines because of lag).

**Never cut:** measurement and holdouts (without them the whole gating logic fails), compliance and legal review, the review programme (Trustpilot), speed-to-lead tooling, brand-protection search, the SEO technical retainer. These total at least SEK 0.41 M of the 2027 committed money (measurement 200, review programme 100, SEO technical retainer 90, compliance 20), plus brand-protection search and speed-to-lead tooling, which cannot be sized until account data and the tooling quote exist [ASSUMPTION; the figure 0.45 M in the first draft had no basis for the brand-search part and was withdrawn].

### 10.5 Rollover and end-of-year rules

- Unspent monthly money returns to the council; it is not carried forward, except the one-month Tranche A early-booking roll.
- A gated tranche not released by 31 Dec of its year lapses; it is not added to the next year.
- Year-end: remaining 2027 committed money is reviewed at Gate 3; Q4 2027 spend that books 2028 departures is allowed to run as planned.
- Scale rule for increases: no line increases by more than 20% of its monthly plan in a single month without a council decision.
