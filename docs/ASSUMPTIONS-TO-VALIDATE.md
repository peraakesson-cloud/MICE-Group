# Assumptions to validate (consolidated register)

Status: v1, 3 Nov 2026 | Owner: Chief of Staff for the CEO | Source: every assumption register in `/docs/00-strategy.md` (section 18) and workstreams 01-36
Purpose: we have no internal company data. Every figure about the company's own performance in this plan is a placeholder. This file tells the CEO and function heads which data replaces which assumption, who supplies it and by when. Part 1 is the short list that matters. Part 2 lists the cross-cutting questions that appear in several plans. The appendices reproduce every register item (about 600) so nothing is lost; IDs are those used in each source file.

How to use it
1. Work Part 1 first. Twelve data packs replace or settle most of the high-impact assumptions. The CFO and Head of Sales own most of them and the deadline is Gate 0 (15 Dec 2026); the full pack goes to the CEO on 30 Nov, with a quick set on 13 Nov.
2. When a pack arrives, re-run the bridge and the finance model (`/docs/workstreams/27-finance-model.csv`) and mark the affected appendix rows as replaced (add the date and source in the Replace-with column of the source workstream file).
3. Items marked VERIFY in the plans (legal and regulatory statements built on secondary sources) are not data questions. They go to counsel (workstream 31) and must be cleared before any claim is used in customer-facing copy.
