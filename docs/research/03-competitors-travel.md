# 03 - Swedish Competitors Selling South Africa Trips

Research date: 2026-11-02. Method: live scrape of public competitor websites and review platforms. No internal client data used. Every number below is a public "from" price or rating seen on the cited URL on the research date; prices change daily and are mostly "from" prices (cheapest departure), not averages. Anything not sourced is marked [ASSUMPTION].

## 1. Executive summary

- The Swedish South Africa market is crowded and tiered: (a) mass-market holiday giants (Ving, TUI, Apollo) selling flight+hotel or one entry-level round trip; (b) group-tour/themed operators with Swedish tour leaders (Albatros Travel, Temaresor, Tourcompass); (c) boutique/tailor-made specialists (MyChoice Sydafrika, Afrikakompaniet, Afrikaresor.se, African Tours & Safaris, Safariresor.se); and (d) sydafrikaresor.se itself, which sits between (b) and (c) with both guided round trips and tailor-made "reseforslag".
- sydafrikaresor.se claims the strongest scale position in the niche ("Skandinaviens storsta arrangor av resor till Sydafrika och sodra Afrika", "Sveriges storsta researrangor till sodra Afrika", operating since 2003, Svenska Resebyraforeningen member since 2010). That claim is the client's own and unverified here [ASSUMPTION: treat as marketing claim until sales data confirms].
- Biggest visible competitive gap for the client: online reputation. Sydafrikaresor has only 8 Trustpilot reviews (2.7/5, profile shows no recent invitation history, two recent negative reviews about service responsiveness and a tour guide), while MyChoice Sydafrika shows 5.0/5 on Reco (236 reviews) and 4.7/5 on Trustpilot (32), and Safariresor.se shows 4.8/5 on Trustpilot (about 622-623 reviews). Social proof is the cheapest lever to pull and the most damaging weakness at 2x volume.
- Price positioning: sydafrikaresor's guided round trips start roughly SEK 44-70k pp, tailor-made proposals SEK 17.9k-95k pp. Direct specialist comparables for a Cape Town + Kruger trip land at SEK 26-43k pp. The client is not the cheapest; it must win on specialisation, trust and tailoring, not price.
- Doubling revenue is not achievable by share-taking from specialists alone (they are small); it requires market growth capture (demand generation, SEO/ads, new segments) plus conversion and average-order-value work. See section 7.

## 3. Competitor profiles

### 3.1 Tier A: Mass-market holiday companies

**Ving** - https://www.ving.se/sydafrika
- Offer: Cape Town hotels (20 listed) plus a "Nyhet" 11-day round trip with safari "Rundresa och safari i Sydafrika" from SEK 31,350. Promotion: SEK 2,000 off per booking on all round trips, code NYHET, valid for bookings 21 Oct - 8 Nov 2026, departures through 31 Mar 2027 (terms page on the same URL).
- Positioning: broad "minne for livet" copy, content marketing by travel expert ("10 dagar i Sydafrika"), weather/visa/vaccination info. Ving only flies to Cape Town per its own text ("Vings resor till Sydafrika gar till Kapstaden").
- Strength: brand reach, advertising budget, trusted booking brand. Weakness: shallow product (one round trip, mainly Cape Town), little tailoring.
- Signal: Ving launched round trips as a new product; a large player is actively testing the round-trip segment and using discount codes.

**TUI** - https://www.tui.se/resa/sydafrika/
- Offer: charter/package flight+hotel to Cape Town, 7 nights. "Pris/person fran" SEK 11,337; sample 2-adult totals SEK 22,674 (no meals, ARN departure, 2026) and SEK 22,734-26,918 for 2027 departures.
- Positioning: sun/city/wine; "Kombinera med vinprovning och safari" but safari is not a packaged product on this page.
- Strength: lowest entry price, flights included, volume. Weakness: no real safari/round-trip depth.
- Implication: sets the "Cape Town for ~SEK 11k pp" price anchor in search results; the client cannot compete here and should not try.

**Apollo** - https://www.apollo.se/sydafrika
- Offer: "charterresor, endast flyg eller paketresor med reguljarflyg"; Kruger/Big Five described in destination copy. No "from" price extracted on the date. 
- Strength: mass reach, flight+hotel flexibility. Weakness: not a specialist.

### 3.2 Tier B: Group-tour operators with Swedish tour leader

**Albatros Travel** - https://www.albatros.se/resor/fargstarka-sydafrika-safari-och-kapstaden
- Offer: "Fargstarka Sydafrika - Safari och Kapstaden", 12 days, Swedish tour leader, flights ARN-JNB / CPT-ARN included, own camps (Mantobeni, Zebra Country Lodge), Kruger/Manyeleti safari, Panorama Route, Soweto/Pretoria, Cape Town, wine tasting. Price SEK 36,998-38,998 (6 dates Jan 2027-Nov 2027); single supplement SEK 7,998; cancellation insurance 7% of price; tips about ZAR 1,170 pp; minimum 16 participants; children under 12 not allowed.
- Reviews: 4.6/5 on 40 reviews on-page.
- Add-ons: Mauritius extension from SEK 18,998; Victoria Falls from 13,998; Cape Town extension from 5,998.
- Strength: group price with flights and Swedish leader at about SEK 37k pp - the most direct price anchor against client's SEK 44-50k guided trips. Weakness: fixed dates, 16-pax minimum, no children, little tailoring.

**Temaresor (TEMA)** - https://www.temaresor.se/resor/afrika/sydafrika/sydafrikas-basta-safari-valar-och-vingardar
- Offer: 14-day "Sydafrikas basta - Safari, valar och vingardar", group 12-24, Swedish TEMA leader. Dates 18 Jul 2027 (SEK 60,890 Stockholm / 61,750 Gothenburg / 62,950 Copenhagen) and 26 Sep 2027 (SEK 59,650); single room SEK 67,550-71,550. A 17-day "Fran Boerland till Kap" is listed from SEK 63,990.
- Strength: premium theme/food-and-wine proposition, multi-city departure (STO/GOT/CPH). Weakness: group-only, high price, few South Africa departures.
- Implication: validates SEK 60k+ pp as sellable for cultural/gastronomic group trips - a ceiling reference for the client's higher-yield products.

**Tourcompass** - https://www.tourcompass.se/sydafrika/resor/safari-i-sydafrika-och-kapstaden.htm
- Offer: 13-day "Safari i Sydafrika och Kapstaden" with 6 nights at Kruger Gate Hotel (half board), Panorama Route, Cape Town. Price SEK 36,095-51,095 pp across 2027-2028 dates (SEK 37,595-42,695 most of 2027; peak Christmas/New Year SEK 49,495-51,095; cheap shoulder window April-May 2028 at SEK 36,095), flights included. "Boka helt riskfritt och med prisgaranti." Shortest round trip 9 days.
- Strength: transparent weekly departures with published dynamic prices for 2027-2028, risk-free/price-guarantee messaging. Weakness: generic product, standard hotel safari.
- Implication: competitor already publishes weekly departures through Nov 2028 - they are planning and selling two seasons ahead.

### 3.3 Tier C: Specialist / tailor-made competitors

**MyChoice Sydafrika** - https://mychoicesydafrika.se/resor-sydafrika/kapstaden-safari-10-dagar/
- Offer: 10-day Cape Town + malaria-free Big Five safari near Mossel Bay "fran 25,900 kr per person" (own-hand trip, flights not clearly included [ASSUMPTION: land-only, since page links a separate "flyg till Sydafrika" page]). Large activity catalogue (shark cage, whale watching, townships, Kirstenbosch concerts, etc.).
- Positioning: local company with HQ in Cape Town (MyChoice Travel (Pty) Ltd) and Swedish customer service office in Visby; "Prisgaranti & oppet kop", on-the-ground welcome meeting at the hotel, B2B "Foretagsresor" page (overlaps with MICE unit), English page.
- Proof: Reco 5.0/5 on 236 reviews (https://www.reco.se/mychoice-sydafrika); Trustpilot 4.7 on 32 (https://se.trustpilot.com/review/mychoicesydafrika.se). Review text emphasises named advisors, quick replies, rebooking help when a safari was cancelled by floods, and "didn't cost a krona more than booking ourselves."
- Strength: best-in-class social proof, on-the-ground presence, price-match and open-purchase promise. Weakness: small Swedish team [ASSUMPTION], so scaling is a constraint.
- This is the closest like-for-like competitor in trust and tailor-made positioning and the most credible threat to client's tailor-made funnel.

**Safariresor.se** - https://www.safariresor.se/upptack-kwazulu-natal

## 6. Strengths and weaknesses summary (relative to sydafrikaresor.se)

Competitor strengths to counter:
1. Review volume and score: Safariresor.se (about 623 at 4.8), MyChoice (236 at 5.0 on Reco), Afrikaresor (124 at 4.9) versus client Trustpilot 8 at 2.7.
2. Price transparency and risk reversal: Tourcompass weekly dates with price guarantee; MyChoice price guarantee/open purchase.
3. Flights-included group pricing at SEK 36-40k: Albatros, Tourcompass, African Tours.
4. Mass reach and discounting: Ving SEK 2,000 code campaign; TUI's SEK 11k entry price; both capture top-of-funnel search.
5. Qualifying lead forms with budget brackets: Safariresor.se, African Tours.

Competitor weaknesses to exploit:
1. Mass-market players have shallow South Africa products (mainly Cape Town).
2. Group operators are inflexible (fixed dates, minimum 16 pax, no children under 12 on Albatros).
3. Several specialists are very small or have thin reviews (African Tours 1 Trustpilot review).
4. Few competitors address families, golf, honeymoons and extensions (Mauritius, Seychelles, Victoria Falls) as systematically as the client does.

## 7. Implications for doubling revenue (SEK 40M to 80M in 24 months)

Arithmetic (illustrative, uses assumption of average revenue per booking; replace with actuals):
- [ASSUMPTION] Average order value of about SEK 70k (2 travellers at about SEK 35k pp) implies about 570 bookings per year today (SEK 40M / 70k) and about 1,140 per year needed at SEK 80M, i.e. about +570 bookings per year. If AOV rises 10% to SEK 77k by upsell, bookings needed at SEK 80M are about 1,040.
- The competitive set above suggests the market leader's niche is not saturated by one player, so growth must come from (1) more demand captured at the top of the funnel (the mass-market players hold most paid search and brand awareness), (2) better conversion of enquiries, and (3) higher value per booking (extensions, upgrades, golf, Rovos Rail, honeymoon).

Recommendations tied to competitor evidence:
1. Fix reputation first (0-3 months). Launch automated post-trip review requests to Trustpilot, Google and Reco; target 150+ Trustpilot reviews and 4.6+ rating by Q2 2027; respond to the two recent negative reviews publicly. Without this, paid traffic will leak to MyChoice and Safariresor.se on comparison. [Competitors show 124-623 reviews at 4.7-5.0.]
2. Differentiate on "largest + local" with proof: publish passenger counts, years, on-the-ground partners, ABTA/SRF membership, and a named-advisor model; consider a price-match or "open purchase" equivalent to neutralise MyChoice's headline guarantee.
3. Build a flights-included guided product ladder in the SEK 36-45k band with weekly/monthly published departures through 2028 to match Tourcompass/Albatros transparency, and a premium SEK 55-70k tier (TEMA-style food and wine, safari+whales) to capture upside.
4. Match funnel mechanics: budget-bracket quote form and "book a call with an expert" CTA (Safariresor.se model); response-time SLA (<2 working hours in peak) given the recent complaint about lack of response.
5. Own search terms the giants ignore: "Kruger safari", "Garden Route", "golf Sydafrika", "bröllopsresa Sydafrika", "familjeresa Sydafrika", "Sydafrika med barn", "bästa tid att resa" and long-tail combination trips; avoid bidding on pure "Kapstaden hotell" where TUI/Ving dominate.
6. Time the demand wave: Ving's NYHET campaign (to 8 Nov 2026) and competitors' 2027-2028 dates show the main selling season for 2027 departures is now; Swedish winter (Dec-Mar) is peak for South Africa summer; client should run an early-booking campaign Nov 2026-Feb 2027.
7. Use sister-company assets: cross-sell to Soderhavsresor customers and use the MICE unit for incentive/corporate groups (MyChoice and others offer "Foretagsresor", so the client can differentiate with the MICE brand).
