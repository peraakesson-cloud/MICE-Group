# 02 - Swedish South Africa Traveller Segments, Demographics, Motivations and Decision Journey

Research agent output | Date: 2026-11-02 | Client: sydafrikaresor.se (goal: 40 -> 80 MSEK in 24 months)
Convention: facts are sourced with URL. Anything not directly sourced is tagged **[ASSUMPTION]** with rationale. No internal company data was available; nothing here describes the client's actual customers or sales.

---

## 1. Executive summary

- The addressable pool is small and concentrated. South African Tourism (SAT) records **27,625 Swedish tourist arrivals in 2025 (+12.5% vs 24,562 in 2024)**, the largest Nordic market ahead of Denmark (19,547), Norway (14,632) and Finland (6,552). Source: SAT Tourism Performance Report 2025, https://live.southafrica.net/media/308701/sat-2025-annual-international-tourism-performance-report-1.pdf?downloadId=436981
- Doubling revenue by volume alone would require the client to capture a much larger share of a market growing ~10-12%/yr (see section 7 for the arithmetic, all flagged as assumption-based). Revenue per traveller (ticket size, mix, upsell) and share gain from competitors must both carry the plan.
- Demand tailwinds are real: Sweden-origin bookings to South Africa are cited at +37% in a Swedish trend report; SA overall hit a record 10.5M tourists in 2025 (+17.7%); Swedes are booking more long-haul and are turning away from the USA.
- Segment evidence from Swedish operators points to six repeatable products: safari+Cape Town "classic", honeymoon, family, golf, food and wine, and self-drive/road trip, with 55+ as a cross-cutting age cohort rather than a product. Adventure is the thinnest-evidenced segment.
- The biggest gaps are internal: the client's own customer mix by segment, age, party size, lead time, channel and margin. Section 8 lists unknowns that must be replaced with CRM data before budgets are locked.

### Swedish outbound demand signals
- Swedish bookings for autumn/winter 2025/26 were up 11% YoY; one third planned an autumn trip and one quarter a winter trip (Ticket/Sifo, via Matochresebloggen). Same source reports South Africa growth of +37% and calls it a "bubbler" for 2026; Japan +176% (new direct Arlanda-Tokyo flights); USA down almost 30% with 64% of Swedes saying they are less keen to travel there. https://www.matochresebloggen.se/resetrender-2026-sa-vill-svenskarna-resa-nasta-ar/ (secondary blog source; underlying Ticket/Sifo data not verified.)
- Ving's 2026 outlook: strongest trend is search for recovery/relaxation and nature. https://via.tt.se/pressmeddelande/4201489/sa-reser-vi-under-2026?publisherId=1726617&lang=sv
- Visit Sweden (domestic-focused but cross-applicable): Swedes plan via Google, map services and social media; many decide late; one in three spent more than SEK 20,000 on their 2025 summer holiday; when costs bite, travellers trade down on accommodation or shift off-peak. https://corporate.visitsweden.com/kunskap/malgrupp/sverige/hur-tanker-svenska-resenarer-om-sitt-resande-nu/

Implication: the macro tailwind (USA avoidance, nature/long-haul appetite, SA growth) supports growth but also invites competitor spending on the same keywords. Price sensitivity is a risk for a SEK 30-60k+ per-person product.

### Seasonality (sourced + inference)
- Overseas arrivals to SA peak in Q1 and Q4 (warm season; Stats SA). Golf operators recommend Sept-April (African Tours). Swedish winter (Nov-Mar) is SA summer, so Swedes get "winter sun"; Jun-Oct is dry-season safari peak (Kruger game viewing) [ASSUMPTION from general knowledge, no source fetched].
- Implication: demand-gen must lead travel by 6-12 months; a year-round pipeline needs separate stories for safari-season (May-Oct) and Cape summer (Nov-Mar).

## 6. Implications for doubling revenue (40 -> 80 MSEK)

1. Treat it as three levers: (a) more first-time travellers (honeymoon, classic, family), (b) higher revenue per booking (55+, golf, wine, premium, add-on nights, combos with Mauritius/Mozambique/Victoria Falls/Botswana), (c) repeat and referral (post-trip programs, anniversary trips, friends' groups). Hypothesis: split the +40 MSEK roughly 50/30/20 [ASSUMPTION - to be replaced by CRM analysis].
2. Prioritise segments with fixed or planned dates and referral loops: honeymoon (wedding calendar), golf (clubs, groups), 55+ (flexible, high ARPU). Use family and self-drive as volume complements rather than the lead.
3. Product gaps visible from competitor content: child-friendly lodges, 55+ small-group Swedish-guided tours, adventure/active, Garden Route self-drive packages with guaranteed support, and off-season (Apr-Aug) value products. Validate against the client's current range before building.
4. Trust assets are a conversion lever more than a nice-to-have: reviews (Reco), named specialists, local-office/guide proof, transparent "from" and "what is included" pricing, and a safety page written with the Embassy facts.
5. Channel emphasis: Google (search/SEO and ads on intent keywords) + Meta/Instagram/YouTube for inspiration, plus email nurturing for the long consideration cycle. The hypothesis that Swedes decide late (Visit Sweden) argues for always-on remarketing rather than only seasonal bursts.
6. Share-of-market ceiling. Illustrative arithmetic, all inputs **[ASSUMPTION]**: if average revenue per traveller is ~SEK 40-45k, 40 MSEK is ~900-1,000 travellers and 80 MSEK ~1,800-2,000. Against SAT's 27,625 Swedish arrivals in 2025 that is ~3.3-3.6% share today and ~6.5-7.2% of 2025 volume at target (about 5-6% if Swedish arrivals grow ~10%/yr to ~33k by 2027). Increasing ARPU by 15-20% (premium mix, add-ons) would lower the required headcount to ~1,500-1,700. SAT arrivals include VFR and business, so the real leisure pool is smaller and the required share of leisure travellers is higher than shown. Replace with actual pax and revenue.
7. Because Swedish arrivals are ~43% below 2017, partnership with South African Tourism (Nordic marketing, trade familiarisation trips) and airlines (Qatar, Emirates, Turkish, KLM, Lufthansa, Swiss, etc. via one-stop; direct flights unverified) is worth testing as a market-expansion lever. Flight connectivity from Stockholm/Gothenburg/Copenhagen was not researched in this run [ASSUMPTION].
