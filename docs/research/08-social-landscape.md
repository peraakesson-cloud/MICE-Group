# 08 - Swedish Social Media Landscape for Travel Inspiration

Research agent output | Date: 2026-11-02 | Scope: platforms by age, creators, communities, UGC, travel media/podcasts, implications for doubling sydafrikaresor.se from 40 to 80 MSEK by end of 2028.

Conventions: every number below is from a cited URL. Anything not sourced is tagged **[ASSUMPTION]** with rationale and is consolidated in section 9. No internal company data was available; nothing here describes Sydafrikaresor's own performance. Customer-facing copy examples are in Swedish.

---

## 1. Executive summary

1. Facebook, Instagram and YouTube are the three dominant platforms among adult Swedes, and the 45-70 age band (the likely core buyer of a 30-50k SEK tailored South Africa trip) is concentrated on **Facebook, Instagram and YouTube - not TikTok**.
2. Instagram is the #1 platform for daily use in Sweden in 2026 (46% daily), with Facebook second (45% daily); both are flat year over year, so growth must come from better content and paid reach, not from platform tailwind.
3. TikTok is only 22% of Swedes overall and 5-12% of those born in the 1950s-1970s, so it is an awareness/younger-family channel, not the main revenue channel for this product.
4. Trust is brokered by people (friends/family recommendations, creators, reviews, specialist guides). The existing site already has the right raw material: Google/Trustindex reviews, customer travel stories, and celebrity-led trips.
5. Influencer marketing in Sweden carries legal duties (marketing law, clear ad labelling); the company is liable for creators' posts, so a contract and checklist are mandatory.

### 2.3 Posting behaviour (UGC supply)

Source (2025 report summary): https://digitalsnack.se/kunskap/insikt/svenskarna-internet-2025-sociala-medier/
- 45% of Swedes post their own content (down from 49% in 2024). Instagram 26% post (twice as many women as men), Facebook 21% (mostly older), Snapchat 13%, TikTok 4%, YouTube 2%.
- Implication: customer UGC will mainly come from **Instagram and Facebook**, and from women - tailor the "share your trip" mechanics there. Do not rely on TikTok UGC for the 45+ customer base.

## 6. Content and UGC patterns that fit this product

1. **Person-as-trust content**: named staff/guides, Swedish-speaking guides and tour leaders, "Angela/Cecilia/Dennis" appear by name in Google reviews on the home page. Reviews are the brand's strongest asset and should be turned into content.
2. **Trip-report/travelogue**: customer stories already published on the site; repurpose to Instagram carousels, Reels and Facebook posts.
3. **Departure-list posts** ("Kommande resor, platser kvar") used by safari operators on Instagram - a proven conversion format; scarcity must be real.
4. **Season inversion**: Swedish winter = South African summer. The home page itself notes favourable SEK exchange rate and English-speaking convenience; Apollo's page leads with the winter/summer contrast: https://www.apollo.se/sydafrika .
5. **Family/hostlov trips and "med barnbarn"** (grandparents + grandchildren): observed on Instagram (https://www.instagram.com/p/DUu4X6LAS1K/), and consistent with the 50-76 cohorts' strong Facebook presence - grandparents decide/pay, younger family members influence.
6. **Wellness/active departures** led by known names (Ann Wilson & Sofia Åhman, already on the site).
7. **Digital fatigue** (TUI survey, but non-Swedish respondents): 26% prefer sharing in small circles - supports a closed Facebook group and email/WhatsApp-style sharing over only public feeds.
8. **AI planning**: early AI use for trip planning among 18-29 (about 1 in 10 per Ticket/YouGov). Make content and FAQs easy for AI search to cite (structured pages, itineraries, prices, safety/entry facts).
9. **Compliance content**: entry requirements for minors surface as a recurring Swedish traveller question (Reseguiden thread) - a high-trust, high-search FAQ asset that also demonstrates expertise.

## 8. Legal and brand-safety guardrails for creator and UGC work

Source: Konsumentverket (checked version dated 2026-09-03): https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/influencers-reklaminlagg-regler-for-foretag/
- Hidden advertising is prohibited; clarity requirements are especially high on social media.
- Advertiser cannot contract away responsibility; liability may extend to posts the influencer makes on their own initiative; the influencer and intermediaries can have parallel liability.
- Misleading claims (e.g. "Sveriges bästa", price claims) are prohibited in influencer ads too.
- Konsumentverket states that influencers and advertisers continue to break the rules: https://www.konsumentverket.se/pressmeddelande/influerare-och-annonsorer-fortsatter-bryta-mot-lagen/

Operational rules to adopt: written creator brief with mandatory "Annons/Reklam i samarbete med Sydafrikaresor" label in first line and in video; approval of claims; archive of all posts; UGC usage licences with explicit consent; GDPR consent for any personal data collected through contests (company policy exists at /villkor/personuppgiftspolicy/).

## 9. Implications for doubling revenue (40 to 80 MSEK)

Arithmetic anchor (illustrative **[ASSUMPTION]**): if the average booking is around 45k SEK per booking (the African Tours 15-day anchor of ~39k SEK per person is a public competitor price point; actual party size and basket are unknown), +40 MSEK requires roughly **900 additional bookings per year** at that average; at 2 travellers per booking and a 90k SEK booking value it is about 450. The real figure must come from internal data.

1. **Audience fit decides the channel mix.** Prioritise Facebook + Instagram + YouTube (+ email/Google) for the 40-76 cohorts. Allocate TikTok (and Snapchat) only to a small test budget aimed at 1990s-cohort honeymooners and family influencers.
   Suggested starting split of social+creator spend **[ASSUMPTION]**: Meta (Facebook+Instagram) 55%, YouTube 20%, Pinterest 5%, TikTok 5%, creators/events 15%.
2. **Turn reviews and staff into the content engine.** The site already shows named specialists and customer stories; convert 40+ per year into Reels/carousels/FAQs with permission.
3. **Hosted-trip model with known names** scales trust: the site already runs one fitness-led departure and one celebrity footprint trip. Target 6-10 themed departures in 2027 (golf, wine, wellness, photography, family/grandparents, honeymoon) **[ASSUMPTION]**.
4. **Own a community** (Facebook group + email) so that returning customers and referrals (41% inspired by family/friends in TUI's non-Swedish survey; direct Swedish figure unknown) drive repeat and recommended bookings.
5. **Retarget and measure.** Because social inspires but the quote is requested elsewhere, every post needs UTM tags and a "how did you hear about us?" field in quote forms; judge channels on assisted revenue and cost per qualified lead.
6. **PR hooks.** US booking softness, seasonal inversion, SEK strength [ASSUMPTION: currency situation must be confirmed], and new themed departures.
7. **Do not underinvest in YouTube.** 83-93% of 1980s-2000s cohorts and 73-80% of 1960s-70s use it; long-form safari/Cape Town/Garden Route itineraries double as SEO/AI-search assets.
8. **Compliance first** (section 8).
