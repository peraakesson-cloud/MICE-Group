# 09 - Regulation, Trust and Safety Perception (Sweden / EU / South Africa)

Prepared: 2026-11-02. Scope: legal and trust conditions for scaling sydafrikaresor.se from 40 to 80 MSEK in 24 months.
Convention: facts carry a source URL. Anything without a source is tagged **[ASSUMPTION]** or **[VERIFY]** and is listed in section 9. This is research, not legal advice; Swedish travel/marketing counsel should confirm before launch.

---

## 1. Executive summary

1. **Resegaranti changed on 1 April 2026.** A collective guarantee fund now exists. Every package-travel organiser pays **0.6% of the package price** into it, in addition to keeping an individual guarantee. The fee applies to trips booked from 1 April 2026 (Kammarkollegiet). Doubling sales therefore doubles fee cash-out and (likely) the individual guarantee that must be posted.
2. **A second law change (SFS 2026:97) is enacted but not yet in force** ("the day the government decides"). It sets the individual guarantee at **6.25% of guarantee-liable annual turnover** (adjustable up or down by risk), replacing the current fund-linked step-down. Date unknown - biggest financial unknown for the plan.
3. **Growth must be notified to Kammarkollegiet in advance.** If sales look set to rise, the operator must report it immediately and submit a new forecast; failure to notify changed conditions can trigger a sanction fee of **5,000 SEK to 10 MSEK** (max 10% of prior-year turnover for the guarantee breach).
4. **The EU package-travel directive is being amended** (Directive (EU) 2026/1024, in force 28 May 2026, 28 months to transpose, i.e. about late September 2028). It removes "linked travel arrangements" and widens "package" - online upsell flows on sydafrikaresor.se may turn into packages automatically. Transposition falls inside the plan horizon.
5. **South Africa's safety image is the main demand-side trust issue, but the official Swedish messaging is "high crime, most visits pass without serious incident"** and there is currently no UD travel warning text shown for South Africa on the embassy page (updated 6 Oct 2026). Demand is strong: record 10.5 M tourists in 2025 and Swedish arrivals +12.5%.

## 2. Package Travel Act (Paketreselagen 2018:1217) - what a seller must do

Sources: Konsumentverket consumer page https://www.konsumentverket.se/lagar/paketreselagen-konsument/ ; business page https://www.konsumentverket.se/marknadsratt-foretag/resor-for-foretag/ ; Riksdag text https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-och-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/paketreselag-20181217_sfs-2018-1217/ ; Kammarkollegiet https://www.kammarkollegiet.se/om-oss/kammarkollegiets-uppgifter/resegarantier

| Topic | Rule (as stated by authority) | Implication for sydafrikaresor.se |
|---|---|---|
| What is a package | At least two different travel services (e.g. transport + hotel) combined, lasting 24 h+ or incl. an overnight stay. Applies to companies selling online. | Flight + lodge + car + safari combos = package. Selling flight-only or hotel-only is not. Product mix determines guarantee base. |
| Linked travel arrangement | Separate contracts where the seller facilitates a further booking (same occasion, or within 24 h at another firm). Partly covered by guarantee. | Cross-sell pages ("add a safari") can create linked arrangements. Law proposes dropping this category (section 4). |
| Pre-contract information | Organiser name, phone, address, email; price incl. tax and additional costs; health rules; passport/visa; cancellation right and cost; minimum number of travellers and notice date; whether insurance can/should be bought; rights and the resegaranti applicable. Confirmation copy of the contract as soon as possible after booking. | Product pages, checkout and confirmation email need standardised blocks. The resegaranti information must be given in a **special standard form** (Konsumentverket business page). |
| Price changes | Only if contract allows it and only for fuel, taxes/fees or exchange rates. Must be notified at least **20 days** before departure. If increase exceeds **8%** traveller may cancel with full refund. | South Africa trips priced in ZAR/EUR/USD: FX clauses must be explicit; plan for margin hedging rather than surcharges. |
| Cancellation | Traveller may always cancel before departure; reasonable fee must be in terms; full refund if unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances at destination or en route (terror, outbreak, natural disaster). | Safety-related cancellations are a real P&L risk: define how fully refundable supplier terms are matched to customer terms. |
| Substantial change by organiser | Traveller may withdraw and get money back; refund within **14 days** if replacement trip not accepted. | Flight schedule changes on long-haul routes drive refund liquidity needs. |
| Errors during trip | Organiser must remedy; price reduction and damages possible. | Need 24/7 local support capacity as volume grows. |
| Dispute resolution | Consumers can go to ARN (fee 150 SEK, ~6 months handling time, one year from first complaint) or court. Konsumentverket supervises the Act. | ARN rulings are public and a trust signal/risk. Track case count per 1,000 travellers. |

Notes:
- The Act is based on EU Directive 2015/2302 (Konsumentverket business page).
- Konsumentverket's business page also points to a new accessibility law effective **28 June 2025** (passenger transport; shared supervision). ## 3. Travel guarantee (Resegarantilagen 2018:1218 as amended by SFS 2026:96 and 2026:97)

Sources: law text https://lagen.nu/2018:1218 ; Kammarkollegiet fee page https://www.kammarkollegiet.se/vara-tjanster/resegaranti/betala-avgift-till-resegarantifonden ; news 26 Feb 2026 https://www.kammarkollegiet.se/aktuellt/nyheter/2026-02-26-ett-forbattrat-garantisystem-ska-starka-skyddet-for-resenarer ; proposition https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/b97ed00fbe68483c95912c4fd98a246a/2526045-webb-tillganglighetsanpassad.pdf

### 3.1 Obligations
- Organiser must ensure the package is covered by a guarantee **before it is offered for sale**; must notify the supervisory authority (Kammarkollegiet) before starting operations (Resegarantilagen 4 and 11 sections).
- Guarantee is an individual guarantee (bank guarantee, insurance, deposited funds or similar - 13 section) **plus** the collective fund (2 section, as amended).
- Guarantee covers refund of payments and, where passenger transport is included, repatriation and accommodation while waiting (6 section).
- Payments in advance matter: authority weighs total traveller payments, time between payments and performance, and estimated repatriation cost (12 section, current wording).
- Operators renew notification annually and set the individual guarantee for planned trips; **if sales look set to increase, report immediately with a new forecast** (Kammarkollegiet fee page).

### 3.2 Fund fee (in force since 1 April 2026)
- **0.6% of the package price** (also 0.6% of payments received for linked arrangements). Example from Kammarkollegiet: 10,000 SEK trip = 60 SEK fee.
- Based on trips **booked from 1 April 2026** and carried out; the entire package price counts even if parts are paid afterwards.
- Reported and paid three times a year (tertial):

| Period | Report by | Pay by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Jan - 30 Apr | 31 May | 10 Jun |
| 1 May - 31 Aug | 30 Sep | 10 Oct |
| 1 Sep - 31 Dec | 31 Jan | 10 Feb |

  First period (Apr-Aug 2026) was due 30 Sep / 10 Oct 2026. Reporting is by departure month, under penalty of perjury ("heder och samvete").
- Fee continues until the fund reaches **SEK 1.5 billion** (fully built); always applies in an operator's first 12 months. Fee returns if the fund falls below SEK 1 billion (13 a-c sections).
- The proposition estimates the 0.6% fee yields about **SEK 204 million per year** to the fund across the industry (proposition excerpt).

### 3.3 Individual guarantee level
- **Today (SFS 2026:96):** the authority must accept reductions of the individual guarantee as the fund hits milestones: -20% at SEK 300 M, -40% at 600 M, -60% at 900 M, -80% at 1.2 bn (12 a section, version in force).
- **Enacted but not in force (SFS 2026:97, "date set by government"):** individual guarantee = **6.25% of the guarantee-liable annual turnover**; higher if specific risk, lower if no risk to the fund, judged on the operator's financial position.
- Law text sanctions: failure to arrange guarantee gives a sanction fee of **SEK 5,000 - 10,000,000**, max 10% of prior-year turnover (25 section). Supervisory orders can carry fines (22 section).

### 3.4 Worked example (illustrative, [ASSUMPTION] that all 80 MSEK is guarantee-liable package turnover)

| Item | At 40 MSEK | At 80 MSEK |
|---|---|---|
| Fund fee 0.6% | 240,000 SEK/yr | 480,000 SEK/yr (+240,000) |
| Individual guarantee if 6.25% rule applies, before risk adjustment | 2.5 MSEK | 5.0 MSEK (+2.5 MSEK) |

Basis: arithmetic on rates from the sources above. Actual guarantee level, instrument (bank guarantee vs deposit) and cost of the guarantee are company-specific unknowns (section 9). The cost of capital tied up in the guarantee should be modelled in the financial plan.

### 3.5 Implications
- Budget the 0.6% fee as a **cost of sales line** (about 0.6% of GMV) in the 2027-28 model; remember it is on total package price, not margin.
- Contact Kammarkollegiet **before** the budget year with a growth forecast; plan the bank-guarantee limit increase early (bank credit lead-time is an execution risk) [ASSUMPTION on lead time].
- Check status quarterly for the commencement date of SFS 2026:97.
- Display the resegaranti prominently: Kammarkollegiet publishes a lookup of operators with a guarantee (https://www.kammarkollegiet.se/vara-tjanster/resegaranti/hitta-reseaktorer-med-resegaranti) and recommends consumers check it before booking - a free trust asset to link from the site footer and checkout.

## 4. EU package travel directive revision (2026)

Sources: Lexology/HFW 14 Aug 2026 https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=83c11353-0dd6-457e-affe-b598ae2fb4c5 ; ABTA background 2025 https://www.abta.com/news/ask-expert-revised-eu-package-travel-directive-and-how-it-may-affect-you

- **Directive (EU) 2026/1024** (29 April 2026) amends 2015/2302. In force **28 May 2026**; Member States have **28 months** to transpose (about 28 Sep 2028 - my calculation, **[VERIFY]**). Swedish implementation bill status: not found.
- Key changes per HFW: linked travel arrangements removed; separately bought services via linked online booking where traveller data is passed and contracts are concluded within 24 h become a package; added-service offers during purchase need a clear notice that no package protection applies, otherwise protection applies and the trader is deemed organiser; vouchers only with explicit consent, valid max 12 months, protected; free cancellation extended to events affecting the departure place or journey and to reasonably expected disruption (official warnings can be indicators); refunds within 14 days; carriers must refund organisers within 7 days; complaint acknowledgement within 7 days and reasoned reply within 60 days; insolvency refunds within 6 months (9 in exceptional cases).
- The 25% cap on prepayments proposed by the Commission in Nov 2023 was opposed by Council and Parliament (ABTA, 2025). The final adopted text on prepayment is **not confirmed in sources read** - **[VERIFY]**, because deposit policy (a lever for cash flow and conversion) depends on it.
- Implication: website journeys that let customers add safari, car hire or extra nights after flight booking must be re-engineered so the legal status (package vs stand-alone) is deliberate. Government/Konsumentverket guidance expected before 2028.

## 5. Marketing law, price information, GDPR and cookies

### 5.1 Sourced
- Konsumentverket requires price **including tax and additional costs**, and travellers are not required to pay extra costs they were not told about (Konsumentverket business page above). Practical rule: advertised "from" prices must reflect an actually bookable price including taxes, fuel surcharges and fees [VERIFY detailed guidance - the page was long and only the first section was read].
- Konsumentverket has a general price-information page (https://www.konsumentverket.se/konsumentratt/felaktig-prisinformation/) - consumers can report misleading prices; this is a monitoring risk for aggressive campaign pricing.
- E-mail marketing to individuals requires consent, with a narrow exception for existing customer relationships (secondary sources: https://www.mailer.se/gdpr-samtycke-e-postmarknadsforing/ , https://kundkanalen.se/gdpr-e-postmarknadsforing ). The exact section is the Marknadsföringslagen provision on unsolicited electronic marketing; secondary sources cite "19 §" - **[VERIFY section number and exception conditions with counsel]**.

### 5.2 Not verified in this research (no primary source retrieved)
- **Cookie consent** for ad/analytics pixels (Swedish Electronic Communications Act) and IMY guidance: **[VERIFY]** - a consent banner with granular opt-in is the standard practice in Sweden **[ASSUMPTION]**. Impact: measurable traffic and retargeting audiences shrink when consent is declined; plan for server-side tagging and first-party data capture.
- GDPR basics for CRM, lookalike audiences, call recording and legal basis for retargeting: **[ASSUMPTION]** that standard rules apply; DPO/legal review required.
- Rules for influencer/affiliate disclosure (labelling of advertising), and Google/Meta ad policy for travel, were not researched. **[VERIFY]**.

### 5.3 Implications for the marketing plan
- Build a consented first-party email/SMS list: the main owned channel for a repeat, high-ticket product.
- Ad copy must show price with all mandatory costs; avoid "from" prices that no booking can reach; archive all price claims per campaign (evidence file).
- Disclose resegaranti and company details on landing pages; use the official standard form text.

## 7. South Africa: safety perception, travel advice and demand context

### 7.1 Official Swedish advice (Sweden Abroad, embassy page updated 6 Oct 2026)
Source: https://www.swedenabroad.se/sv/om-utlandet-f%C3%B6r-svenska-medborgare/sydafrika/reseinformation/ambassadens-reseinformation/ and general page https://www.swedenabroad.se/sv/om-utlandet-f%C3%B6r-svenska-medborgare/sydafrika/reseinformation/

- Current events: "no current events to report". The page says a UD travel warning (reseavrådan) would be shown at the top; none was shown at the time of reading.
- Wording on security: "despite the very high crime level and the large number of traffic accidents, the vast majority of visits pass without serious incidents"; be careful in traffic, after dark and when handling money or valuables.
- Concrete advice relevant to product design: avoid exchanging money/ATM at airport; use well-known transport companies airport transfers, especially after dark (armed robberies of travellers reported, especially on transfers from OR Tambo); avoid city centres at night except at restaurants etc.; do not visit townships alone, use experienced guide/driver; avoid commuter trains in Johannesburg/Pretoria/Cape Town (Gautrain is considered safe); avoid long rural drives at night; carry copies of passport.
- Health: private hospitals may demand payment before treatment even with insurance - carry a credit card; malaria risk in parts of Kruger/Mpumalanga/Limpopo/KwaZulu-Natal, particularly Oct-Apr; cholera outbreaks in some provinces (drink no tap water in affected areas); mpox: few cases reported; check insurance.
- Other: left-hand traffic; rip currents and occasional shark incidents; loadshedding (scheduled power cuts) has paused since late March 2024 but outages can still occur; demonstrations possible with short notice.
- Entry: Swedish citizens up to 90 days normally need no visa; passport valid at least 30 days after departure with two blank pages; rules can change at short notice; yellow fever and children-travel rules referenced (South African Embassy and Home Affairs).
- Separate: the page from 9 Sep 2026 warns about aviation disruption from the Middle East crisis - a relevant risk for routings via Gulf hubs [implication: routing via Europe vs Gulf is a trust and cancellation factor - confirm with air research file 04].

### 7.2 Demand context (to weigh against perception risk)
- Stats SA: South Africa received **10.5 M tourists in 2025** (+17.7% on 2024; 2.6% above 2019); overseas share 22.8%; 97.3% leisure; overseas average stay 14 days. Source: https://www.statssa.gov.za/?p=19362
- South African Tourism: Europe +12.6% in 2025; **Sweden +12.5%**, UK +15.4%, Germany +14.0%; North Europe hub reports Sweden within a recovering market; airlift and flight cost cited as ongoing challenges. Source: https://www.southafrica.net/gl/en/trade/press/strong-europe-performance-fuels-south-african-air-tourism-growth-in-2025
- Swedish absolute arrivals number: not found in these sources - see file 01 (market size).

### 7.3 Perception assessment and implications
- The official Swedish advice is cautionary but not a warning; a doubling strategy relies on that status. **A change to a UD travel warning, or an unavoidable-and-extraordinary-circumstances event, would trigger free cancellation rights** (Konsumentverket) and sharply hit cash flow and guarantee exposure. Under the new EU directive, official warnings can justify cancellation even before an event occurs (HFW).
- **No Swedish survey data on safety perception of South Africa was retrieved.** The perception level and its effect on conversion is **unknown** and should be measured (on-site survey, search-term analysis, call-centre objection log).
- Recommended trust positioning (for the marketing plan): "safe-by-design" itineraries (pre-booked transfers, daytime driving, guided township visits, vetted lodges), transparent safety page quoting UD/embassy advice rather than denying risk, pre-departure briefing and 24/7 local emergency number, and a safety-incident reporting log. Messaging should not claim South Africa is "safe" in absolute terms [consumer-law risk: misleading claims].

## 8. Implications for doubling revenue (40 to 80 MSEK)

1. **Capital and compliance scale linearly with turnover:** +0.24 MSEK/yr fund fee and possibly +2.5 MSEK in guarantee capacity at 80 MSEK (illustrative, section 3.4). Start the guarantee-limit dialogue with the bank and Kammarkollegiet in Q4 2026 / Q1 2027 [ASSUMPTION: lead time several weeks].
2. **Notification duty is a launch gate:** campaigns that produce volume surges must be matched by an updated forecast to Kammarkollegiet. Put this in the growth governance (owner: CFO/CEO).
3. **Product architecture matters legally:** each added service combination (safari, car, activities) changes whether the sale is a package, which sets guarantee base, information duties and, from about 2028, a broader package definition.
4. **Cancellation exposure rises with volume:** safety events or warnings drive refunds of 100% within 14 days; ensure supplier terms and liquidity buffers, and consider scenario analysis (e.g. 10% of bookings cancelled).
5. **Trust is the conversion constraint** for a 30-60k SEK purchase [ASSUMPTION on price range - replace with real average order value]; visible guarantee, reviews, human advisers and a rational safety narrative should be on every landing page.
6. **Data-driven acquisition is exposed to consent rules:** plan for lower tracked conversion and build first-party data.
7. **Complaint handling must scale:** new EU rules (7 days acknowledgement, 60 days reply) will come; set service levels now and track ARN cases.
