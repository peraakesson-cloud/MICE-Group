## 1. Headline findings

1. **The market is small in absolute terms.** South African Tourism reports only **27,625 Swedish arrivals to South Africa in 2025, +12.5% YoY**, for all purposes (holiday, VFR, business, MICE). Norway 14,632 and Denmark 19,547 for comparison. [SOURCED: South African Tourism, Tourism Performance Report Jan-Dec 2025, appendix table, https://live.southafrica.net/media/308701/sat-2025-annual-international-tourism-performance-report-1.pdf?downloadId=436981]
2. **80 MSEK is a very large share of that market.** If average revenue per traveller is ~45,000 SEK [ASSUMPTION, see section 7], 40 MSEK is about 890 travellers (about 3% of all Swedish arrivals) and 80 MSEK is about 1,780 travellers (about 6.4%). Market growth alone (12.5% a year, if sustained [ASSUMPTION]) would take 40 MSEK to roughly 50 MSEK by end of 2028; the remaining ~30 MSEK must come from share gain, higher revenue per traveller, or new segments.
3. **Search demand is concentrated in a few high-volume head terms and a long tail of low-volume, high-intent queries.** Google Trends relative data shows "Sydafrika" and "Kapstaden" register, but long-tail strings such as "resa till Sydafrika" and "Sydafrika safari" register as ~0 on the Trends scale when compared with the head term. Absolute monthly volumes require Google Keyword Planner or Ahrefs/Semrush [TOOL-LIMIT].
4. **The commercial SERP is crowded with big and niche rivals.** Ving, TUI, Apollo, Albatros, Rolfs, Kilroy, plus about ten specialist Africa operators (African Tours, Afrikakompaniet, Safariresor.se, Afrika Safari Resor, Nyati, Orient Travel, Jambo, Temaresor, TourCompass, Sydafrikaexperten, MyChoice Sydafrika, Koch Ljungberg, Andersons). sydafrikaresor.se holds #1 for the broad "resa till Sydafrika" in the two snapshots I took, but is weak or absent on the informational queries that feed the funnel (best time, cost, safety, itineraries).
5. **The site's content layer is thin on exactly the topics that rivals rank for.** The site's own sitemaps show only ~6 "tips" articles (self-drive, Kruger by car, restaurant tips, travelling with children, coffee guide) and no dedicated "best time to travel", "cost", "safety", "Garden Route itinerary", or "how many days" guides. Details in section 5.

- Average index: Sydafrika **11**, Kapstaden **2**, Krugerparken ~0, "resa till Sydafrika" ~0, "Sydafrika safari" ~0.

**3b. Seasonality of "Sydafrika" (average index by calendar month, 5-year)**
Jan 12.6, Feb 11.1, Mar 9.9, Apr 8.4, May 9.6, **Jun 15.0**, Jul 11.1, Aug 8.1, Sep 9.8, Oct 10.1, Nov 10.7, Dec 10.7.
- Highest interest in **January-February** (booking/dreaming season for the following winter-spring, and Swedish winter) and a **June** peak (likely news/event driven; cause unverified [unknown]).

Jan 2.5, Feb 2.2, Mar 1.6, Apr 1.2, May 1.0, Jun 1.2, Jul 1.1, Aug 1.2, Sep 1.5, **Oct 2.0**, Nov 1.7, **Dec 2.0**.

Weekly index mostly 55-75, with spikes of 99-100 in mid-June and early July 2026 (event/holiday driven, cause unverified). Compared with "Kenya", seasonal peak is **December (avg 82)** and a trough in Aug-Sep (~34).
- Safari interest is **always on** and large compared with South Africa-specific terms: the biggest content opportunity is capturing "safari" demand and steering it towards South Africa (Kruger/Greater Kruger, malaria-free reserves, combined with Cape Town).

**3e. Brand terms**
"Sydafrikaresor", "Ving Sydafrika", "Albatros Sydafrika", "Apollo Sydafrika" all register ~0 on Trends in Sweden over 12 months. Branded search volume for sydafrikaresor.se is therefore small. ## 8. Implications for doubling revenue (40 to 80 MSEK in 24 months)
