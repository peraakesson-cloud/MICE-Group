# 01 - Market Size, Growth, Seasonality, Booking Windows and Price Levels

Research date: 2026-11-02. Scope: Swedish outbound / long-haul leisure market and South Africa (SA) demand from Sweden, as the basis for doubling sydafrikaresor.se turnover from 40 MSEK to 80 MSEK by end of 2028.

Method note: only facts with a cited URL are stated as facts. Everything else is tagged [ASSUMPTION] with a rationale. No internal company data was available or used. The page sydafrikaresor.se was read as a public website only.

## 2. Market size

### 2.1 Swedish travel industry (context)
- SRF (Svenska Resebransch Foreningen) states members represent the "vast majority" of Swedish travel agencies and tour operators; business volume approx. SEK 60 bn and ~8,000 employees at end of 2025. Source: https://srf-org.se/about-srf/
- SRF Reserapporten 2024 (via Travel Report, published 2024-11-25): travel agencies' total sales 2023 approx. SEK 33 bn (private ~17.5 bn, business ~15.5 bn); tour operators approx. SEK 23 bn in 2023 (+6.9 bn vs 2022); 221 travel agencies and ~430 tour operators active in Sweden; SRF members = 77% of agency sales and 86% of tour operator sales; 2019 reference level SEK 58 bn. Source: https://www.travelreport.se/svenska-resebransch-foreningen-reserapporten-2024-ar-har/ (figures extracted from page excerpt; verify against the full report before quoting externally).
- IBISWorld estimates the Swedish "Travel Agencies and Tour Operators" industry at about EUR 5.5 bn in 2026 with 1,567 businesses (definition differs from SRF; paywalled detail). Source: https://www.ibisworld.com/sweden/industry/travel-agencies-tour-operators/200304/

Implication: the total Swedish packaged-travel pool is tens of billions SEK. An 80 MSEK SA business is ~0.13% of SRF-reported volume, so the constraint is category demand for SA specifically, not total industry size.

### 2.2 South Africa demand from Sweden (the relevant market)
- Stats SA counts **27,625 Swedish tourist arrivals in 2025** (24,562 in 2024). This is the best public proxy for the size of the SA-bound Swedish market. Note: it includes all purposes (holiday, business, visiting friends and relatives) and all booking channels (self-booked, airline-direct, other operators), so the leisure packaged/tailor-made addressable portion is smaller.
- Context: Sweden is a mid-sized European source. Switzerland ranked #19 in one table cited on Wikipedia with 42,255 arrivals (year not verified, so use only as a rough comparator): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tourism_in_South_Africa . Sweden's 2025 figure (27.6k) is below that, i.e. Sweden is outside SA's top-10 overseas markets (top 10 in Dec 2025: UK, Germany, USA, Netherlands, Australia, France, Brazil, Canada, Switzerland, Russia, per the Stats SA release).
- Total tourist arrivals to SA grew from 8.9 M (2024) to 10.5 M (2025), but 75.2% came from neighbouring southern African countries (Stats SA post: https://www.facebook.com/StatsSA/posts/wedotourism-in-2025-752-of-all-tourists-came-from-neighbouring-southern-african-/1353343670163315/). The overseas/Europe segment relevant to Swedish operators is a minority share of the headline growth.

### 2.3 What 40 MSEK implies (illustrative, all [ASSUMPTION])
Public prices on sydafrikaresor.se (see section 5) range from SEK 17,900 to 95,200 "from" per person, with most guided tours at SEK 34,500-69,900.
- [ASSUMPTION] Realised average revenue per passenger (incl. flights, tailor-made mix, multi-person bookings) is SEK 35,000-50,000. Rationale: midpoint of published "from" prices for 11-18 day trips; flights may or may not be in the "from" price (unverified).
- At SEK 35k-50k per passenger, 40 MSEK = roughly **800-1,150 passengers per year**; that is **~2.9%-4.2% of the 27,625 Swedish arrivals** in 2025.
- To reach 80 MSEK at constant average price: roughly **1,600-2,300 passengers** in 2028.
- [ASSUMPTION] If Swedish arrivals to SA keep growing 8-12% p.a. (2025 actual: +12.5%; Europe +12.6%), the market would be about 32,000-35,000 in 2027 and 34,500-39,000 in 2028 (compounded 8-12% from 27,625 over three years from 2025). Rationale: continuation of the 2025 trend, tempered by base effects.
- Market growth alone would take 40 MSEK to roughly 47-50 MSEK by 2028 at constant share (+17-25% over two years from the 2026 base, assuming 2026 grows similarly). The remaining gap to 80 MSEK must come from **share gain (needed share roughly 5-6.5% of Swedish arrivals vs ~3-4% today)** and/or **higher revenue per passenger**.

These numbers are triangulation only; the CEO should replace the passenger and average-price assumptions with actual booking data.

## 3. Growth and demand momentum

Facts:
- Swedish arrivals to SA: +12.5% in 2025 (Stats SA, link above). December 2025 +15.2%.
- Ticket winter 2025 booking data: Africa +29%, SA more than tripled y/y, SA new in Africa top 5 (RES.se, 2025-11-06, link above). Single-agency data, short booking window (5 weeks), winter departures only; directionally positive but not a market-wide measure.
- Competing African winter destinations also grew strongly (Egypt, Cape Verde, Morocco, Mauritius), so SA competes for the same "winter sun/Africa" wallet. Tanzania bookings fell ~35% after post-election unrest (Oct 2025), showing safety news can redirect Swedish demand quickly (same RES.se article).
- Competitor activity indicates active marketing: TourCompass advertised a "trygghetsgaranti" / price guarantee for bookings made by 31 Oct 2026 for departures 2026-2028 on SA trips: https://www.tourcompass.se/sydafrika/resor.htm . Several specialist rivals market SA in Swedish (Afrika Safari Resor, Temaresor, TourCompass, Koch & Ljungberg, Winberg Travel, African Tours, Gonana). Presence of many specialists suggests a contested but proven niche.
- Visit Sweden April 2026 (YouGov): 58% plan an outbound trip in the next 6 months; Swedes "make late decisions" in the domestic context (not specific to long-haul). Source above.

Not found (see unknowns): a Swedish-market-specific 2026 YTD figure for SA arrivals from Sweden (Stats SA monthly releases for 2026 exist; the August 2026 headline reported total visitors up 7.4% per a social post, unverified: https://www.facebook.com/newsroomza/videos/south-africa-welcomed-more-than-1-million-international-tourists-in-august-2026-/2308585939960078/ ).

## 4. Seasonality

Facts (SA side):
- Stats SA: overseas tourists are generally highest in quarters 1 and 4 of the year (i.e. Oct-Mar, SA summer), with exceptions in 2021-2022. Source: Stats SA P0350 Dec 2025 (link above).
- Swedish tour operators describe the best season for Cape Town/Garden Route/winelands as the SA summer, roughly **October/September to April**; winter (May-Oct) is cooler and wetter in the Western Cape: Winberg Travel https://www.winbergtravel.se/sydafrika/garden-route/ ; Afrika Safari Resor https://afrikasafariresor.se/praktisk-information/nar-ska-man-besoka-sydafrika/ ; Koch & Ljungberg https://www.koch-ljungberg.se/reseguider/sydafrika/basta-tiden-att-resa-till-sydafrika/ (March-April good for Cape Town and Garden Route as safari conditions in the northeast get drier).
- Safari (Kruger and Greater Kruger) peak is the dry SA winter, roughly **June-August**, popular with Swedes; one Swedish SA operator advises booking safaris at least six months ahead (Gonana excerpt, https://www.gonanasouthafricantravel.se/blogg-de-perfekta-arstiderna-for-svenskar-att-uppt/ ; excerpt only, page scrape failed).
- Time zone: no jetlag; SA is on the same time as Central Europe in winter and 1 hour behind Swedish summer time (Afrika Safari Resor travel advice). This supports Swedish family and short-break use.
- Malaria exists only in some provinces and mainly October-May (Afrika Safari Resor). Visa-free for Swedes up to 90 days; passport must be valid 30 days beyond departure with two blank pages (Swedish embassy): https://www.swedenabroad.se/sv/om-utlandet-f%C3%B6r-svenska-medborgare/sydafrika/reseinformation/ambassadens-reseinformation/

Implication for Swedish demand calendar (synthesis, [ASSUMPTION] on exact peaks since no Sweden-specific monthly arrival series was retrieved):
- Peak 1: Swedish winter (Dec-Mar): sun escape, Cape Town + Garden Route + safari; school break "sportlov" (Feb/Mar) and Christmas/New Year.
- Peak 2: Swedish summer (Jun-Aug): safari in dry season, Swedish school holidays, family trips (cheap for Swedes due to SA winter low season in Cape Town).
- Shoulder: Sep-Nov (spring, wine and flowers) and April-May (Easter, autumn): attractive for 50+ market.
- Marketing consequence: booking-decision peaks fall in Jan-Mar (for next winter and summer) and Sep-Nov (for the following winter), consistent with long-haul lead times (section 5), but must be validated with the company's own booking-date vs departure-date data.

## 5. Booking windows

Facts are thin. Public sources found:
- Gonana (Swedish SA operator) recommends safari booking at least **6 months** ahead (excerpt above).
- Ticket's winter-2025 data was gathered from bookings made 1 Oct - 4 Nov 2025 for 1 Dec 2025 - 28 Feb 2026 departures, i.e. an observed 1-5 month booking window for winter long-haul sun trips (RES.se). This is a sample window, not a lead-time distribution.
- TourCompass offers "riskfree booking" with price guarantee for departures 2026-2028 when booked by 31 Oct 2026 (promotional device that pulls bookings forward 12-24 months): https://www.tourcompass.se/sydafrika/resor.htm
- Visit Sweden (domestic context) notes many Swedes decide late: https://corporate.visitsweden.com/kunskap/malgrupp/sverige/hur-tanker-svenska-resenarer-om-sitt-resande-nu/

[ASSUMPTION] Typical lead time for tailor-made SA trips is 4-9 months and for guided round tours 6-12 months. Rationale: lodge/safari availability and multi-sector flights constrain tailor-made trips; no Swedish public lead-time statistic found.

Implication for the 24-month horizon: because many bookings for the 2027 season (Jan-Apr 2027 and Jun-Aug 2027) are being made now (Nov 2026 - Mar 2027), bookings taken in Q4 2026 - Q1 2027 largely determine 2027 revenue; revenue timing (recognition at departure vs deposit) must be defined in the financial plan. Growth actions started after mid-2027 mostly pay out in 2028 departures. Recommend tracking **booked-not-departed order book** weekly.

## 6. Price levels

Facts (public "from" prices, per person, taken 2026-11-02 from https://www.sydafrikaresor.se/ ; content modified 2026-10-02; whether prices include flights is not stated in the excerpt, treat as unverified):

Guided round tours: 11 days family trip SEK 34,500; 13 days food/wine + Big Five SEK 44,300; 18 days safari + Garden Route + Cape Town SEK 45,900; 17 days "Sydafrikas skatter" SEK 49,900; 15 days golf safari SEK 50,995; 15 days Cape Town/Victoria Falls/Botswana safari SEK 53,900; 16 days golf + Big Five SEK 64,995; 21 days southern Africa SEK 69,900.

Tailor-made: 11 days Kruger + Cape Town SEK 17,900; 15 days Pilanesberg + Garden Route SEK 25,900; 10 days Sol & Safari SEK 29,600; 13 days honeymoon SEK 31,700; 11 days Lottie Knutson trail SEK 55,200; 19 days golf with Rovos Rail SEK 95,200; Seychelles extensions SEK 46,900-61,900.

Competitor/market reference prices (public, not like-for-like):
- Flights Stockholm - Cape Town: Expedia shows round trip from about USD 690 with typical return range wide (USD 1,028-5,641) https://www.expedia.com/lp/flights/arn/cpt/stockholm-to-cape-town ; Google Flights snippet: no direct flights on this route; Emirates from ~USD 775 typical USD 790-1,550 https://www.google.com/travel/flights/flights-from-stockholm-to-cape-town.html ; Qatar Airways from USD 418 (one-way?) and ~USD 787-852 priced https://www.travelocity.com/lp/flight-routes/qatar-airways-from-arlanda-to-cape-town-intl/qr/arn/cpt ; Condor lists Stockholm departures and a Cape Town page https://www.condor.com/en/flights-from-stockholm-arlanda-to-cape-town (details not verified). Flight times via hubs ~19.5 h (Emirates page https://www.emirates.com/english/destinations/arn/cpt/flights-from-stockholm-to-cape-town/ ).
- Competitor advertising emphasises value ("mer resa for pengarna") due to the rand: https://africantours.se/sydafrika-mer-resa-for-pengarna-i-kontrasternas-land/ ; the SEK/ZAR rate is a core price driver (current rate not retrieved).

Implications:
- Flights are a large share of trip cost and are demand-sensitive; hub-connection pricing (Gulf carriers, European hubs) is what customers compare against.
- A bulk of tailor-made "from" prices below SEK 30k vs guided tours at SEK 35-70k shows a ladder from entry to premium. Doubling revenue can come from volume at entry price points, from premiumization (golf, rail, honeymoon, multi-country), or both. Choice should follow the company's own margin data.

## 7. Implications for doubling revenue (40 to 80 MSEK in 24 months)

1. Market tailwind covers only ~20-25% of the growth. Public data show the Swedish-origin SA market at 27.6k arrivals growing ~12% per year; at constant share that yields roughly +17-25% over two years. The other ~75-80% of the target must be share gain and revenue-per-passenger increases. [ASSUMPTION based on section 2.3]
2. Required share of Swedish arrivals rises from roughly 3-4% to roughly 5-6.5%. This is plausible but aggressive: it means taking customers from rivals (TourCompass, Temaresor, Afrika Safari Resor, Koch & Ljungberg, etc.) and from self-bookers.
3. Expand the funnel beyond "SA only": the site already sells Seychelles and Botswana/Victoria Falls combos, which increases basket size without needing extra arrivals. SA arrivals data do not capture neighbouring-country add-ons.
4. Seasonal balance: concentrate paid acquisition in the Sep-Mar research/booking window for Swedish winter and summer-safari departures; build off-peak (Apr-May, Sep-Nov) offers to flatten capacity use.
5. Order book first: with 4-12 month lead times, 2027 revenue is largely determined by bookings made between now and mid-2027; waiting for 2027 results to react means only 2028 can reflect new actions.
6. Risk factors visible in public data: safety news can cut demand sharply (Tanzania -35%); rand strength and air fares move price competitiveness; Gulf-hub flight dependence; competitors using price guarantees and early-booking incentives.
7. Segments to test (hypotheses, no sourced sizing): 55+ couples (wine, Garden Route), families (11-day family trip product exists), golf (two golf products listed), honeymoon/weddings, safari add-on from Kruger, repeat customers/referrals.
