# 07 - Paid Media Benchmarks for Swedish Travel (Google, Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, Affiliate)

Prepared: 2026-11-02 | Purpose: ground the paid-media budget and KPI model for growing sydafrikaresor.se from 40 to 80 MSEK in 24 months.
Convention: every number carries a source URL. Anything without a source is tagged **[ASSUMPTION]** and listed in sections 9-10.
Important caveat: nearly all public benchmarks are global/US, in USD, and mix lead and purchase conversions. There is almost no published Swedish long-haul travel benchmark. Treat all figures as planning ranges to be replaced by the company's own account data in the first 60 days.

---

## 1. Headline findings

1. Generic "Travel" benchmarks are cheap on paper (Google CPC about USD 1.5-2.1, Meta traffic CPC about USD 0.4-0.5) but they are dominated by hotel/flight/OTA traffic. A high-ticket, high-consideration, human-assisted product (guided South Africa trips) will see far lower click-to-booking rates than the headline "conversion rate" suggests. Plan on an enquiry/lead funnel, not a checkout funnel.
2. Sweden's Meta costs are near the global median on average (about USD 1.07 CPC) but extremely volatile month to month; the cheapest months are Nov-Feb, which coincide with the main long-haul booking window.
3. Reach: YouTube (77-78%), Facebook (69-71%) and Instagram (65-66%) are the three biggest social platforms among Swedes; TikTok (22%) and Pinterest (18%) are niche in reach and skew young (TikTok) or low-daily-use (Pinterest). Paid budget should be concentrated on Google Search, Meta and YouTube; TikTok and Pinterest are test channels only.
4. Affiliate/comparison channels for travel pay low percentage commissions (4-8% typical for OTAs/experiences; 5-20% quoted for tour operators). For a tour operator paying out of a thin margin, this is a selective partnership channel, not a volume engine.
5. Doubling revenue by paid media alone would require roughly 5-10 MSEK of incremental annual media at plausible ROAS (section 6), which is feasible only if the funnel (enquiry handling capacity, close rate, repeat/referral) scales in step with the ads.

- Brand + destination-intent search is the highest-ROI layer; capture 100% of branded and competitor-adjacent queries, then expand to high-intent destination/itinerary terms.
- Optimise to qualified enquiry and, via offline conversion import, to booked revenue, not to clicks.
- Performance Max and Demand Gen can scale reach into inspiration audiences but need first-party audiences and clean conversion values to avoid wasting spend on low-value leads.

- Cost per click is very cheap relative to Google, but intent is lower; Meta's role is demand generation (inspiration, video, retargeting, lead-gen forms) feeding the enquiry pipeline.
- Cheapest media months (Nov-Feb) line up with the likely peak planning period for next-year long-haul trips ([ASSUMPTION], see section 5): front-load prospecting and lead generation there.
- Reach: 69% of Swedes use Facebook and 66% Instagram (https://svenskarnaochinternet.se/utvalt/sociala-medier/); Facebook daily use is 49% and skews older, which fits a leisure South Africa audience (affluent 40+ couples and families) [ASSUMPTION about target persona].

### 4.3 Implications
- YouTube is the highest-reach platform in Sweden and a natural home for destination films (safari, Cape Town, Garden Route, wine regions); use for awareness and remarketing, measure via view-through and branded search lift.
- TikTok: low reach among the likely 40+ target; run only as a small test (for example 3-5% of budget) aimed at younger couples/honeymooners and for organic content discovery.
- Pinterest: only 2% daily use in Sweden; treat as a minor itinerary-inspiration/SEO-adjacent test, not a growth driver. Reconsider only if the audience data show a strong fit.

## 5. Seasonality

What is sourced:
- Meta CPC in Sweden is lowest Nov-Feb and spikes in spring (March, May) and Aug/Oct (Superads link in 3.2). Midsummer is active for travel and food advertising; Black Friday/December lift CPMs (same page).
- The cruise industry's equivalent "wave season" runs January-March, with deals starting in November (https://www.royalcaribbean.com/guides/what-is-wave-season-best-time-to-find-cruise-deals). This is a cruise-sector proxy only, not evidence for South Africa.

What is NOT sourced (no reliable public Swedish long-haul seasonality data found):
- **[ASSUMPTION]** Swedish long-haul leisure demand shows a main booking peak from January to March (post-Christmas, "early-bird" for next autumn/winter) and a secondary peak in September-November for the Swedish winter months. Rationale: general industry pattern for long-haul and the Nordic sun-and-winter-escape booking behaviour; must be replaced by the company's own booking-date curve.
- **[ASSUMPTION]** South Africa travel is demand-shaped by two things: Swedish winter (Nov-Mar = South African summer, strong Cape Town/safari demand from Swedes) and the Southern-Hemisphere safari/whale season (Jun-Sep). The company should confirm which departure months drive revenue.

Implication: weight the media calendar to be roughly 50-60% of annual spend in Nov-Mar (when Meta is cheapest and booking intent peaks, per assumption), with always-on brand/search protection the rest of the year. Final phasing must be set from the company's own booking-date and departure-date data.

## 6. What it takes to add 40 MSEK (illustrative model, all inputs [ASSUMPTION] unless sourced)

Inputs to be replaced with company data:
- Average booked revenue per booking: **[ASSUMPTION] SEK 35,000** (about 2 travellers). Needs actual value.
- Incremental revenue target: SEK 40,000,000 over 24 months (given) = about 1,140 additional bookings per year at the end-state versus today.
- Share of incremental revenue attributable to paid media: **[ASSUMPTION] 40-60%**, remainder from SEO/content, email/CRM, partners, referrals, repeat customers and PR.

Media spend required by assumed blended revenue-to-media ratio (ROAS) on the paid-attributed share (SEK 16-24 MSEK):

| Blended ROAS (revenue / media) | Media for 16 MSEK paid revenue | Media for 24 MSEK paid revenue |
|---|---|---|
| 4x | 4.0 MSEK | 6.0 MSEK |
| 6x | 2.7 MSEK | 4.0 MSEK |
| 8x | 2.0 MSEK | 3.0 MSEK |
| 10x | 1.6 MSEK | 2.4 MSEK |

ROAS values are planning scenarios, **not benchmarks**: the only sourced ROAS-type figure in this research is Pinterest ecommerce 2.30x median (https://www.benchmarketing.org/benchmarks/pinterest-ads), which is irrelevant to high-ticket travel. Sweden's retail Google CPA of SEK 114 (Kvantic) is also not transferable.

Funnel sanity check (**[ASSUMPTION]**, for planning only): click-to-enquiry 2-4%, enquiry-to-booking 15-25%. At that funnel, click-to-booking is 0.3-1.0%, so each booking needs 100-330 paid clicks. At a blended CPC of SEK 8-20 [ASSUMPTION, bracketing Swedish e-commerce SEK 4.58 and travel USD 1.5-2.1], cost per booking is about SEK 800-6,600, i.e. 2-19% of a SEK 35,000 booking. Gross margin per booking is unknown; the sustainable CAC cap must be set from it.

Capacity note: 1,140 extra bookings/year implies several times more enquiries (at 20% close rate, about 5,700 extra enquiries per year). With about 22 employees, sales capacity and speed-to-lead are likely the real constraint, not media cost.

## 7. Recommended measurement and test plan (derived from the above)

1. Weeks 1-4 (Nov-Dec 2026): set up conversion tracking to enquiry and, with CRM offline import, to booking value; run branded + 20-30 high-intent Swedish destination keyword groups on Google Search; a Meta lead + traffic test with video; YouTube remarketing. Budget cap for test: **[ASSUMPTION] SEK 150-250k**.
2. Replace every benchmark in this document with the account's own CPC, CTR, CVR, enquiry-to-booking rate and booking value by end of Jan 2027.
3. Keep TikTok/Pinterest tests at 5% or less of media until a channel shows enquiry-to-booking at or below the blended CAC.
4. Add a monthly seasonality dashboard (CPC, CPM, enquiry volume, booking-date curve) to revise phasing each quarter.
5. Use affiliate/comparison/influencer partners on a pay-on-booking basis only (section 8 commission ranges); no fixed-fee placements without proof.
