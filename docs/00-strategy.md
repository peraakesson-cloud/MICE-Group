## 1. Executive summary

1. **80 MSEK in 2028 is a stretch target, not a base case.** Swedish arrivals to South Africa were 27,625 in 2025 (+12.5%) (R01, R02), but 2026 momentum is weaker than 2025 (RT-D: total SA arrivals -6.5% y/y in May 2026 and about -1.3% in Sep 2026, categories to be verified; Sweden YTD unknown). Against the leisure and intermediated pools (section 3) 80 MSEK would need roughly 16-23% of Swedes who book through an intermediary, about 90% of the extra travellers taken from rivals or DIY. Three independent red-team views put achievable 2028 at 55-70 MSEK.
2. **Plan of record: core bridge to about 64.4 MSEK in 2028 and 48 MSEK in 2027** (section 9). Levers L1-L10 sum to +24.4 MSEK, are net of overlap, and match the demand-side 55-65 range. Reaching 80 additionally requires **+15.6 MSEK from four gap levers** (Nordic scope extension, MICE/incentive cross-sell, one inorganic move, B2B wholesale; section 9.3). They are not in the budget until a gate releases them. If the CEO needs 80 MSEK as a commitment, the honest statement is: *80 is reachable only if at least three of the four gap levers land and the core bridge delivers at the top of its range.*
3. **Economics are thin.** On the programme as designed, incremental cost is about 3.3 MSEK in 2027 and 7.1 MSEK in 2028. At the assumed 20% gross margin the programme loses about 1.7 MSEK in 2027 and 2.2 MSEK in 2028 (cumulative about -3.9 MSEK) and breaks even only at about 23.5% gross margin on the whole book [A7]. The real margin is the single most important unknown (section 12). A loss budget and stop-loss rule are set so the CEO is spending a known amount to learn fast.
4. **Positioning.** "Sydafrika-specialisten sedan 2003": named personal adviser, guided trips with Swedish-speaking guides and fully tailor-made trips, backed by a visible travel guarantee. We do not compete on lowest price (R03).
5. **The binding constraints are people, supply timing and trust proof, not media.** Allocation requests for Jun-Sep 2027 and festive 2027 start **this week**, not 30 Nov (RT-S S1). Workload rises about 60% by 2028 (about +3.5 to +4.5 FTE; +6 at 80 MSEK). Trustpilot is 2.7 on 8 reviews (R03); service quality is a hard gate on spend.
6. **Marketing budget (excluding salaries): 0.30 MSEK in Q4 2026, 3.6 MSEK in 2027 (7.5% of 48) and 4.6 MSEK in 2028 (7.1% of 64.4)**, of which only 2.4 and 3.0 MSEK are committed; the rest is released by gate (section 11). A further 1.0 MSEK in 2028 is releasable only if incremental ROAS is proven. Pre-Gate-1 paid commitment is capped at 0.5 MSEK (v1: 1.1-1.3).
7. **No irreversible commitments before 31 Jan 2027** on the three unknowns that swing the answer most: revenue per traveller (35-60k plausible), real gross margin, and the true leisure demand pool (section 3.3). No-regret actions proceed now.

## 5. Situation in ten facts

1. Record demand: South Africa 10.5 M international tourists in 2025 (+17.7%); Sweden 27,625 (+12.5%), Europe +12.6% (R01 Stats SA https://www.statssa.gov.za/?p=19362). Swedish arrivals are still about 43% below 2017 (48,552) (R02), which suggests headroom but also that the market is volatile.
2. Swedes are turning away from the USA (Ticket: US bookings -12% vs 2024, https://www.travelreport.se/ticket-svenskarnas-usa-bokningar-minskar-med-12-procent/) and Ticket's winter 2025 data showed South Africa bookings more than tripled (one agency, five weeks, winter only, small base; directional only) (https://res.se/svenskar-valjer-afrika-for-vinterresan-sydafrika-ny-favorit-2025) (R01, R08). Single-agency data, directional only.
3. Competition is dense and tiered: mass-market (Ving, TUI, Apollo) with shallow Cape Town-centred products; group operators with flights and Swedish leaders at SEK 36-40k (Albatros, Tourcompass); premium group (Temaresor SEK 60k+); and trust-rich specialists (MyChoice, Safariresor.se, Afrikaresor.se) (R03).
4. The client's "largest" claim is uncontested and a real asset, but it is unverified marketing copy and must be substantiated before use in ads (R03, R08, R09).
5. Search: sydafrikaresor.se ranks #1 for "resa till Sydafrika" and "rundresa" queries but is absent or weak on the informational queries that feed the funnel (best time, cost, safety, itineraries). The site has only about 6 tips articles (R06).
6. Air: no nonstop from Sweden; all customers connect. Cape Town capacity is growing (+250,000 seats, +12% for 2026/27, ACSA https://www.airports.co.za/news?item=447), but Gulf hubs carry disruption risk (Emirates -34%, Qatar -56% flights in April 2026, Cirium via Travel Weekly) (R04, R12).
7. Supply is the likely binding constraint in peak: Sabi Sand/Greater Kruger flagship lodges sell 6-12 months ahead, Kruger conservation fee rises about 15% from 1 Nov 2026 (R602 to R692), Cape Town luxury ADR grew 8.7% a year 2015-2025 (R05, HVS).
8. Currency: SEK/ZAR about 1.696 on 2 Nov 2026, krona down 7% in 12 months, i.e. South Africa got more expensive for Swedes (R10, https://tradingeconomics.com/sekzar:cur). The "more trip for your money" argument is weaker than before.
9. Regulation scales with turnover: 0.6% resegaranti fund levy on package price since 1 April 2026; an enacted but not yet in force rule sets individual guarantees at 6.25% of guarantee-liable turnover; growth must be notified to Kammarkollegiet; EU Directive 2026/1024 broadens "package" by about September 2028 (R09).
10. Safety perception is the central objection. The Swedish Embassy states the crime level is very high but most visits pass without serious incident; no UD travel warning was shown on 6 Oct 2026. A change in that status would trigger free-cancellation rights (R09). Swedish safety-perception data does not exist in our sources (unknown).

## 6. Positioning

### 6.1 Positioning statement
For Swedes planning a long-haul trip to South Africa who want it done properly and are willing to pay for expertise, sydafrikaresor.se is the **South Africa specialist since 2003**: a named personal adviser, guided trips with Swedish-speaking guides or fully tailor-made itineraries, and a booking that is protected by a travel guarantee. Unlike mass-market operators (Cape Town packages) and group-only operators (fixed dates, minimum participants), we cover the whole of South Africa and southern Africa and adapt the trip to the traveller.

### 6.2 Where we play and how we win
| Competitive set | Their strength | Our stance |
|---|---|---|
| Ving, TUI, Apollo | Reach, price anchors (TUI from SEK 11,337; Ving round trip from SEK 31,350 with SEK 2,000 code to 8 Nov 2026) | Do not bid on "Kapstaden hotell"; win round-trip, safari, Garden Route and segment queries where their products are shallow (R03, R06) |
| Albatros, Tourcompass, Temaresor | Flights-included group trips with published dates and prices; Tourcompass price guarantee | Build a flights-included guided ladder at SEK 36-45k with published 2027-28 departures plus a premium SEK 55-70k tier; offer flexibility they lack (children, solo, private) (R03) |
| MyChoice, Safariresor.se, Afrikaresor.se | Review volume (5.0/236, 4.8/~623, 4.9/124), local presence, price/open-purchase promises | Close the review gap first; prove local presence (named guides, 24/7 line); consider an open-purchase or price-match equivalent after margin review (R03) |
| DIY (self-booked, Reddit, Facebook groups, resatill.com) | Free information, lowest cost | Be the expert source that answers the same questions and converts (R06) |

Price posture: we are not the cheapest. Our guided 13-day product at SEK 44.3k sits roughly 15-20% above the group-with-flights cluster (R03), so every premium must be visible in value (inclusions, guide, flexibility, support), not argued.

### 6.3 Message pillars (Swedish, customer-facing; claims to be legally checked)
1. **Specialist sedan 2003.** "Vi har skickat svenska resenärer till Sydafrika sedan 2003." (Use "Skandinaviens största" only once passenger numbers can be shown publicly; Konsumentverket treats unsupported superlatives as misleading, R08.)
2. **Din egen Sydafrika-rådgivare.** "Du får en namngiven specialist som svarar inom en timme på vardagar." (use only once the SLA is met)
3. **Rundresa med svensk guide eller helt skräddarsytt.** "Följ med på en färdig rundresa eller låt oss bygga din egen."
4. **Trygg bokning.** "Din resa omfattas av resegaranti via Kammarkollegiet." Safety copy states facts and links to UD/Ambassaden; never "Sydafrika är säkert". Show "så planerar vi för din trygghet": förbokade transfers, dagkörning, guidade township-besök, jourtelefon (R09).
5. **Hela södra Afrika.** "Kombinera Kapstaden och safari med Victoriafallen, Botswana, Mauritius eller Seychellerna."

Seasonal hook (test, not proven): "Vinter hemma, sommar i Sydafrika" and "långresan efter USA" (R08).
