# Executive summary: sydafrikaresor.se from 40 to 80 MSEK

For the CEO | 3 Nov 2026 | Basis: `00-strategy.md` v2 and workstreams 01-36. No internal company data was available; every company figure is a placeholder listed in `ASSUMPTIONS-TO-VALIDATE.md`.

## 1. Ambition and the honest answer
Grow South Africa leisure sales to Swedish customers through sydafrikaresor.se from 40 MSEK to 80 MSEK in 24 months. After three independent red-team reviews the plan of record is **48 MSEK in 2027 and 64.4 MSEK in 2028**. 80 MSEK is a stretch that needs about +15.6 MSEK from four gap levers outside the core budget, and at least three of the four must land. Reasons: 80 MSEK would need roughly 16-23% of Swedes who book South Africa through an intermediary (about 90% of extra travellers taken from rivals or DIY), 2026 market momentum is weaker than 2025, and peak lodge supply for 2027 is largely committed.

## 2. Strategy in one paragraph
Position as **"Sydafrika-specialisten sedan 2003"**: a named personal adviser, Swedish-guided departures or fully tailor-made trips, protected by a visible travel guarantee. Do not compete on lowest price; sit 15-20% above flights-included group operators and prove the premium in value. Six pillars: trust and reviews, demand engine (SEO, search, Meta/YouTube, creators), conversion and CRM, product ladder with secured supply, partnerships and B2B2C, and a financial and compliance backbone. Trust, capacity and supply come before media spend.

## 4. Budget and economics
- **Marketing (excluding salaries): 0.30 MSEK Q4 2026, 3.6 MSEK 2027 (2.4 committed, 1.2 gated), 4.6 MSEK 2028 (3.0 committed, 1.6 gated)**, plus up to 1.0 MSEK paid in 2028 only on proven incremental ROAS of 5x. Paid commitment before Gate 1 (31 Mar 2027) is capped at SEK 0.5 M.
- **People:** +5 FTE in the core case (first consultant 1 Feb 2027, booking-operations coordinator by 1 Aug 2027, second consultant at Gate 1, two more in 2028); 6+ for 80 MSEK.
- **The programme loses money at the planning margin.** Incremental cost is about 3.3 MSEK (2027) and 7.1 MSEK (2028); at a 20% gross margin net contribution is about -1.7 and -2.2 MSEK (cumulative -3.9). Break-even needs about 23.5% gross margin. Approved 2027 loss budget: up to SEK 3.0 M with a stop-loss at -3.0 MSEK on 30 Jun 2027.
- **The workstreams add cost the strategy table does not contain.** Indicatively 4-5 MSEK in 2027 of people, operations, customer experience, legal, sales, supply and web asks, not yet de-duplicated. This is the largest budget risk and needs a CFO ruling.
- Cash and currency: peak working capital 5-12 MSEK; liquidity facility 10-15 MSEK (finance model: 16-20 for 2028); hedge 50-70%; 6% price buffer.
