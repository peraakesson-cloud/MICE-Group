# MICE-Group: growth plan for sydafrikaresor.se (40 to 80 MSEK, 2027-2028)

Prepared 3 Nov 2026 for the CEO. Scope: leisure travel to South Africa sold to Swedish customers through www.sydafrikaresor.se. Horizon: Q4 2026 preparation plus calendar years 2027 and 2028. Plans are in English; customer-facing copy (ads, posts, web, email) is in Swedish.

Important: there was no internal company data. Facts carry a source URL in the research files; everything else is marked as an assumption. Replace the assumptions with real data using `docs/ASSUMPTIONS-TO-VALIDATE.md`.

## Where to start (30 minutes)
1. `docs/EXECUTIVE-SUMMARY.md` (2 pages): ambition, strategy, revenue bridge, budget, first 30 days, risks, CEO decisions.
2. `docs/00-strategy.md`: the master plan every other document inherits from (targets, budget envelopes, guardrails G1-G10, gates).
3. `docs/ASSUMPTIONS-TO-VALIDATE.md`: Part 1 lists the twelve data packs to collect first.
4. `docs/workstreams/34-roadmap-90day.md`: week-by-week plan to 31 Jan 2027.

## How to use the documents
- **Precedence.** `00-strategy.md` is binding for targets, budgets and guardrails. Workstream plans inherit it. Where they conflict, the conflict is listed in `ASSUMPTIONS-TO-VALIDATE.md` Part 2 and in `34-roadmap-90day.md` section 13 for the first 90 days.
- **Weekly use.** Follow `34-roadmap-90day.md` and `36-governance-cadence.md` (Monday stand-up, Friday dashboard, monthly growth council). KPI definitions and thresholds are in `29-kpi-okr.md`.
- **Gates.** Gate 0 (15 Dec 2026), Gate 1 (31 Mar 2027), Gate 2 (30 Jun 2027), Gate 3 (31 Oct 2027), Gates 4 and 5 (31 Mar and 30 Jun 2028). Money is released by gate, not by calendar. Pass conditions are in `00-strategy.md` 16.2 and `35-roadmap-24month.md` section 5.
- **Money.** The ledger by month and channel is `28-budget-allocation.md`; the model is `27-finance-model.csv` (change only the input block).
- **Customer-facing copy.** Swedish drafts sit inside the workstream files (ads, posts, emails, job ads) and in `website/`. Legal review (workstream 31) is required before use.
- **Updating.** When real data arrives, replace the assumption in the source file, note the date and source, re-run the model, and update the Replace-with column in the assumptions register.

## Known open issues (see `ASSUMPTIONS-TO-VALIDATE.md`)
Margin and the definition of the 40 MSEK are unknown and decide whether the programme pays (break-even about 23.5% gross margin). Programme costs requested by the workstreams exceed the strategy cost table. Trustpilot is 2.7 on 8 reviews, so the strategy's 4.2 service gate is breached from day one. Several claims (founding year, association membership, guarantee listing, 24/7 or jourtelefon, "largest") are unverified.
